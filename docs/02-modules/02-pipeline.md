# 2. Pipeline / Opportunities (the seller's book of business board)

> **Module id:** `pipeline` · part of [`docs/02-functional-map.md`](../02-functional-map.md)

## Verdict
KEEP as the #1 MVP anchor — RESHAPE on three points. (1) This module must own the **earnings_ledger**, not the leaderboard module: the Closed-Won stage transition is the ONLY writer of Earnings, and splitting the write path across two modules is how leaderboard numbers drift from pipeline numbers. Leaderboard/dashboards become pure readers of `earnings.updated` + the ledger. (2) It must NOT own the contact, the timeline, or the activity — it owns the **opportunity** and consumes the rest; a contact can carry an FE opportunity in 2026 and an IUL opportunity in 2028, and the board must never be the reason we duplicate that person. (3) The insurance depth (carrier, underwriting status, placement, chargeback) belongs in an optional **vertical pack** table hanging off the opportunity, never in the core opportunity row — that is exactly how the core stays vertical-agnostic while the FE/IUL board feels purpose-built. No merge, no cut: if this module is weak the product is a contact list with a leaderboard bolted on.

## Purpose & value
It is the seller's entire working day on one screen: every deal they own as a card they can call, text, schedule and drag — and the single gesture (dropping a card into Closed Won) that turns a sale into Earnings and moves them up the leaderboard.

## Screens & states (9)

### My Pipeline (Kanban board)
The seller's default landing surface: every open opportunity they own, as movable cards, with health and next step visible without opening anything.

**Key actions:** `Drag a card to another stage` · `Open the win gate by dropping into Closed Won` · `Open the loss modal by dropping into Closed Lost` · `Call / SMS / Schedule / Note from the card` · `Snooze a card to a date` · `Switch pipeline (FE / IUL)` · `Apply or save a view (Needs attention, Untouched today)` · `Sort a column` · `New opportunity` · `Open card detail`

- **Empty:** Headline: "Your pipeline is empty." Body: "This board is your book of business. Every person you're working lives here as a card you move from New Lead to Closed Won — and closing a card is what adds to your Earnings." Primary button: "Add your first opportunity". Secondary link: "New leads land in New Lead automatically as they arrive." Per-column empty text (e.g. Quoted): "Nothing here yet. Cards land in Quoted once you've presented a price." Filtered-empty: "No cards match these filters." + "Clear filters". Needs-attention empty: "Nothing has gone cold. Everything's been touched in the last 7 days."
- **Loading:** Column skeletons render immediately with real stage names and header placeholders; filters, pipeline switcher and New opportunity stay interactive. Cards fade in per column as each page resolves. No full-page spinner, no layout shift. If a column takes >3s it shows "Still loading this stage…" inline.
- **Error:** Board-level: "We couldn't load your pipeline. Your deals are safe — this is a display problem." + "Retry", with the last cached board shown dimmed and read-only behind a banner "Showing your last loaded board. Some cards may be out of date." Move failure: the card snaps back and a toast says "Move failed. Maria Lopez is still in Quoted. Retry" with the server reason underneath (e.g. "This stage requires an annual premium."). Stale-data conflict: "Someone else moved this card. Refreshing."
- **No permission:** Not applicable for a seller's own board (a seller always has one). If the pipeline itself was deactivated by an admin: "This pipeline is no longer active. Your cards are safe — ask an admin which pipeline to use."
- **Mobile:** Single-column paged view with a stage pill selector at top showing count + total premium; swipe or tap to change stage view. No drag: card actions are Call, SMS, Schedule, and "Move to…" (bottom sheet, gates still enforced). Sticky bottom bar with "Untouched today (N)" as the primary quick-work path.

**Key actions:** `Enter premium as Monthly or Annual (toggle) and see the derived annual figure` · `Enter carrier / effective date / policy number when the tenant requires them` · `Confirm the win` · `Cancel (card returns to its original stage)`

- **Empty:** Modal headline: "Nice — let's log it." Field label: "Annual premium". Helper text under the field: "This is what your Earnings and your leaderboard rank are calculated from. If you sold it monthly, switch to Monthly and we'll do the math." Primary button: "Mark as won". Below the amount, live derived text: "$47/mo = $564 per year."
- **Loading:** Confirm button shows an inline spinner and locks the amount field; the card behind the modal stays in its original column until the write is confirmed. No optimistic Earnings update.
- **Error:** Validation: "Enter an amount between $1 and $100,000." / "Pick a carrier before closing — your team requires it." Save failure: "We couldn't save this win. Nothing was changed and Maria is still in Application." + "Try again". Explicit reassurance that no partial Earnings were written.

- **Mobile:** Full-screen sheet, numeric keypad by default, Monthly/Annual as a large segmented control (Monthly first — that's how FE is quoted), confirm button pinned to the bottom above the keyboard.

## Typical automations
- When an opportunity is created from a purchased/ping-post lead → pin it to the top of the first column, start the speed-to-lead clock, and offer one-tap Call now (Aloware Two-Legged Call).
- When a lead has not been touched 5 minutes after arriving → notify the owner on mobile: "New lead untouched for 5 minutes — Maria Lopez, Final Expense."
- When a call is completed with a connected disposition and the card is still in New Lead → move it to Contacted automatically.
- When a call is attempted 3 times with no connection and the card is still in New Lead → flag it on the board and suggest Closed-Lost with reason 'not contactable'.
- When a card enters Quoted → create a follow-up activity 24 hours out if the seller didn't schedule one (post-quote drop-off is where deals die).
- When a card sits in any stage past its SLA days → raise a board alert on the card, not an email report.
- When an opportunity has no activity for 7 days (configurable) → mark it cold, show it on the board, and add it to 'Needs attention'.
- When a lead replies by SMS or email → clear cold, cancel the snooze, badge the card 'Replied', and pause any running outbound sequence.
- When an appointment is booked → set the card's next activity and move it to the appointment stage.
- When an appointment is a no-show → badge the card, clear the next step, create a reschedule task, and keep the stage unchanged.
- When a card is dropped into Closed Won → require the annual premium, write the earnings ledger entry, fire the celebration, re-rank the leaderboard, and enroll the contact in the policy-delivery sequence.
- When a Final Expense policy is won → create a cross-sell reminder for an IUL opportunity on the same contact 90 days after the effective date.
- When a card is dropped into Closed Lost with reason 'price/affordability' → schedule a recycle reminder in 60 days with the original quote attached.
- When an application is submitted and no carrier decision is recorded within 14 days → alert the seller and the supervisor.
- When underwriting comes back 'approved — rated' → alert the seller to re-present the adjusted premium before the offer expires.
- When a policy is issued but the first premium is unpaid 30 days later → flag 'not placed' and open an earnings-reversal review.
- When a contact's consent is revoked or a DNC flag arrives → disable Call and SMS on every card for that contact and surface it in 'Needs attention'.
- When an admin publishes a new pipeline template version → notify affected sellers: "Your team's pipeline changed. Two stages were renamed."
- When a seller is deactivated → freeze their open cards and queue them for admin reassignment, leaving their historical Earnings untouched.

## KPIs
- Speed-to-lead: median and p90 seconds from opportunity.created to opportunity.first_touch_recorded, split by lead_source (the #1 conversion lever for purchased FE leads)
- Untouched-today count per seller (open opportunities with no activity in the last 24h)
- Cold rate: % of open opportunities in cold health at any moment, and mean days_idle at the moment of opportunity.went_cold
- 'No next step' rate: % of open opportunities without a future scheduled activity
- Stage conversion: % of opportunities entering stage X that ever reach the closed_won stage, per pipeline
- Median and p90 time_in_from_stage_seconds per stage (where deals actually stall — expect Quoted and Application)
- Win rate: opportunity.won ÷ (opportunity.won + opportunity.lost) per seller and per lead_source
- Average annual premium per won deal, per product_type (FE vs IUL) and per lead_source
- Days-to-close: median opportunity.created → opportunity.won
- Open pipeline value: Σ deal_value_annual_premium of open opportunities per seller and per stage
- Loss reason distribution (which objection costs the most premium, not just the most cards)
- Gate-block rate: opportunity.gate_blocked per 100 attempted closed_won transitions (a UX-friction signal, should trend to near zero)
- Recycle yield: win rate of opportunities created via opportunity.recycled vs fresh ones
- Placement rate (Life pack): policies with placement_status='placed' ÷ policies issued
- Early-lapse / chargeback rate and total reversed Earnings per period (the honesty check on the leaderboard)
- No-show → loss rate: % of opportunities with no_show_count > 0 that end Closed-Lost
- Board responsiveness: p95 board load time and p95 stage-move round trip (a laggy board stops being used)
- Data hygiene: % of open opportunities with a premium value present before reaching the Quoted stage

## Insurance specifics (Life / FE / IUL) & vertical-agnostic core
GENUINELY LIFE/FE/IUL-SPECIFIC HERE: (1) deal_value = ANNUAL PREMIUM, but FE is quoted and sold as a monthly bank draft — the win gate must accept a monthly figure and derive the annual one, otherwise Earnings on a public leaderboard will be wrong by 12x. (2) Face amount and premium are different numbers and sellers conflate them; the card shows premium (what the agency earns from) and the detail shows face amount (what the client bought). (3) The sale does not end at 'sold': application → underwriting → decision (approved / rated / postponed / declined) → issued → PLACED (first premium drafted). FE has a real gap between issued and placed, plus an early-lapse chargeback window — which is why the ledger supports reversals and why 'Not placed' is a distinct card state, not a loss. (4) Loss reasons are health/age-driven ('doesn't qualify') and budget-driven ('no funds', 'already insured' from a competing final-expense telemarketer) — this taxonomy is domain knowledge, not generic CRM vocabulary. (5) Purchased ping-post leads decay in minutes, so the board leads with a speed-to-lead clock rather than a tidy alphabetical list. (6) TCPA: consent status and the lead-vendor consent certificate live on the opportunity and gate the Call/SMS actions per card; DNC state is visible before the seller dials. (7) One contact legitimately buys twice — FE now, IUL when their situation changes — so the multi-opportunity-per-contact model is a revenue mechanic here, not a data-modeling nicety. (8) Two products with radically different cycles (FE closes in days off one call; IUL takes multiple illustration meetings) justify two typed pipelines. HOW THE CORE STAYS VERTICAL-AGNOSTIC: none of the above touches the core schema. `opportunity` has zero insurance columns — product_type is a label, deal_value_annual_premium is a generic currency field the UI can relabel per tenant ('Annual premium' / 'Contract value'), and everything from carrier to placement lives in `opportunity_policy_detail`, an optional 1:1 extension rendered from `opportunity_field_definition` rows tagged with vertical_pack='life_us'. Stage sets, gates, SLA days and the loss-reason taxonomy are all tenant data, not code. The agnosticism test is concrete: drop the policy-detail table and the life field definitions, and what remains is a complete, working, generic pipeline module — a different vertical is a new template + a new field pack, never a migration.

## ⚔️ Adversarial review (sales-ops critic)

**Verdict:** NEEDS WORK — the event/ledger architecture and the gate are genuinely right, but the module models a B2B kanban for a business that runs on dial attempts, calling windows and placement: no attempt cadence, no Power Dialer list push, no DOB/state, no multi-carrier re-shop, no lead cost, and it never decides whether a "win" is a submitted app or a placed policy — plus a cross-owner contact-merge reaction that silently splices two sellers' books together.

### Must fix — missing for real operation (13)
- DIAL ATTEMPT DISCIPLINE — the single biggest gap. A purchased FE lead needs 6-12 attempts across different day-parts over 10-14 days before it is genuinely dead. The spec has attempt_count and last_attempt_at and nothing else: no attempt cadence/schedule, no next-attempt window, no day-part rotation (a lead never answers at 10am, try 6pm), no per-lead attempt cap, and no rule that 8 attempts over 14 days auto-routes to 'not contactable'. Without this, 'cold after 7 days' just labels leads the seller never actually worked.
- ALOWARE POWER DIALER LIST PUSH. The spec uses only the Two-Legged Call API for one-off dials. The Power Dialer list API is the one Aloware capability that fits the silo model perfectly (agent-paced, per-seller list, no shared queue) and it is the difference between 40 dials/day and 150. There must be a 'Work this column / work this view' action that pushes the filtered, consent-clean, in-calling-window set into that seller's Power Dialer list, plus a way to reconcile dispositions back onto the cards. Missing entirely.
- LEAD-LOCAL CALLING WINDOW. TCPA restricts calls to 8am-9pm in the LEAD'S timezone, and several states are stricter. Contact timezone appears once as a denormalized display field. There is no per-card 'callable now / callable at 8:00am ET' state, no suppression of out-of-window leads from Needs Attention or from a dialer list, and no state-level restriction data. A West Coast seller starting at 6am is dialing New York at 9am — fine — but the reverse case is a fine, and the seller has no surface telling them.
- DATE OF BIRTH and STATE are not in the opportunity or in the policy detail table. Without them you cannot compute the FE rate band, cannot check carrier availability by state, cannot enforce the calling window, and cannot flag age knockouts. These are asked in the first 60 seconds of every FE call. Add DOB, state, tobacco, and a short health-knockout note (oxygen, dialysis, insulin, recent stroke/cancer) — that block is what determines whether there is a sale at all.
- MULTIPLE APPLICATIONS PER OPPORTUNITY. opportunity_policy_detail is a 1:1 extension with one carrier and one policy_number. A declined or heavily-rated FE app gets re-shopped to carrier B and often C — that is normal, not an edge case. As modeled, the re-shop either overwrites the decline (losing the history the owner needs to pick carriers) or forces a duplicate opportunity (double-counting pipeline). This must be 1:N applications hanging off one opportunity, with the won premium tied to the placed application.
- THE SUBMITTED-vs-ISSUED-vs-PLACED DECISION IS NEVER MADE. The spec writes Earnings on entry to closed_won but never says whether closed_won means 'application submitted' (the agent's felt sale) or 'policy placed' (the money). This is the most consequential business decision in the module and it is silently deferred. Submit-time wins fill the public leaderboard with junk that never places; placement-time wins delay the celebration 3-6 weeks and disconnect it from the sale. The answer is almost certainly two numbers — Submitted premium (motivational, real-time) and Placed premium (the ledger of record) — and the spec must pick and defend one.
- FIRST DRAFT / PERSISTENCY WORKFLOW. draft_day_of_month exists as a field with no behavior. The FE money killer is the first bank draft failing. There is no 'first draft in 3 days' alert, no NSF/returned-draft state, no policy-delivery or welcome-call step, no re-draft workflow. Not-placed is treated as a reversal event rather than as a workflow the seller can actually save.
- LEAD COST. The owner buys leads. Nowhere is there cost_per_lead, a vendor batch/order id, or cost per placed policy. lead_vendor_id and campaign_id exist but carry no money, so the KPI list can compute win rate by source and never compute CPA or ROI. That is the number an agency owner buys this product for, and it is absent.
- SPOUSE / HOUSEHOLD SECOND POLICY. The cross-sell feature jumps straight to FE→IUL at 90 days. The cross-sell that actually happens in FE is the spouse on the same call, five minutes later. There is no household link, no 'add second insured', no 'spouse opportunity' one-tap create — so it will be logged as an unrelated lead or not at all.
- QUOTED PREMIUM AS A RECORDED NUMBER. The Quoted stage stores nothing. So 'price/affordability' as a loss reason has no number attached, quote-to-sale gap cannot be measured, and the owner cannot answer 'are we quoting too high or is the objection real'. Capture quoted premium and quoted face amount when the card enters Quoted.
- AN ALOWARE DISPOSITION MAP. The spec hand-waves 'auto-advance on a connected disposition'. Aloware disposition strings are tenant-configured; without an explicit disposition→outcome mapping entity (connected / no answer / voicemail / wrong number / DNC requested / not interested / callback set) the webhook auto-logging produces uncategorized noise and the auto-advance rule cannot be written.
- INBOUND CALLS. sms.received and email.received are treated as the strongest buy signals; an inbound CALL from the lead — which is stronger than either — has no consumed event at all. call.completed and call.missed both read as outbound. A lead calling back must clear cold, cancel any snooze, badge 'Called us back', and pin the card.
- CONTACT RATE. The KPI list has speed-to-lead, cold rate, win rate — but not dials-to-contact, contact rate (connects ÷ dials), or attempts-to-first-contact. On a real floor, contact rate is the floor metric checked before anything else. Also missing: appointment set-to-sat (kept) rate, placement lag, and persistency at month 3/6/9.

### Too generic / boilerplate (10)
- 'Board filters + saved views' with a filter builder (product, source, health, value range, tags) is B2B-SaaS boilerplate. An FE seller does not build views — they work six fixed queues: Untouched, Callback due now, Quoted-no-app, App submitted-no-decision, Draft date this week, Chargeback window open. Ship those as hardcoded system views and cut the builder and the 'save named view' machinery entirely.
- 'Weighted pipeline value' with per-stage probability_pct is pure enterprise forecasting imported from a 90-day B2B cycle. An FE deal closes in 1-3 days off one call; there is nothing to weight. What the owner actually needs to plan lead spend is cost-per-lead x leads bought vs placed premium per vendor batch — which the spec does not have. Replace probability_pct with lead_cost and placement rate.
- health ∈ hot|warm|cold plus a 'health dot' is generic CRM color-coding. In life telesales the only states that change behavior are: never contacted (n dial attempts remaining), contacted-not-quoted, quoted-no-app, app-pending-carrier, issued-not-placed. A single dot flattens all of that. Cut the dot; the days-since-touch number colored by threshold already carries it, and the real signal (attempt count / next dial window) is missing.
- 'Curated custom fields per pipeline' — an admin field builder, marked complexity high, in a product that ships exactly two known verticals (FE, IUL) with an authored pack. Keep opportunity_field_definition as the internal rendering mechanism for the packs; cut the admin-facing field builder from the roadmap until vertical #3 is actually sold.
- 'Column sort and pagination', 'List/table view with column chooser', 'Bulk actions (move/snooze/tag)' — these three could be lifted verbatim into any Pipedrive clone. The only FE-specific bulk action that matters is 'mark these 30 not-contactable after 8 attempts' and 'push this column into the Aloware Power Dialer'; neither is named.
- Per-seller 'rename a stage for my board only' is generic personalization that actively costs money here: the seller says the deal is in 'Pending', the supervisor's roll-up says 'Application', and every coaching conversation starts with a translation. Reorder is equally meaningless because canonical order defines the funnel. Reduce per-seller override to collapse/hide-empty + a stricter personal cold threshold, and say so.
- 'Average card age' in the column header is a vanity metric nobody acts on. The column header numbers that change behavior are: count, total annual premium, and how many in this column have no next step.
- The win-gate sanity range '$1 and $100,000' is a generic validation string applied to two products whose premium distributions do not overlap: FE annual premium clusters $300-$1,800, IUL starts where FE ends. A single tenant-wide range catches nothing. The range must be per-pipeline, and the real guard is a soft 'that's unusually high/low for Final Expense — confirm' band, not a hard min of $1 that happily accepts a monthly figure typed as annual.
- Four overlapping state machines on one card — snooze, next_activity, health, and 'no next step' — is generic feature accretion. A scheduled callback IS the snooze; a snooze without a callback booked is exactly the 'no next step' the spec says is the most common silent death. Collapse snooze into 'book the callback' and the card gets simpler and more honest.
- Card anatomy already carries 7 chips (name, product badge, premium, days-since-touch, next activity, source chip, TCPA/DNC badge, health dot). That is a generic 'show everything' card that will be unreadable on the phone the spec says is for quick contact.

### Bloat to cut (11)
- Weighted pipeline value + probability_pct on pipeline_stage. Cut the feature and the column. Nothing in a 3-day FE cycle is probabilistic in a way a stage percentage captures, and the supervisor number it produces will be quietly ignored.
- The admin custom-field builder ('Curated custom fields per pipeline', complexity high). Ship the FE and IUL packs as authored field definitions; cut the builder UI. What breaks without it: nothing, for the first two verticals — which is the entire MVP.
- Per-seller stage RENAME and REORDER. Keep hide/collapse and the stricter personal cold threshold. Renaming buys a seller nothing and costs every coaching conversation and every screen-share a translation step.
- Saved-view builder, view naming, default-view management, and the tag filter. Replace with six hardcoded FE/IUL queues. What breaks without it: a seller cannot invent a seventh queue — which nobody has asked for.
- 'Average card age' in the column header.
- Column chooser in the table view.
- Snooze as an independent mechanism. Fold it into booking the callback activity, which the board already tracks, and delete snoozed_until as a separate suppression path — otherwise a seller can silence the cold alert without committing to a next step, which is precisely the failure mode feature #9 exists to catch.
- 'Cross-sell trigger: FE won → IUL opportunity at 90 days'. This is an automation template, not a pipeline feature. It belongs in the automations module consuming opportunity.won; owning it here duplicates the trigger vocabulary in two places.
- The health dot. Days-since-touch, colored, plus the no-next-step flag already say everything the dot says, with a number attached.
- gate_overridden on opportunity_stage_history and the 'Override a required-field gate' permission row that says No for all three roles in v1. Dead schema and a dead row that reads as a hint to add the bypass later. Delete both, or the first support escalation will get it turned on.
- The Cmd+K palette's stated justification ('a keyboard-first seller is 3x faster'). That number is invented and the premise is wrong — the desktop speed lever on a telesales floor is the Power Dialer list, not a command palette. If the palette ships, justify it on 'find a lead by phone number while they are on the line', which is real.

### Event gaps (12)
- NO EVENT ENVELOPE. Not one of the 19 emitted events carries event_id, event_version, or correlation_id, and none of the consumed events define an idempotency key. Aloware webhooks retry. A retried call.completed will double-increment attempt_count, re-stamp first_touch_at, and re-fire opportunity.recovered. For a system the house style calls an organism, this is the missing spine.
- earnings.updated carries new_all_time_total — a derived running total shipped inside an event from an append-only ledger. Out-of-order or retried delivery makes the leaderboard show a stale total, which re-creates the exact drift the ledger was designed to eliminate. Either drop the field, or attach a monotonic ledger_sequence so the leaderboard can discard out-of-order updates. Also missing: an idempotency key so a replayed win does not double-credit.
- THE PLACEMENT LIFECYCLE HAS NO EVENTS. Everything from application to money is collapsed into one catch-all opportunity.underwriting_status_changed with from_status/to_status strings, forcing every consumer to switch on text. These move money and deserve first-class events: policy.application_submitted, policy.decision_received, policy.issued, policy.first_draft_scheduled, policy.first_draft_failed, policy.placed, policy.lapsed_in_chargeback_window.
- call.missed is the wrong name and the wrong model. It reads as an inbound missed call; it is used for an unanswered outbound dial. Rename to call.attempt_completed carrying a disposition code, and add a genuinely missing call.inbound_received — a lead calling back is the strongest buy signal on the board and there is no reaction for it anywhere.
- ALOAI SUMMARY ARRIVES LATE. The spec says the card surfaces the AloAi summary snippet in the call.completed reaction, but transcription and AI summary arrive on separate, later Aloware webhooks. As written the card renders a summary that does not exist yet. Add consumption of call.recording_ready, call.transcription_ready and call.summary_ready, plus call.disposition_updated (agents fix dispositions after the fact and the stage auto-advance must re-evaluate).
- STOP / OPT-OUT IS BURIED. Aloware's STOP handling is delegated to a generic contact.consent_changed. An SMS opt-out must be a first-class consumed event (sms.opt_out_received) that instantly disenrolls every sequence, kills the SMS quick action, badges the card, and writes an audit row. Compliance events should not arrive through a general-purpose consent update.
- SEQUENCE STATE IS NEVER TOUCHED. Research pattern #6 (cadences auto-pause on reply or booking) is claimed as adopted, but this module never emits or consumes anything about Aloware sequence enrollment. There is no stated contract that entering a closed stage, a DNC flag, or an inbound reply disenrolls the lead. The visible failure: the CRM keeps texting a customer for a week after they bought.
- SNOOZE WAKE IS ON THE DAILY TICK. system.daily_tick 'wakes snoozed cards whose date has arrived'. A callback snoozed to 3:00pm today wakes at tomorrow's tick — after the callback. Wake must be time-scheduled, not batch. Same class of problem for went_cold, though 7 days tolerates a daily batch.
- SPEED-TO-LEAD CLOCK STARTS AT THE WRONG EVENT AND HAS NO BREACH EVENT. The clock starts at opportunity.created, i.e. at whatever moment our intake finished processing; it must start at the vendor's ping-post timestamp carried on lead.created, otherwise our own latency is invisible in the metric we claim is the #1 conversion lever. And there is no opportunity.speed_to_lead_breached at N minutes — first_touch_recorded only fires on success, so nothing can trigger the escalation for the leads that were never called.
- opportunity.won carries `carrier` in its payload — a vertical-pack field leaking into a core event, in a module whose own verdict says the core must stay vertical-agnostic. Move it into a vertical_attributes map or drop it.
- NO ATTEMPT EVENT. Attempts are only stamped as a side effect inside the call.missed reaction, so no consumer can compute attempts-to-contact, dial curves by day-part, or 'worked out' status. Emit opportunity.contact_attempt_recorded with channel, disposition, and attempt ordinal.
- opportunity.duplicate_flagged lists lead-intake as a consumer while lead-intake emits lead.duplicate_detected that this module consumes. Two modules both claiming the dedupe decision, in a circle, with no statement of which one wins.

### 🚨 Silo violations (8)
- CROSS-SELLER DEDUPE LEAKS THE SILO. 'Creating a second OPEN opportunity for the same contact + same product warns and offers to open the existing card' never says the check is scoped to owner_user_id = self. If the contact record is tenant-wide, seller A gets told that a person already has an open FE deal — a deal that belongs to seller B. That is cross-seller lead visibility through a validation message. Two sellers legitimately buying the same person from two vendors is normal in this business. Dedupe must be self-scoped; cross-owner collisions surface only in the supervisor/admin view.
- contact.merged IS THE WORST SILO BREACH IN THE SPEC. The stated reaction re-points every opportunity from the losing contact to the surviving one. If the two contacts are owned by different sellers, the result is one contact carrying two sellers' opportunities, and the unified per-lead timeline — calls, recordings, AloAi summaries, SMS — is now shared between two sellers' books. Merging across owners must be an admin-only operation with an explicit decision about the timeline, never an automatic reaction to an event.
- SUPERVISOR CREATE-ON-BEHALF IS ASSIGNMENT BY ANOTHER NAME. Permissions say 'Supervisor: Yes, must explicitly pick an owner' and the quick-create screen says 'supervisors create on behalf of a seller'. The brief says there is no supervisor assignment in the MVP. Either cut it, or state it explicitly as the single, manual, one-record-at-a-time exception with an audit row — but do not let it sit in the permission table looking like ordinary CRM behavior.
- 'ADMIN REASSIGNMENT QUEUE' (user.deactivated reaction) is a routing engine wearing a different hat. A queue of unowned leads waiting to be handed out is exactly the central assignment model the architecture forbids. Reframe as a one-off offboarding transfer screen, drop the word queue, and constrain it to deactivated-user cleanup only.
- 'Transfer ownership ... for offboarding, PTO ... bulk supported' — offboarding is defensible; PTO coverage is routine reassignment and turns transfer into the assignment engine the MVP does not have. Cut PTO from the justification or the feature will be built as a scheduling tool.
- contact.consent_changed propagates 'to every opportunity for that contact' — correctly crossing the silo, since compliance must. But it is not declared as an intentional exception, and it means a card in seller A's book visibly changes state because of something seller B did. It needs an explicit statement in the permissions section listing the three legitimate cross-silo paths (leaderboard aggregates, supervisor/admin views, compliance state propagation) and closing everything else.
- The 'board dedupe banner' on lead.duplicate_detected does not say whose board it renders on. If the duplicate was detected against another seller's lead, that banner is a leak.
- Supervisor tenant-wide CSV export is granted with only 'logged' as a control. That is the one action that takes 50 sellers' books out of the system in one file. It is permitted by the role model, but it needs a stated retention/audit contract and an explicit note that it exports contact PII under CCPA — otherwise the silo holds in the UI and evaporates at the export button.

## Risks & dependencies
- HARD DEPENDENCY — activity/timeline module: last_activity_at is the input to health, cold alerts and 'Needs attention'. If call/SMS/email events are lost or delayed, the board lies about which deals are dying, and sellers stop trusting the health dot within a week. Mitigation: idempotent event ingestion keyed on external ids, a backfill job that recomputes last_activity_at from the activity table nightly, and a visible 'Activity synced HH:MM' indicator on the board.
- HARD DEPENDENCY — Aloware webhooks (comms module) for call disposition/recording/transcript/AloAi summary. There is no embeddable softphone for us, so the card's Call action depends on the Two-Legged Call API plus the Aloware Talk Chrome extension as a fallback. If the API path is unreliable the whole 'quick action from the card' value proposition degrades to a tel: link.
- HARD DEPENDENCY — contacts/lead-intake owns contact identity and dedupe. Weak dedupe upstream produces duplicate cards downstream, and our opportunity-level guard only catches same-contact duplicates, not same-person-two-contacts.
- HARD DEPENDENCY — calendar module for next_activity and meeting.no_show. Without it, the 'next activity' line on the card is empty and half of the card's value disappears.
- ARCHITECTURAL RISK — earnings ownership: this spec claims the earnings ledger for the pipeline module (single writer at the closed_won transaction). If the leaderboard module also claims it, we get two sources of truth and guaranteed divergence. This MUST be resolved in consolidation.
- TRUST RISK — all-time, never-reset Earnings with no reversal path inflates permanently from policies that are issued but never place. Research already warns that a never-reset revenue-only board demotes most of the team; adding un-reversed phantom wins makes it worse. Mitigate with the reversal feature and the secondary recognition category, and keep the period_key columns so a monthly reset is a query change.
- GAMING RISK — the required-field gate makes the premium mandatory but not honest. A seller can type an inflated annual premium to climb the podium. Mitigate with range validation per product, an outlier flag in the supervisor roll-up, full audit on opportunity.deal_value_changed, and (later) carrier/policy-number confirmation before the win counts.
- FRAGMENTATION RISK — per-seller stage overrides. If sellers can add or delete stages, cross-seller funnel reporting and the common template both die. Mitigation is baked in: overrides are display-level only against an immutable stage_key.
- PERFORMANCE RISK — 50 sellers × hundreds of open cards, plus a supervisor board spanning everyone, on a sub-$100/month infra budget. Mitigation: per-column pagination, virtualized rendering, pre-aggregated roll-ups for the supervisor strip, and never computing Earnings by scanning opportunities (the ledger is the read path).
- MOBILE RISK — drag & drop on touch is error-prone and a mis-drop into Closed Won writes Earnings. Mitigated by replacing drag with a move sheet on phones plus the 5-second Undo, but this must be tested on a real phone, not a resized browser.
- CUT — WIP limits / column capacity warnings: in a per-seller silo with no routing, capping how many deals sit in Quoted solves nobody's problem and just nags. Cut.
- CUT — offline-first mobile board: high complexity, sync-conflict surface on the exact records that write Earnings, and sellers are on cellular in a US market. Cut in favor of fast optimistic UI and clear failure messages.
- CUT — generic condition-builder automation UI inside the pipeline: it is the blank-canvas anti-pattern. The pipeline emits a short, named, plain-English event vocabulary and the automations module ships templates on top of it.
- CUT — weighted probability forecast on the seller's board: on a 3-day FE cycle it is noise and invites gaming. Kept only in the supervisor/admin roll-up.
- CUT — deal-level file vault / document storage on the opportunity: illustrations and applications belong to the contact record and to the carrier's own portal; duplicating them here adds storage cost against a <$100/month budget with no seller-facing payoff.
- CUT — multi-currency: the operation is USD-only; currency is stored on the record for multi-tenant future-proofing but no conversion logic ships.

## Open questions
- Who owns the earnings ledger — this module (recommended: single writer at the Closed-Won transaction) or a dedicated earnings/leaderboard module? This must be decided before any other module reads Earnings.
- How far can a seller deviate from the tenant template? Recommendation: reorder, rename-for-self, hide only — no adding or deleting stages, closed stages locked. Needs the client's explicit yes, since discovery said 'template + per-seller adjustment' without bounding it.
- What exactly does the Closed-Won gate require? Annual premium alone (recommended for MVP speed), or premium + carrier + effective date? Every added field raises data quality and lowers the chance the seller logs the win at all.
- Is the authoritative premium the seller-entered annual figure, or monthly × 12? Recommendation: store both (premium_input_amount + premium_mode) and derive the annual value, so the audit trail shows what the seller actually sold.
- Does the win count at 'issued' or at 'placed' (first premium drafted)? Counting at issued makes the leaderboard fast and motivating but inflates it; counting at placed is honest but delays celebration by weeks. Recommendation: count at issued and ship the reversal path.
- Is the chargeback/lapse reversal in scope for v1? It directly affects whether the leaderboard is trusted after month three.
- Two separate pipelines (Final Expense, IUL) or one pipeline with a product field? Recommendation: two, because the cycles differ enough that shared stage metrics would be meaningless.
- Does an opportunity auto-open for every incoming lead, or does the seller 'open' one deliberately? Auto-open maximizes speed-to-lead; deliberate opening keeps the board clean. Recommendation: auto-open, with archive-as-junk one tap away.
- Can a supervisor move another seller's card, or only observe and request? Recommendation: observe + request, because a supervisor silently moving cards breaks the silo model the client described.
- Who runs the cold/health evaluator — this module (recommended, since it owns health and the board treatment) or the automations module? Two evaluators would produce conflicting health states.
- Does 'no answer, left voicemail' count as a touch that clears cold, or only as an attempt? Recommendation: attempt only — otherwise a seller can keep a dead lead 'warm' forever by dialing it.
- What is a sane per-product premium range for the outlier/validation check on the win gate (FE vs IUL)? Needs a number from the client, not an industry guess.
- Is the vertical-pack field set admin-editable in v1, or seeded and locked? Editable is more agnostic; locked is safer for reporting consistency in the first release.
