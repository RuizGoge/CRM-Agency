# 11. Notifications & Alerting (Signal Router + Attention Inbox)

> **Module id:** `notifications` · part of [`docs/02-functional-map.md`](../02-functional-map.md)

## Verdict
KEEP — but RESHAPE from "a settings page + a bell icon" into a **staff-facing Signal Router with one Attention Inbox and a hard anti-noise contract**. Three reshaping decisions define the module: (1) It is STAFF-FACING ONLY. Every message that goes to a *lead* (appointment reminders to the client, SMS cadences, drip email) belongs to Comms/Sequences — Notifications never touches a consumer's phone, which also keeps TCPA surface out of this module. (2) It owns no business truth. The board, the timeline and the leaderboard remain the source of truth; a notification is a *disposable pointer with an action button* that must be able to die on its own when its reason disappears. (3) Its top-line feature is not the bell — it is the **sub-60-second fresh-lead interrupt**, because a purchased FE ping-post lead decays in minutes and is worthless if the seller learns about it from a list refresh. Do NOT merge it into Dashboard/My Day: "My Day" is a planned worklist the seller pulls; Notifications is an unplanned interrupt that pushes. Merging them guarantees that the one alert worth interrupting for (fresh lead) gets buried among 30 FYIs. Do NOT cut it either: without this module the Aloware webhooks arrive silently, the seller has to poll a screen to discover a $4k IUL replied, and the whole "speed-to-lead is the #1 conversion lever" thesis collapses.

## Purpose & value
It taps you on the shoulder — within seconds, on the device in your hand — the moment one of YOUR leads becomes worth money (a fresh purchased lead just landed, they texted back, you missed their call, they no-showed), gives you a one-tap "Call now" right there, and shuts up about everything else.

## Screens & states (7)

### Attention Inbox (bell popover + full page)
The single place where everything the system needs from this user lives, grouped by urgency and actionable in place.

**Key actions:** `Call now / Reply by SMS / Reschedule / Mark no-show (inline)` · `Open the lead, opportunity or meeting at the exact anchor` · `Snooze (15m / 1h / tomorrow)` · `Mute this lead or this alert type (with expiry)` · `Filter: needs action, type, specific lead` · `Mark all read`

- **Empty:** Headline: "Nothing needs you right now." Body: "When a fresh lead lands, a client texts back, or you miss a call, it shows up here with a Call now button — and on your phone, if push is on." Primary button: "Turn on phone alerts" (opens the push permission primer). Secondary link: "Choose which alerts you get". If push is already on, the primary becomes "Send me a test alert".
- **Loading:** Three skeleton rows preserving the grouped layout (Act now / Today / FYI headers stay visible so the page does not jump); the bell badge shows its cached count rather than 0 to avoid a false 'all clear'.
- **Error:** Inline banner: "We couldn't load your alerts. Urgent alerts may still reach your phone." with Retry. If the live connection drops specifically: a subtle bar "Live updates paused — reconnecting…" that turns into "Live updates are back" and refetches, because silently going stale here is worse than a visible warning.
- **No permission:** Not reachable for a signed-in user (everyone has their own inbox). Individual items whose subject is no longer yours are withdrawn and, if already open, replaced by: "This lead isn't in your book anymore." with no data preview and no actions.
- **Mobile:** Mobile is the primary surface. Full-screen inbox, one card per row, thumb-reachable primary action; Call now is the largest tap target; swipe left = snooze, swipe right = open. Push action buttons mirror the top two inline actions so the seller can dial from the lock screen.

### Fresh Lead Alert (P0 interrupt surface)
Convert a just-purchased ping-post lead into a dial in under a minute, from wherever the seller is.

**Key actions:** `Call now (fires the Aloware two-legged call)` · `Snooze 60 seconds` · `Open the lead` · `Dismiss (records why: not mine now / can't call)`

- **Empty:** Never empty by nature — it only exists when a lead exists. Its 'empty' analogue lives in preferences: "No fresh-lead alerts yet. The moment a purchased lead lands in your book, this is the alert that will interrupt you." plus a "Preview this alert" button so the seller sees it once before it matters.
- **Loading:** The alert renders immediately with name, source and a live age counter; the enrichment strip (state, product line, vendor, consent status) fills in as a shimmer. The Call now button is enabled the instant the phone number and consent flag are present — never gated behind full lead load.
- **Error:** If the click-to-call fails: the alert stays open and switches to "We couldn't start the call from here." with Copy number, Open in Aloware Talk, and Retry. The alert does NOT auto-resolve on a failed call, and the escalation clock keeps running.
- **No permission:** If consent is missing or revoked, Call now and Reply are hidden and replaced with: "No consent on file for this lead — calling and texting are blocked." plus Open lead. If the lead is no longer in this user's book, the alert self-withdraws with: "This lead isn't in your book anymore."
- **Mobile:** Delivered as a high-priority web push with action buttons; tapping Call now from the notification tray dials without a full app boot. On desktop it is a persistent corner takeover with a soft repeating chime until acknowledged, dismissible only by acting or explicit snooze.

### My Alert Settings
Let each seller tune their own interruption level without a support ticket, in plain English.

**Key actions:** `Set each rule to Instant / Bundled / Daily digest / Off per channel` · `Set thresholds (minimum annual premium, stage, product line, lead source)` · `Set quiet hours and timezone; choose whether urgent alerts break through` · `Set digest send time` · `Manage push devices; send a test alert` · `Restore recommended setup`

- **Empty:** Applies to the devices block when none are registered: "No devices yet. Phone alerts are how you beat the other agents to a fresh lead." Primary button: "Turn on alerts on this device". After enabling: "This device is set up — send yourself a test alert". Rule rows are never empty; they ship pre-filled with the recommended profile plus the note "You're on the recommended setup. Change anything — we'll remember it."
- **Loading:** The rule matrix renders from the tenant catalog with per-row toggles disabled and shimmering until the user's overrides resolve, so nothing can be toggled against a stale value.
- **Error:** "We couldn't save that preference — it's still set to the old value." Inline on the affected row with Retry; the rest of the matrix stays editable. Push registration failure: "Your browser blocked alerts" with browser-specific instructions and, on iOS, "Add this app to your Home Screen to get phone alerts."
- **No permission:** A seller sees only their own settings; there is no user picker. Rules locked by the tenant (compliance, integration health) render with a lock icon and the tooltip "Required by your agency — this alert can't be turned off." Supervisors opening another user's settings get a read-only view: "You can see this seller's alert setup but not change it. Ask an admin."
- **Mobile:** Matrix collapses from grid to one expandable card per rule (rule name + current mode summary); channel toggles live inside the expanded card. The push permission primer is the first thing shown on a mobile first visit.

### Alert Rules & Escalation (admin)
Set the org-wide alerting policy, the speed-to-lead escalation ladder and the noise ceilings so 50 sellers start from a tuned default instead of chaos.

**Key actions:** `Enable/disable rules tenant-wide and set default severity and channels` · `Edit the fresh-lead escalation ladder (delays, targets, stop conditions)` · `Set rate caps per channel per hour and the daily interruption ceiling` · `Mark rules as locked (non-disableable by sellers)` · `Edit and preview message templates; send a test to a chosen user` · `Reset a user or the whole tenant to the recommended pack`

- **Empty:** Only on a brand-new tenant before seeding: "No alert policy yet. Start from the Life / Final Expense playbook — fresh-lead interrupts on, cold-lead alerts in the daily digest, compliance alerts locked." Primary button: "Install the recommended playbook". Secondary: "Start from scratch" (with a warning that sellers will get no alerts until rules exist).
- **Loading:** Rule list skeleton; the escalation ladder editor stays locked until the current policy version loads, to prevent saving over a concurrent edit.
- **Error:** "We couldn't save the policy. Your sellers are still on the previous version." — explicit, because a half-saved policy silently changing 50 people's alerts is the worst failure mode here. Shows the last-known-good version and a Retry.
- **No permission:** Sellers never see this screen in navigation; direct URL returns "Alert policy is managed by your admin." with a link to My Alert Settings. Supervisors get read-only with the escalation ladder visible but greyed: "View only — ask an admin to change the policy."
- **Mobile:** Read-and-review on mobile (see current policy, see which rules are locked); editing the escalation ladder and rate caps is desktop-only by design — it is a configuration task, not a field task.

### Daily Digest (email + in-app equivalent)
Absorb the whole non-urgent class into one glanceable message per day so nothing needs to interrupt.

**Key actions:** `Open any item at its exact record` · `Touch these today (pre-cold block, ordered by annual premium)` · `Change my digest time` · `Turn a noisy alert type into digest-only from a footer link`

- **Empty:** We do not send an empty digest. In-app the equivalent panel reads: "Nothing to catch up on — you closed out yesterday clean." Preferences explain the behavior: "We skip the digest on days with nothing to report."
- **Loading:** In-app digest panel shows a skeleton of its three blocks (Needs action, Going cold tomorrow, Yesterday's results). Email is pre-rendered, so no loading state.
- **Error:** In-app: "We couldn't build your digest." with Retry. If the email hard-bounces, the user sees an in-app banner: "We couldn't reach your email — your digest is here instead" with a link to update the address, so a bounced digest never becomes silent data loss.
- **No permission:** Digest content is generated per recipient and scoped to their own book by construction; a supervisor's digest is explicitly labeled "Team view" so a seller receiving a forwarded copy can never mistake team data for their own.
- **Mobile:** Email template is single-column, 44px tap targets, deep links open the app on the exact record; the pre-cold block is capped at the top 5 by annual premium so the email stays scannable on a phone.

### Snoozed & Muted
Make every active silence visible and reversible, so 'mute' never becomes permanent invisible rot.

**Key actions:** `See what's snoozed and until when` · `See muted leads, threads and rule types with their expiry` · `Unmute / unsnooze now` · `Extend a mute`

- **Empty:** "Nothing is muted. If an alert type is bothering you, mute it for a week instead of turning it off for good — we'll bring it back automatically." with a link to My Alert Settings.
- **Loading:** Simple list skeleton; counts in the section headers appear only once loaded (a stale '0 muted' would be actively misleading).
- **Error:** "We couldn't load your muted items. Alerts you muted are still muted." with Retry — stating the current effective state matters more than the failure.
- **No permission:** Own scope only for sellers. Compliance-class rules never appear here because they cannot be muted; if a user searches for them: "Compliance alerts can't be muted."
- **Mobile:** Reachable from the inbox overflow menu; single list with expiry chips and a one-tap Unmute.

### Notification Health (admin)
Prove alerts are actually arriving and being acted on, and catch noise before sellers rebel.

**Key actions:** `View delivery latency (lead.created → delivered → acted) by rule` · `View suppression counts by reason (quiet hours, rate cap, collapsed, muted, duplicate)` · `View push coverage per seller and failed/expired subscriptions` · `View notifications-per-seller-per-day distribution and per-rule ignore rate` · `Drill into a specific delivery's log` · `Nudge sellers with no active device`

- **Empty:** "No delivery data yet. Once alerts start flowing you'll see how fast they arrive and whether anyone acts on them." with a "Send a test alert" button.
- **Loading:** Metric tiles show skeletons with their labels intact; charts render axes first so the layout does not reflow.
- **Error:** "Health metrics are unavailable right now. This doesn't affect alert delivery." — critical reassurance so an admin doesn't start disabling things during a dashboard outage.
- **No permission:** Sellers see only their own delivery history from their inbox ("Why didn't I get this?"), never other sellers'. Supervisors see team aggregates with per-seller ack rates but NOT per-message read receipts. Direct URL for a seller: "Delivery health is available to supervisors and admins."
- **Mobile:** Tiles stack; charts scroll horizontally in their own container; the per-seller table collapses to cards showing name, push coverage and fresh-lead ack rate only.

## Typical automations
- When a purchased lead is created with TCPA consent on file → raise an urgent alert to the owner within seconds with a Call now button, and start the escalation clock.
- When a lead is created without consent on file → raise the same urgent alert but hide Call and Reply, showing only Open lead and the reason.
- When the owner hasn't acknowledged a fresh-lead alert in 60 seconds → re-alert the owner on every registered device.
- When the owner still hasn't acted after 3 minutes → notify the supervisor that a paid lead is unworked. The lead stays in the owner's book; nothing is reassigned.
- When a lead texts or emails back → alert the owner instantly with the message preview and a Reply button, and collapse further messages from that lead into the same alert.
- When an inbound call is missed → alert the owner with Call back, and auto-resolve the alert the moment any outbound call to that lead completes.
- When an appointment is 60 minutes and 15 minutes away → remind the seller (the client-facing reminder is sent by Sequences, not here).
- When a meeting is marked no-show → alert the owner immediately with Call now and Reschedule.
- When a meeting is rescheduled or canceled → cancel the obsolete reminders instead of firing them.
- When a lead crosses the cold threshold → put it in tomorrow's digest, not in an interrupt — unless it exceeds the seller's premium threshold or sits in Quoted or later, in which case interrupt.
- When a lead is one day from going cold → add it to the 'Touch these today' block of the daily digest, sorted by annual premium.
- When any activity is logged on a lead → auto-resolve its open cold, missed-call and nudge alerts.
- When a deal is dropped into a Closed-Won stage → congratulate the owner and broadcast a throttled celebration to the team and the kiosk screen.
- When someone passes you on the leaderboard → notify only if you opted in, at most once a day.
- When a new #1 takes the podium → notify the team once and refresh the kiosk.
- When a lead replies STOP or a number hits DNC → send an un-mutable urgent compliance alert to the owner and strip Call and Reply from every open alert about that lead.
- When Google Calendar auth expires or Aloware webhooks go silent → alert admins and show the affected sellers a banner explaining what is not working.
- When the recipient acts on an alert in-app within 30 seconds → cancel the queued push and drop it from the digest.
- When the recipient is the person who caused the event → send nothing.
- When the same lead generates repeat events within the collapse window → update the existing alert and its counter instead of creating a new one.
- When a user exceeds their hourly interruption cap → roll the overflow into a single 'and N more need you' bundle.
- When it's inside a user's quiet hours → hold non-urgent alerts for the digest, and hold or break through with urgent ones according to that user's explicit choice.
- When a seller ignores the same alert type 10 times in 14 days → offer to move it to the daily digest in one tap.
- At each seller's chosen digest time → send one email of everything still unacted; send nothing if there is nothing.
- When a lead changes owner → withdraw every pending and open alert about it from the previous owner.
- When a push subscription is rejected as expired → prune it and warn the seller that their phone alerts are no longer working.
- When a seller's own email hard-bounces → show the digest in-app instead and prompt them to fix the address.
- When a bulk import finishes → send one summary alert with created / merged / skipped counts, never one per row.
- When a user is deactivated → revoke their devices, stop their digests and withdraw their pending alerts.

## KPIs

## Insurance specifics (Life / FE / IUL) & vertical-agnostic core
Genuinely Life/FE/IUL-specific here: (1) **The P0 tier exists because of this vertical.** Purchased Final Expense leads arrive by ping-post and decay in minutes — no other module in this CRM justifies an interrupt-with-countdown UI, and no generic CRM ships one. (2) **Every actionable alert carries the TCPA payload.** The fresh-lead / inbound-SMS / missed-call notification includes `tcpa_consent` and `consent_certificate_id`, and the router HIDES the Call now / Reply buttons when consent is missing or revoked, degrading to "Open lead" — the alert can never be the thing that causes an unconsented dial. (3) **A compliance class that nobody may mute:** STOP received, DNC hit, consent revoked are forced P0, un-snoozable, un-mutable, un-disableable even by admin, because TCPA liability is per message and personal to the seller. (4) **Underwriting silence is normal.** A Life application sits in Application/Underwriting for weeks with zero lead activity; a naive "7 days without activity" cold alert would scream about every healthy pending policy. Cold rules are therefore **stage-aware** (evaluated against the stage's `closed`/`in_underwriting` flags, not against a hardcoded stage name) — this single nuance is what separates a usable alert from a muted one. (5) **FE vs IUL asymmetry drives thresholds.** A $700 FE annual premium going cold is a digest line; a $6,000 IUL going cold is an interrupt. Per-rule `min_deal_value` and `product_line` filters exist for that reason. How the CORE stays vertical-agnostic: the router itself only knows `rule_key`, `subject_type`, `severity`, `channels`, `collapse_key`, `resolve_condition` and generic numeric/enum threshold fields. It never hardcodes "Final Expense", "IUL", "underwriting" or any stage name — it reads the tenant's configurable stage flags and configurable field values. All insurance flavor lives in three swappable places: the seeded **rule pack** (rows in `notification_rule`), the **message templates**, and the **default preference profile**. Adding a second vertical = shipping a second rule pack; zero router changes.

## ⚔️ Adversarial review (sales-ops critic)

**Verdict:** NEEDS WORK — the anti-noise architecture and silo enforcement are genuinely well-built, but the module ships a P0 tier that iOS web push physically cannot deliver (no action buttons, no custom sound, PWA-install-only) while the SMS fallback is marked non-MVP; it has zero Life policy-lifecycle awareness (no draft date, NSF, lapse, delivery requirement, chargeback) despite correctly diagnosing underwriting silence; it demotes the FE callback — the seller's most-broken promise — to a bundled P2 while interrupting for leads that merely landed; it gates dials on consent but never on the consumer's TCPA calling window; and it carries ~26 MVP features and 7 screens of generic notification-platform machinery that will delay the pipeline and dialer an agency owner is actually buying.

### Must fix — missing for real operation (12)
- ZERO policy-lifecycle alerts — the single biggest omission. The spec correctly notices that Life sits silent in Underwriting for weeks, then does nothing with that insight. Post-submission is where FE commissions are actually won and lost, and none of it is here: carrier decision received, APS/medical requirement outstanding, policy approved/issued, DELIVERY REQUIREMENTS outstanding, FIRST DRAFT DATE TOMORROW (call to confirm funds — this is the highest-ROI alert in Final Expense and it is absent), draft NSF/payment failed, lapse-pending, free-look expiry, chargeback-window warning. Declare and consume policy.decision_received, policy.requirement_outstanding, policy.draft_upcoming, policy.payment_failed, policy.lapse_pending, policy.chargeback_risk. An unpaid first draft = no commission and a clawback; a CRM that doesn't buzz for it is not a life CRM.
- No lead-feed watchdog. integration.disconnected covers Google Calendar and Aloware, but the money-critical feed is the ping-post vendor. 'No leads received from Vendor X in 90 minutes during a buying window', 'Vendor X posts spiking duplicates', 'posted leads missing consent certificate' are owner-level alarms — the owner is spending real dollars per lead and a dead or garbage feed looks exactly like a slow day. Consume lead_source.feed_stale, lead_source.quality_anomaly.
- Scheduled CALLBACKS are demoted to 'bundled into My Day' (activity.due, P2). On an FE floor the promised callback ('I'll call you back at 4 after your check clears') is the most common commitment a seller makes and the most common one broken. A time-specific callback is a P0/P1 interrupt at T-0 with Call now, full stop. As written, the spec interrupts for a lead that landed but not for the lead the seller PROMISED to call — that inversion alone will get the bell distrusted.
- No inbound-call-in-progress signal. Missed-call-after-the-fact is covered; 'YOUR lead is calling you RIGHT NOW' (mailer/TV callbacks, the bread and butter of FE) is not. Consume call.inbound_ringing to pop lead context before the seller answers, and to suppress a duplicate missed-call alert if they do.
- No 'seller is currently on a call' state. A desktop takeover with a soft repeating chime while the seller is closing a $6,000 IUL is how this feature gets disabled in week one. Use Aloware call state (call.started / answered) to hold P0 interrupts until wrap-up, then deliver with the age counter still running.
- No first-touch SLA alert distinct from the landing alert. Fresh-lead P0 fires at minute 0; if it is acknowledged (tapped) but never dialed, or dialed once and abandoned, nothing ever fires again. Owners buy on 'no paid lead dies untouched', which needs a lead-level SLA (no completed call attempt in 30 min / no 3rd attempt in 24h), not just an ack on a toast.
- Nothing handles the seller who is offline, sick, or on PTO while their purchased leads keep landing. The spec is right to refuse reassignment, but it stops at 'notify the supervisor' with no lever: the supervisor gets awareness and can do literally nothing. Declare the dependency explicitly — an admin-only, audited 'change owner' in the Leads module plus an away/PTO flag that pauses that seller's vendor posting — otherwise the escalation alert is a receipt for money already lost.
- No Aloware agent identity in the data model. There is staff_sms_number but no aloware_user_id / agent_call_number on notification_channel_setting or the user record. Two-Legged Call needs to know which leg to ring, and every inbound webhook (call.missed, sms.received) must resolve an Aloware user to OUR recipient to enforce the silo. Without this mapping the entire delivery path and the ownership check are undefined.
- Consent restoration is missing. consent.revoked strips Call/Reply from open notifications; there is no consent.captured / consent.updated to put them back when the seller re-obtains consent or the vendor certificate lands late. Sellers will be permanently locked out of dialing leads whose consent arrived out of order.
- No age-band / birthday trigger. FE premiums are age-banded and IUL illustrations are age-priced; a lead crossing an age band is a concrete, vertical-specific reason to call today and it costs almost nothing to emit. Also missing: quote/illustration expiry.
- The fresh-lead latency KPI is measured from lead.created, which is OUR record-creation timestamp. The clock the money runs on starts at vendor post receipt. Measure vendor_post_received_at -> notification.delivered -> notification.acted(call_now), or you can hit 'median < 5s' while consistently losing leads to intake and dedupe latency.
- No durable scheduler is named for the things that require one: escalation steps, snooze wake-ups, digest sends, meeting reminders at 60m/15m, pre-cold at day 6, draft-date warnings. On a <$100/month budget this is the real infra decision in this module and it is unaddressed.

### Too generic / boilerplate (7)
- The whole noise-control stack is Slack/Jira-grade platform boilerplate wearing insurance clothes: severity tiers + quiet hours + rate caps + collapse keys + cross-channel suppression window + snooze + mute + per-rule thresholds + fatigue loop + health dashboard = SEVEN overlapping mute mechanisms for 50 commission-paid sellers who want MORE alerts, not fewer. Replace with three things that are actually FE/IUL-shaped: (a) P0 is reserved for purchased-lead landed + inbound SMS/call + compliance, and NOTHING else can ever be P0; (b) collapse_key + auto-resolve (the only two controls that kill real duplicates); (c) three shipped profiles — 'Hunter' (everything instant), 'Closer' (only my book's replies + appointments), 'Digest' — instead of a 25-rule x 3-channel x 4-mode matrix.
- 'Quiet hours per user timezone' is the wrong quiet hours for this vertical. A commission FE seller does not want the CRM deciding it is too late for him; what he needs and what the spec never mentions is the CONSUMER calling window — TCPA 8am-9pm in the LEAD's local time plus stricter state rules (and state-specific Sunday/holiday bans). The Call now button on a P0 fresh-lead alert must be time-gated by the lead's state and displayed as 'Callable at 8:02am ET' — otherwise this module is the thing that manufactures the violation. Rewrite quiet_hours as (1) an optional personal do-not-buzz and (2) a mandatory, non-configurable lead-side call-window gate on every dial action.
- The per-rule threshold vocabulary (min_deal_value + stage_scope + product_line_scope + lead_source_scope, per user, per rule) is exactly the blank-canvas condition builder the anti-patterns forbid — it is a WHERE clause with a nicer label, and it is 'simple to use, brutal to administer' at 25 rules x 50 sellers. Cut to two knobs the floor actually uses: 'only interrupt me above $X annual premium' and 'purchased leads only'. Then ADD the one scope axis that is real in US life and is missing entirely: lead STATE vs the seller's resident/non-resident license footprint — a Texas-only agent being interrupted for a Florida FE lead is noise AND an unlicensed-solicitation risk.
- Severity names are generic product-speak ('Act Now / Needs You Today / FYI / Digest'). On a life telesales floor the taxonomy that maps to behavior is: DIAL NOW (fresh purchased lead, inbound SMS/call, no-show), MONEY AT RISK (draft date, lapse-pending, underwriting requirement outstanding, pre-cold on a $6k IUL), COMPLIANCE (STOP/DNC/consent), FYI. Naming the tiers after the money moment is what stops the next designer from filing 'automation failed' as 'Needs You Today'.
- 'Snooze 60 seconds' on a ping-post lead is a fake affordance invented to fill a button slot. Sixty seconds later the lead has been dialed by three other agencies. The only honest options are Call now, Text now, or 'I can't take it' (which starts the escalation clock immediately rather than after 60s).
- Dismiss-reason capture on the fresh-lead alert offers 'not mine now' — impossible in a silo model where notifications are ownership-scoped and re-checked at render, and no seller will ever fill a reason picker mid-dial. Delete the reason picker; infer everything from notification.dismissed + absence of a call within N minutes.
- Seven screens (Inbox, P0 surface, My Settings, Admin Rules & Escalation, Digest, Snoozed & Muted, Health dashboard) for a notifications module in a <$100/month, 50-seat product. 'Snoozed & Muted' is a section inside My Alert Settings, not a screen; 'Notification Health' is three numbers on the existing admin page. Screen count is itself the schedule risk that delays the pipeline and dialer.

### Bloat to cut (11)
- notification_rule_engagement table + notification.fatigue_detected + the 'you've skipped this 14 times' nudge. Per-user-per-rule rolling aggregates for 50 users is analytics infrastructure built to police a noise problem that a hard P0 whitelist prevents by construction. Compute ignore rate on demand from notification/notification_delivery when someone asks. Cut the table, cut the event.
- Notification Health dashboard with latency percentiles, suppression breakdowns and per-seller distributions. What is actually needed is one admin line — 'sellers with no working push device' — and a per-notification delivery log for 'why didn't I get this?'. The rest is a dashboard nobody opens twice.
- Rate caps with overflow bundling (marked MVP). With collapse keys, actor suppression, auto-resolve and a P0 whitelist, a 60-push hour cannot happen unless the rule catalog is wrong. Ship without caps, measure, add later. Every cap you ship is a lead someone didn't hear about.
- Admin escalation-ladder editor (configurable steps, delays, targets, stop conditions, max_steps) — that is a workflow engine UI for what should be two numbers seeded in the rule pack: re-alert owner at 60s, notify supervisor at 3m. Keep the escalation_policy table, kill the screen.
- Full template/i18n admin editor with preview and 'test send to a chosen user'. Keep versioned templates in the DB (cheap, enables Spanish later); cut the editor UI and the send-as-another-user path — it is an impersonation surface with an audit trail bolted on to compensate.
- 'While you were away' catch-up card — duplicates the Attention Inbox opened at 'needs action'. One surface, not two.
- kiosk as a value in notification_delivery.channel. The kiosk is an unauthenticated broadcast surface, not a per-recipient delivery. Modeling it as a delivery row per recipient will either create 50 phantom rows per close or a single row with no recipient. Remove it from the channel enum; celebration is a broadcast on the live transport.
- notification.suppressed as a bus event. Emitting an event every time you decide NOT to do something inverts the cost model — on a heavy lead-buy day suppression events will outnumber notifications, and this is the budget line that eats the <$100/month. It is a column (suppressed_reason) already on notification_delivery. Delete the event.
- Cross-channel suppression window as a 30-second timer job. Same outcome with no timer: at push-send time, check read_at/acted_at and skip. Keep the behavior, drop the scheduled machinery.
- Staff SMS 'reserved but not built' — this one is inverted, not bloat to cut but bloat to PROMOTE: see the iOS problem below. Reserving the slot while shipping a P0 tier that iOS cannot honor is the wrong half to build.
- Four-axis per-rule thresholds and the 25-row x 3-column x 4-mode preference matrix (300 cells per seller). Ship three named profiles plus a per-rule Off.

### Event gaps (11)
- notification.acted lists comms-aloware, calendar-scheduling and pipeline-opportunities as consumers — that turns an audit event into a COMMAND channel and makes Notifications a controller over modules it doesn't own. Combined with the module's own idempotency story (replays 'update rather than duplicate'), a redelivered notification.acted could re-fire a dial. Inline actions must call the owning module's command directly (Comms fires the Two-Legged Call and emits call.initiated); notification.acted stays a pure attribution/telemetry event consumed only by analytics.
- notification.celebration_broadcast is emitted by Notifications from opportunity.closed_won, carrying deal_value — Notifications is synthesizing a business fact about Earnings it does not own, in a module whose own verdict says it 'owns no business truth'. Leaderboard owns ranking and celebration; it should emit leaderboard.celebration (already recalculating for the real-time board) and Notifications should DELIVER it. As written there are two independent paths to the kiosk (celebration_broadcast and podium_changed) that can disagree on ordering.
- lead.pre_cold_warning declares a fallback: 'if this event is not produced upstream, Notifications derives the block by querying leads at cold_threshold minus one day.' A module querying another module's tables when an event is missing is exactly the drift the event backbone exists to prevent. Pick an owner (Leads/Pipeline emits it) and make it a hard dependency, not a fallback.
- sms.received is shipped with an unresolved alias ('canonical name to be confirmed, alias sms.inbound_received') for the module's own self-declared second-highest-value alert. Unnamed events don't wire. Same problem on call.missed: Aloware delivers dispositions on a completed-call webhook, so 'call.missed' vs 'call.completed(disposition=missed/abandoned)' must be decided now — right now the spec consumes BOTH and uses call.completed as the resolver for call.missed, which is a race whenever the two webhooks arrive out of order.
- Subject-type mismatch in auto-resolve. notification carries a single subject_type/subject_id, cold alerts are lead-scoped, and opportunity.closed_won is told to 'cancel every open cold/nudge notification for that opportunity'. There is no declared lead<->opportunity resolution join, so either cold alerts survive a closed-won (the exact 'don't tell me to call someone I already closed' failure the module promises to kill) or a hidden denormalized lead_id is required on notification. Add lead_id AND opportunity_id to the notification row and define resolution across both.
- earnings.updated is consumed 'to enrich the congratulation body' with no ordering guarantee against opportunity.closed_won. Either the congrats fires with a stale total or it waits on an event that may never arrive. Take the total from the closed_won/leaderboard payload and stop consuming earnings.updated.
- Namespace inconsistency: push.subscription_registered / push.subscription_expired sit outside the notification.* namespace this module owns. Rename to notification.push_subscription_registered/_expired or declare a push.* namespace explicitly — otherwise the global catalog has an orphan prefix owned by nobody.
- 'Most Improved' recognition is named in insurance_specifics, in features, and in the fairness mitigation — but there is no consumed event for it (leaderboard.most_improved_changed) and no emitted one. The single explicit mitigation for an all-time never-reset board is unwired.
- Missing consumed events with no home: consent.captured/updated (nothing ever restores stripped Call/Reply actions), call.inbound_ringing, call.recording_ready (only summary_ready is consumed — recording and transcript arrive as separate Aloware webhooks), sms.delivery_failed vs sms.failed naming, and any Aloware webhook-heartbeat event that would let integration.disconnected actually fire for 'webhooks stopped arriving' (nothing is declared as the detector — silence has no event).
- notification.escalated has no counterpart notification.escalation_stopped/resolved, so nothing downstream can close the loop on 'the supervisor was alarmed and then it got handled'. Similarly notification_mute emits mute_created but there is no snooze event, no mute_expired — asymmetric lifecycle that makes the Snoozed & Muted screen unauditable.
- meeting.starting_soon is consumed as the trigger for seller reminders at 60m/15m, but the offsets are per-user preferences — meaning the emitter would have to know each recipient's offsets. Either the calendar module emits one event per configured offset (leaking preference ownership) or Notifications schedules off meeting.scheduled/rescheduled. Decide; as written the reminder chain has no defined scheduler.

### 🚨 Silo violations (7)
- notification.celebration_broadcast defaults to broadcasting deal_value + product_line + seller name to every logged-in client and the kiosk, gated only by a tenant show_amount flag whose default is unstated. The public contract is TOTAL Earnings standings on the leaderboard — not 'Maria just closed a $6,400 IUL' pushed to 49 other sellers. That is per-deal business data about another seller's book leaking through the recognition channel. Default show_amount = false; broadcast 'closed a policy' + the new total that is already public.
- leaderboard.rank_changed reaction says 'Public class: no ownership check, everyone is a legitimate recipient.' On an all-time REAL-TIME board every close re-ranks, so this reads as N sellers x every rank shift. Scope recipients to the users whose OWN rank changed (plus the person passed, opt-in) — 'no ownership check' is not the same as 'notify everyone', and conflating them turns the motivational module into the noisiest rule in the catalog.
- note.mention_created is described as 'the only cross-user message path' and justified by 'supervisor coaching', but the permission table never restricts who may mention whom. As specified, seller A can @mention seller B on A's own lead and push a note excerpt containing consumer PII plus a deep link into B's inbox — a direct silo breach, and the deep link then 403s, which is a worse experience than not sending it. Restrict mention notifications to supervisor/admin -> seller, and validate the recipient's access to the subject before the notification row is even created.
- The escalation ladder sends 'unworked fresh lead' to the supervisor with, per the permissions table, 'full detail allowed'. Supervisor global visibility is legitimate, but detail here should be the minimum that supports coaching — seller, vendor, minutes unacked, count — not lead PII replicated into a second table with its own retention. Every consumer PII copy in notification.title/body is a CCPA erasure surface, and the cascade that redacts it is marked non-MVP while the writes are MVP. That ordering is backwards.
- 'Nudge sellers with no active device' as an admin action on the Health screen has no defined channel and sits one step from becoming supervisor-to-seller messaging — the beginning of an in-app chat nobody scoped. Define it as an in-app banner on the seller's own settings screen, or cut it.
- escalation_policy.target allows admin|supervisor|owner and the module correctly refuses a reassignment field, which is good — but nothing prevents a future consumer from treating notification.escalated as a work-claim signal. State the invariant in the event contract itself (a required 'visibility_only: true' or an explicit 'consumers MUST NOT mutate ownership' clause), since the doc's prose guard disappears the moment someone reads only the event catalog.
- Supervisor rollup counts 'sellers with zero first-touch in X hours' — fine — but the digest permission note says a supervisor digest is labeled 'Team view' so a forwarded copy isn't mistaken for a seller's own. If a supervisor digest containing team data can be forwarded at all, it should carry no lead-level rows, only per-seller counts. Say so.

## Risks & dependencies
- HARD DEPENDENCY on a reliable event bus with at-least-once delivery and a stable event vocabulary. Every rule in this module is a contract with another module; a renamed event silently stops an alert, and a stopped alert looks exactly like a quiet day. Event-name drift is the single largest operational risk here.
- Aloware webhooks retry and can arrive out of order (recording after summary, disposition after completion). Idempotency on source_event_id and update-in-place on collapse_key are non-negotiable, not optimizations.
- iOS web push only works from a PWA installed to the Home Screen. Sellers on iPhone who merely bookmark the site will get NO phone alerts while believing they are covered — the biggest single threat to the speed-to-lead promise. Mitigations: is_pwa_installed tracking, an onboarding step that forces a successful test push, an admin coverage report, and the reserved staff-SMS channel as a paid fallback.
- Real-time transport (websockets) can be the most expensive line item against the <$100/month infra target at 50 concurrent sellers. Fallback ladder: SSE, then short polling for in-app while push carries the urgent tier. Degrading to polling must not degrade the P0 path.
- Email deliverability: the digest requires an authenticated sending domain (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and bounce handling. A silently spam-foldered digest is indistinguishable from a working one without the health dashboard.
- Notification bodies are a shadow copy of lead PII (names, message previews) living outside the lead table, plus a copy inside push payloads at the OS level. CCPA erasure must cascade into notifications and delivery logs; push payloads should carry minimal identifying content. Unbounded retention also quietly consumes the infra budget.
- Timezone correctness across 50 US sellers: quiet hours and digest times must use the USER timezone. A tenant-level default guarantees somebody is woken at 5am and permanently disables push.
- Escalation could be misread by a future implementer as 'reassign the lead', which would silently reintroduce a routing engine and break the defining architectural promise. Mitigated structurally: escalation_policy has no reassignment field.
- Boundary risk with Comms/Sequences: appointment reminders and follow-ups exist on BOTH sides (to the lead, and to the seller). Without an explicit rule that Notifications never messages a lead, we will build the same reminder twice and, worse, create an untracked TCPA-exposed path outside Aloware's consent gating.
- Boundary risk with Leaderboard: this module delivers the celebration signal, Leaderboard owns the podium, the kiosk and the rank math. If both compute rank-change logic we get contradictory alerts.
- Dependency on Pipeline exposing STAGE FLAGS (closed/won/lost, and ideally an underwriting/pending flag) as data rather than names. Without a pending/underwriting flag, cold alerts will fire on healthy Life applications and the cold rule will be muted org-wide within a month.
- Dependency on Lead Intake carrying source, vendor, tcpa_consent and consent_certificate_id on lead.created, and on the event being emitted at ingestion time rather than at first read — the age counter is only honest if it starts at the vendor's post.
- Over-alerting is this module's characteristic failure mode and it is not preventable by design alone; without the engagement telemetry and the fatigue nudge, the anti-noise claim is unverifiable in production.
- CUT — team chat / internal messaging inbox: there is no collaboration surface in a silo model; @mentions for supervisor coaching cover the real need.
- CUT — a generic condition builder for notification rules: violates the plain-English trigger vocabulary pattern and produces the 'simple to use, brutal to administer' anti-pattern. Fixed rule catalog + a handful of thresholds instead.
- CUT — native mobile apps with APNs/FCM: cost and maintenance far outside the budget; PWA web push covers it, with the iOS caveat tracked as an open question.
- CUT — staff SMS alerts in MVP: per-segment cost duplicating a free channel; kept as a reserved channel slot only.
- CUT — per-message read receipts visible to supervisors ('did you open my alert'): surveillance with negligible coaching value. Replaced by aggregate ack rates and SLA-breach alerts.
- CUT — Slack/Teams connectors: no evidence the client uses them; would be the first piece of agency bloat.
- CUT — user-authored custom alert rules: a blank canvas that guarantees noise nobody can support. Thresholds on curated rules deliver 90% of the value.

## Open questions
- Should the team celebration show the actual annual premium, or just 'closed a policy'? Motivation argues for the number; a $12k IUL next to a $600 FE may demotivate the FE-only sellers, and it leaks book-size signal across silos. Proposed default: show the number, tenant-configurable.
- Does every seller have an iPhone, and will they install the PWA to the Home Screen? If iOS push coverage lands below ~90%, we need budget approval for staff SMS as the urgent-tier fallback — this decision gates whether speed-to-lead actually works in the field.
- Do leads get purchased overnight? If a ping-post lead lands at 2am, does the urgent alert break quiet hours, get held until wake time, or does the agency simply not buy overnight? The answer changes both the default policy and the escalation ladder.
- What interruption ceiling per seller per day will the client accept? We need a number to tune rate caps against, otherwise 'not noisy' is unfalsifiable.
- Should 'someone passed you on the leaderboard' exist at all? Research says never-reset revenue boards demote most of the team; we propose it off by default and capped, but the client's motivational philosophy should decide.
- Do supervisors want unacked-fresh-lead alerts in real time, or only in a daily rollup? Real time is more effective and more surveillance-flavored; this is a culture decision, not a technical one.
- Does the Pipeline module expose an 'underwriting / awaiting carrier' stage flag? Without it, stage-aware cold suppression cannot be implemented cleanly and Life applications will generate false cold alerts.
- Confirm the canonical inbound-SMS and inbound-email event names with the Comms/Aloware module (we consume sms.received and email.received; aliases sms.inbound_received / email.reply_received). Same for lead.pre_cold_warning — if Lead Lifecycle does not emit it, Notifications must derive it by query.
- Retention window for notifications and delivery logs: 90 days keeps storage inside budget but destroys long-window engagement analysis. Proposal: full rows 90 days, aggregated engagement retained indefinitely.
- Does the kiosk/TV screen live on an authenticated always-on session, and who owns its token? Celebration delivery depends on it, and an unauthenticated wall display showing seller earnings has its own privacy question.
- Should admins be able to fire an ad-hoc broadcast announcement to all sellers ('leads paused today, don't wait by the phone')? Cheap to add, obviously useful, and the fastest thing to abuse into noise — needs an explicit rate limit if approved.
- Should the daily digest also include yesterday's leaderboard movement, or does that belong exclusively to the Leaderboard module's own email? Risk of two competing daily emails to the same 50 people.
