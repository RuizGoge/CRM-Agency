# 12. Administration & Audit

> **Module id:** `admin-audit` · part of [`docs/02-functional-map.md`](../02-functional-map.md)

## Verdict
KEEP — but RESHAPE into two clearly separated surfaces under one module, because today's brief reads like a settings junk drawer and that is exactly how admin modules rot. (1) **Configuration Console** — the runtime contract the whole organism reads: users/roles/scopes, pipeline+stage flags, required-field gating, custom fields, loss reasons, cold threshold, products/carriers, integrations, branding, locale. Nothing here is "settings"; every row is a variable that other modules evaluate at runtime, so every change must emit an event and show its blast radius before saving. (2) **Audit & Compliance Ledger** — an append-only evidence store (audit entries, TCPA consent records, opt-out/DNC suppression, CCPA privacy requests, retention, legal hold). Same module because both are backed by the same immutable event stream and the same actor identity, and because the compliance story is what makes this product sellable to a third-party agency owner. Two things I explicitly REFUSE to build here: a lead-routing/round-robin/assignment configuration (it contradicts the per-seller silo architecture — the ONLY sanctioned bulk reassignment is book-of-business transfer on seat deactivation), and a custom-role builder / per-field ACL matrix (that is the "simple to use, brutal to administer" anti-pattern; three fixed tiers + supervisor scope + a PII flag on fields covers 100% of the real cases at 50 seats).

## Purpose & value
It is the single place where the owner decides how the CRM behaves — who sees what, what the stages are, what counts as a sale, when a lead goes cold, how Aloware is wired — and the single place that can prove, on demand, who did what and that every call and text had consent, which is what keeps the agency out of TCPA/CCPA trouble and what a prospective third-party client will ask about before signing.

## Screens & states (15)

### Admin Home / Config Health
One glance at whether the system is actually configured to work: integration health, unmapped agents, gating gaps, expiring licenses, pending privacy requests.

**Key actions:** `Fix an issue via deep link` · `View recent admin activity` · `Jump to any configuration area` · `Dismiss a resolved warning`

- **Empty:** Fresh tenant, nothing configured yet. Headline: 'Let's get your agency running.' Body: 'Four things to set up before your team starts dialing.' Checklist with buttons: 'Invite your sellers', 'Choose your pipeline template', 'Connect Aloware', 'Add your first lead source'. Footer link: 'Start from the Final Expense + IUL template' — one click seeds stages, required fields, loss reasons and products.
- **Loading:** Skeleton tiles for each health card; health checks stream in independently so a slow Aloware ping never blocks the page.
- **Error:** Per-card failure only: 'Couldn't check Aloware connection status.' with 'Retry'. The rest of the page stays usable.
- **No permission:** Sellers and supervisors never see this route; navigation omits it and a direct URL renders 'This area is for administrators. Ask your agency admin if you need a change here.' with a link back to their pipeline.
- **Mobile:** Health cards stack vertically; checklist is thumb-sized. Read and fix-by-link only — structural editing (stage designer, field designer) is intentionally desktop-first.

**Key actions:** `Invite user` · `Change role` · `Assign supervisor scope` · `Map Aloware agent` · `Deactivate (opens book transfer)` · `Resend/revoke invite` · `Export user list`

- **Mobile:** Card list instead of table; role badge and Aloware-mapping badge always visible. Invite and role change work on mobile; book transfer is desktop-only by design.

## Typical automations
- When a seller is deactivated -> block the login immediately, pause all their running sequences, and open the book-transfer flow with the exact counts of leads, open opportunities and scheduled meetings that need a new owner.
- When a book transfer is confirmed -> reassign leads, opportunities and future meetings in one atomic batch, keep closed-won history attributed to the departing seller, re-rank the leaderboard, and notify the receiving seller with a summary of what landed in their book.
- When no Aloware event has arrived for 30 minutes during business hours -> mark the integration degraded, show a banner to admins, and email every admin with what has stopped working (calls, texts, recordings).
- When an Aloware webhook fails signature validation or exhausts retries -> park it in the dead-letter queue, keep processing everything else, and alert admins once per hour with a replay link rather than per event.
- When an SMS opt-out (STOP) arrives -> add the number to the suppression list across all channels, halt every sequence touching that contact, stamp the lead timeline, and show the do-not-contact badge on the card before the seller can dial.
- When a lead arrives from a vendor without a consent certificate -> flag it 'consent missing', hard-block SMS, require an explicit acknowledgment before a call, and count it against that vendor's quality score.
- When a lead arrives whose phone or email is already on the suppression list -> accept the lead but mark it do-not-contact immediately, so the seller never learns the hard way.
- When an admin changes a stage's closed-won or closed-lost flag -> show the affected opportunity count and Earnings delta before saving, then recalculate Earnings, re-rank the public leaderboard, and post a system note explaining why ranks moved.
- When an admin archives a stage that still holds opportunities -> require a destination stage and move them in the same transaction; never allow a publish that would orphan a card.
- When a required field is added to a Closed-Won stage -> list the deals already sitting there that now fail the rule and let the admin fix or grandfather them explicitly.
- When the cold threshold changes -> re-evaluate every open lead overnight and refresh the rotting indicators on all boards.
- When a duplicate contact is merged -> merge the consent records keeping the earliest verifiable certificate and apply strictest-wins on opt-outs, so a merge can never resurrect a suppressed number.
- When an import matches a contact that belongs to a different seller -> send it to the admin duplicate queue instead of creating it, because a silent cross-silo duplicate means two agents calling the same person.
- When an export exceeds the large-export threshold -> require a written reason before it runs, and email every admin the moment it completes with who, what scope and how many rows.
- When mass export, mass delete, suppression removal, off-hours bulk activity or a long impersonation session is detected -> raise an anomaly alert to all admins in-app and by email within the same hour.
- When a privacy deletion request is identity-verified -> anonymize the contact's personal fields across leads, activities, messages, recordings and transcripts, keep the de-identified opportunity and Earnings history intact, and generate a fulfillment certificate.
- When a privacy request reaches day 35 of its 45-day window -> escalate to all admins daily until it is fulfilled.
- When a deletion or retention purge targets a record under legal hold -> skip it, log the skip with the hold reference, and tell the admin why.
- When a state license, carrier appointment or E&O policy is 60/30/7 days from expiry -> notify the seller and their supervisor, and at expiry soft-block marking new business Closed-Won in that state.
- When an admin starts an impersonation session -> show a persistent banner, block all outbound calls, texts and emails for the duration, auto-expire the session, and write the session into the impersonated user's own visible history.
- When branding is updated -> push the new logo and accent color to the app shell, email templates and the kiosk leaderboard without a redeploy.
- When a new tenant applies a vertical pack -> seed the pipeline, stages, closed flags, required fields, custom fields, loss reasons and product catalog in one step, then hand the admin the four-item setup checklist.

## KPIs
- Aloware mapping completeness: % of active sellers with a valid aloware_user_id (target 100% — anything less means calls are landing nowhere)
- Webhook processing success rate and dead-letter depth (target >99.5%, DLQ trending to zero)
- Time-to-detect an integration outage: minutes from last successful Aloware event to integration.health_degraded firing (target <30 min)
- Consent coverage at intake: % of leads from registered vendors that arrive with a stored consent certificate (target ~100%; the gap is the TCPA exposure)
- Opt-out enforcement leakage: count of outbound calls/SMS/emails to a suppressed identifier (hard target 0 — this is the metric that keeps the agency out of court)
- Earnings gate integrity: % of Closed-Won transitions that passed required-field gating without an admin override (target >98%)
- Config safety: opportunities orphaned or silently moved by a configuration publish (target 0), and number of publishes rolled back within 24 hours
- Privacy SLA: median and worst-case days from privacy.request_received to privacy.request_fulfilled against the 45-day window (target median <10 days, zero breaches)
- Audit coverage: % of mutating actions with a corresponding audit_entry (target 100%; measured by sampling module events against the log)
- Export governance: number of large exports per month and % with a recorded reason; time from export.requested to admin acknowledgment on anomaly alerts
- Dedupe effectiveness: duplicate rate surviving into sellers' books after import and intake (target <1%), and cross-seller duplicate collisions caught by review (should be non-zero — zero means the check is not running)
- Configuration bloat: custom fields with zero values after 60 days and stages with zero throughput in 90 days (prompt to archive)
- Credential compliance: active sellers with an expired state license or carrier appointment (target 0)
- Admin self-service ratio: % of configuration changes made by the agency owner without developer involvement (the real test of 'configurable, not brutal to administer')
- Storage cost per active seat per month for recordings, transcripts and audit data (must keep total infrastructure under the $100/month target)

## Insurance specifics (Life / FE / IUL) & vertical-agnostic core
Genuinely Life/FE/IUL-specific in this module, and none of it belongs in the core: (1) **TCPA consent as a first-class stored artifact.** FE and IUL leads are bought by ping-post from vendors and decay in minutes; the vendor sends a consent certificate (URL/id, origin IP, origin URL, exact disclosure wording, timestamp) with the post. Every other CRM throws that away. Here the lead_source registry forces a mapping for it at intake, and a lead without it is flagged, SMS-blocked and counted against the vendor's quality score. This is the single most insurance-specific thing in the entire product. (2) **Suppression that must be ours, not just Aloware's.** Aloware provides STIR/SHAKEN, A2P 10DLC, DNC scrubbing and TCPA-approval gating for its own sends — but our sequences, appointment reminders and click-to-call must honor the same list, so we maintain an internal suppression ledger fed by Aloware STOP webhooks and enforce it fail-closed. (3) **Recorded-call retention.** Insurance sales calls are recorded and transcribed by default; two-party-consent states and carrier requirements make retention windows a legal setting, not a storage optimization. (4) **Agent credentials.** State licenses, NPN, carrier appointments and E&O are prerequisites to writing business; an expired license turns a Closed-Won into a chargeback and a regulatory event, so the registry can soft-block Closed-Won by state. (5) **Annual premium as the money field.** The required-field gate ships pre-wired to demand deal_value (annual premium in USD), product line and carrier before Closed-Won, because Earnings and the public leaderboard are computed from exactly that. (6) **Loss reasons that are underwriting-shaped** — "doesn't qualify (health/age)" and "no funds" are FE realities, not generic sales objections. **How the core stays vertical-agnostic:** none of the above is hardcoded. The core knows only generic primitives — pipelines with stages carrying is_closed/closed_type, typed custom fields with a PII class, a controlled loss-reason vocabulary, a product catalog, a credential registry, a consent ledger keyed by (identifier, channel), and a suppression ledger. Everything insurance-flavored ships as a seeded, versioned **vertical configuration pack** (Life: FE + IUL) applied at tenant setup and fully editable afterward. Adding a second vertical means authoring a new pack — new stages, fields, loss reasons, products, credential types — with zero schema change and zero code branch. The only insurance concept with real gravity in the core is the consent/suppression ledger, and that is defensible: any US outbound-calling vertical needs exactly the same thing.

## ⚔️ Adversarial review (sales-ops critic)

**Verdict:** NEEDS WORK — the governance skeleton is right, but this is a generic SaaS admin console wearing an insurance label: it never decides when a life sale counts as Earnings (submitted vs issued vs paid) or how a chargeback reverses a PUBLIC all-time leaderboard, it has no calling-window/quiet-hours engine and no TrustedForm/Jornaya certificate claiming (the only real TCPA defense), it gives ping-post lead sources no seller owner and inbound calls no owner at all (both of which quietly require the forbidden routing engine), it has no suppression.created event so in-flight Power Dialer lists and Aloware sequences keep dialing after STOP, and it leaks cross-seller lead content to supervisors through exports, audit diffs and evidence packs.

### Must fix — missing for real operation (13)
- EARNINGS RECOGNITION POLICY — the single biggest omission. The spec defines Earnings as sum(deal_value) in Closed-Won stages and never asks WHEN a life sale counts: application submitted, policy issued, or first premium drafted. In FE, 20-40% of submitted business never issues (declines, not-taken, first-draft NSF) and early lapses/chargebacks are routine. With a PUBLIC ALL-TIME board and no recognition policy, the leaderboard is a lie inside 60 days and agents will game it by submitting junk. This module must own `earnings_recognition_basis` (submitted | issued | paid_first_premium) plus an audited reversal/chargeback path that de-ranks the board with a reason.
- CALLING-WINDOW / QUIET-HOURS ENGINE — nothing in this admin module configures time-of-day compliance. Federal TCPA is 8am-9pm in the CALLED PARTY's local time; several states are stricter and some restrict Sundays/holidays. Aloware will not enforce this for OUR sequences, OUR appointment reminders, OUR click-to-call or OUR power-dialer list pushes. Needs: per-state calling windows with a federal default, holiday blackout calendar, contact-timezone derivation (state + area code, stored on contact), and a hard pre-send/pre-dial gate. A 50-agent floor calling coast-to-coast without this generates violations daily.
- TRUSTEDFORM / JORNAYA (LeadiD) CERTIFICATE HANDLING — the spec stores a generic `certificate_url` / `certificate_id`. A dead URL is not a TCPA defense. Needs: claim/verify the certificate at intake (TrustedForm certs expire unless claimed and retained), store the claimed copy, capture which seller/brand names appeared in the disclosure (post-IMC v. FCC the operative standard is prior express written consent naming the seller), record the disclosure text verbatim, and alert on certificates approaching their retention expiry. This is the difference between a purchased lead and a purchased liability.
- VERBAL REVOCATION CAPTURE — the suppression ledger is fed by Aloware STOP webhooks, email unsubscribes and manual entry. In FE, revocation is almost always SPOKEN on the call ('take me off your list'). Needs a one-tap agent action and a call-disposition mapping that writes suppression instantly, plus the current FCC posture: revocation by any reasonable method, honored across ALL channels, not limited to keywords, within 10 business days. Today a verbal opt-out lives in a note and gets called again next week.
- CALL-RECORDING CONSENT POLICY — mentioned once, as a throwaway justification for retention windows. Needs real config: per-state recording disclosure policy (two-party states), whether recording is auto-on, a per-call recording-consent flag carried into the timeline and the evidence pack, and an explicit decision on whether SUPERVISORS may listen to recordings — which is the actual mechanism the supervisor tier exists for and is nowhere defined.
- INBOUND-CALL OWNERSHIP IN A SILO — `aloware_user_id` maps outbound identity only. An inbound call from an unknown number with no matching contact currently belongs to nobody, and the forbidden fix is round-robin. This module must define the non-routing rule: a per-seller DID / tracking-number registry (number dialed determines owner) plus an admin-only unassigned inbox for true strays. Without it, either calls vanish from the timeline or someone quietly builds routing.
- PER-SELLER LEAD SOURCE KEYS + VENDOR ECONOMICS — `lead_source` has cost_per_lead and default_pipeline_id but no target seller, no daily cap, no budget, and no return/credit workflow. FE agencies return 10-20% of purchased leads (wrong number, deceased, outside the age band, duplicate) inside a vendor-specific return window. Needs: per-seller posting key, per-source daily cap and spend, return-reason taxonomy, credit tracking, and a duplicate-within-N-days rule that returns to the vendor AND routes back to the ORIGINAL owner rather than opening a new book entry.
- SPEED-TO-LEAD SLA CONFIGURATION — the brief calls speed-to-lead the #1 conversion lever and this module gives the owner exactly one dial: a 7-day cold threshold. Those are different clocks. Needs a fresh-lead SLA (first touch within N seconds/minutes, escalation at N minutes, breach visible per seller and per source) configured here and consumed by intake, notifications and reporting. A ping-post FE lead is dead in minutes; a 7-day cold flag is irrelevant to it.
- LICENSING GATING THAT MATCHES FE REALITY — the credential registry is a good instinct but wrong shape. Missing: an 'appointment pending' state (many FE carriers appoint at first submission, so a hard block would kill sales), per-carrier per-agent WRITING NUMBER (it goes on the application), CE due dates, and — most important — the states-writable list exposed to intake so a seller never works a lead in a state they are not licensed in. Also make hard-vs-soft block a per-carrier decision, not one global optional toggle, and pull it into MVP: an unlicensed writing is a regulatory event and a full chargeback.
- A2P 10DLC BRAND/CAMPAIGN STATUS AND PER-SELLER NUMBER HEALTH — the spec correctly says Aloware provides A2P, then never surfaces it. If the campaign is pending or a number gets flagged/filtered, SMS silently dies and the seller blames the CRM. Needs campaign status, per-number health, and 'SMS blocked, here's why' in the config health widget.
- EARNINGS / DEAL-VALUE CORRECTION OBJECT — the audit section justifies itself with 'earnings disputes', but there is no sanctioned way to FIX a mistyped $12,000 annual premium on a public all-time board. Needs an audited adjustment record (original, corrected, reason, actor) that the leaderboard consumes as a first-class event, so corrections are visible rather than looking like tampering.
- DNC SCRUB CONFIGURATION AND EBR WINDOWS — the spec says Aloware handles DNC and then correctly notes our own sends must honor our list, but never configures the scrub: who holds the SAN for the federal registry, scrub cadence for any non-consented list, per-state registry handling, and automatic evaluation of the existing-business-relationship exemptions (18 months post-transaction, 3 months post-inquiry). Right now 'consent missing' is the only state; EBR does not exist.
- THE AGENCY OWNER'S BUY OBJECTIONS THAT NOTHING HERE ANSWERS: (a) 'prove my agents can't see each other's leads' — there is a matrix viewer but no automated isolation test with a visible pass date; (b) 'what happens to my book if I leave you' — CSV export is not a portability guarantee, and the entire product value IS the book; (c) 'what happens when Aloware goes down or drops us' — no degraded-mode posture, no manual-log fallback, no vendor-exit story for the one integration the timeline depends on; (d) 'my top producer just quit on Friday' — book transfer exists but there is no seat-suspension-freezes-export control, which is the actual moment the book walks out the door.

### Too generic / boilerplate (9)
- Custom field designer is described purely as a type system (text/number/date/picklist/boolean/phone/email) — that paragraph could be lifted into any CRM spec. For THIS product it must ship a Life/FE/IUL field pack as first-class seeded config: face_amount, annual_premium (= deal_value), modal_premium + payment mode, draft_day/draft_date, bank routing + account (sensitive, write-once, never exported, never included in an evidence pack), beneficiary name/relationship, tobacco, height/weight, knockout health conditions, Medicaid/Medicare status, existing coverage, replacement flag, policy_number, and the agent's carrier writing number. The designer exists to EXTEND that pack for vertical #2, not to leave the owner staring at 'Add your first field'.
- Three-tier RBAC + permission matrix viewer + a `permission_rule` table that is explicitly seeded and not user-editable — that is code cosplaying as data, and the whole block reads like every SaaS admin spec. What actually sells here is one generated, read-only isolation proof page ('Seller A cannot see Seller B's leads') backed by an automated silo-isolation test suite whose latest pass date is printed on the page. Ship the proof, delete the rules table.
- Email sending domain configuration (DKIM/SPF/DMARC records, from-name, postal footer block) is textbook marketing-SaaS boilerplate. This is a dial-and-text FE floor; email is receipts, appointment confirmations and CCPA artifacts. One pre-verified transactional domain, zero admin UI. If the agency wants bulk email marketing, that is a different product.
- 'Config blast-radius preview & safe publish' + `config_version` + diff view + one-click rollback of a publish is enterprise change-management ceremony for a SINGLE 50-seat tenant that will change its pipeline maybe four times a year. Collapse to three guarded operations with counted confirms: closed-flag change, stage archive-with-remap, required-field add. Everything else saves immediately.
- Import governance (column mapping, batch history, downloadable error files, full batch rollback) is sized as a flagship — that is a data-migration CRM's centerpiece, not ours. In an FE shop leads arrive by ping-post in seconds; CSV import is the occasional aged-lead list. Demote it; the lead-vendor/ping-post registry is the flagship intake surface and should carry the dedupe policy with it.
- 'Integration health monitor: success rate, latency, error breakdown' is generic APM. The owner does not read p95 latency. The two sentences that matter: 'Maria's calls stopped logging at 9:12am (Aloware agent unmapped)' and 'SMS is blocked — your A2P 10DLC campaign is pending'. Per-seller attribution health and channel-blocked status, not a metrics dashboard.
- Retention policy is a generic data-class builder with a 'projected storage impact' estimator. The rules that actually bind in this vertical are fixed, not configurable: internal do-not-call entries retained 5 years and NEVER purged, consent certificates retained beyond the TCPA statute-of-limitations window (4+ years), call recordings on the shortest window because they are the storage driver against the sub-$100/month target. Ship presets with a legal-hold exemption, not a policy builder.
- 'Security & access log with anomaly alerts' including 'configure alert thresholds' is SOC furniture. Hardcode the four alerts that matter in a book-of-business product: mass export, suppression-list removal, impersonation started, off-hours bulk activity. A threshold configuration screen is one more thing to misconfigure silently.
- Tenant branding with light/dark logo variants, favicon, and a translation-coverage view is standard multi-tenant decoration. Only the kiosk/TV logo + accent color earns MVP (the board is a physical object on a wall). Externalize strings, but a translation-coverage dashboard for a language nobody has enabled is a screen built for a screenshot.

### Bloat to cut (15)
- Per-seller pipeline personalization / `seller_pipeline_override` — cut entirely, not defer. Fifty divergent boards destroy supervisor roll-ups, leaderboard comparability, automation stage bindings and reporting, and the guardrails needed to make it safe cost more than the feature. The client's 'each seller can adjust' is satisfied by a VIEW preference (column order, hidden columns, WIP hints) that touches no data model.
- Vertical configuration packs as versioned, previewable, DIFFABLE bundles — for one tenant with one vertical this is theater. A seed script plus a single 'reset to the Life template' action delivers the entire demonstrable answer to 'how do you add a vertical?'.
- `config_version` + config diff view + one-click rollback of a publish. Nobody will roll back a pipeline publish; they will fix the stage. Keep counted confirms on the three dangerous operations and delete the versioning machinery.
- `permission_rule` table — declared seeded and non-editable, which means it is source code stored in a database, with the drift risk that implies. Authorization in code; render the matrix page from the code.
- Multiple typed pipelines in the MVP UI. Keep the pipeline_id foreign key so vertical #2 is not a refactor; do not build the create-a-second-pipeline flow for an agency that sells FE and IUL out of one funnel.
- `role_visibility` per custom field — this IS the per-field ACL matrix the spec loudly refuses two paragraphs earlier, smuggled in through the field designer. Keep `pii_class` only; visibility follows role tier + PII flag.
- Email sending domain screen (DKIM/SPF/DMARC/verification status) — see generic findings. One pre-verified transactional domain.
- Integration success-rate/latency/error-breakdown analytics. Keep: last-event-received per event type, dead-letter queue, single-event replay. Cut: bulk replay UI and the metrics dashboard.
- Alert threshold configuration in the security log. Hardcode the four alerts.
- 'Dismiss a resolved warning' on the config health widget — a warning that needs manual dismissal is a warning that will be dismissed while still broken. Health items resolve themselves or they are not health items.
- Signed PDF + hash manifest on evidence packs and exports. An unnotarized self-generated hash proves nothing to opposing counsel; a timestamped ZIP of CSV+JSON with the audit trail included is the deliverable.
- 'Projected storage impact' estimator in retention config.
- The three FUTURE items (tenants console, plans/billing, SSO/SCIM) presented as full feature cards with descriptions, complexity ratings and value justifications — that is three cards of noise in a module already accused of being a junk drawer. One paragraph each in a 'deliberately deferred' note.
- Impersonation session time-boxing as configuration. Hardcode 30 minutes. Keep the banner, the dual-actor stamp, and the entry in the impersonated seller's own visible history — those are the trust mechanics; the config knob is not.
- Branding light/dark logo variants + favicon + translation-coverage view. Logo + accent color, applied to shell and kiosk.

### Event gaps (12)
- NO `suppression.created` / `suppression.released` EVENTS. The spec enforces suppression as a synchronous pre-send check only — which does nothing about work already in flight: an Aloware Power Dialer list we already pushed, an Aloware Sequence the contact is already enrolled in, a scheduled appointment reminder. `suppression.created` must fire and be consumed by automation-sequences (cancel enrollments), communications (disenroll from the Aloware sequence, purge the number from any pushed power-dialer list), and calendar-activities (cancel pending reminders). One text to a STOP number after our own pre-send check passed is exactly how this fails.
- NO CONSENT LIFECYCLE EVENTS: `consent.recorded`, `consent.missing`, `consent.expired`, `consent.revoked` are all absent even though `consent_record.expires_at` exists in the data model. The consent-aware intake gate, the amber 'consent missing' lead-card badge and the SMS block have nothing to subscribe to — they are described as UI states with no delivery mechanism.
- NO `legal_hold.applied` / `legal_hold.released` AND NO `privacy_request.received` / `privacy_request.fulfilled` EVENTS, yet the Privacy Requests screen promises a 'do not contact until it's resolved' banner on the affected contact and the deletion engine promises to be blocked by holds. Cross-module state asserted in prose with no event to carry it.
- NO `contact.anonymized` EVENT WITH AN EXPLICIT EARNINGS-NEUTRAL CONTRACT. The deletion engine's entire justification is that anonymize-and-retain must not silently re-rank the public all-time leaderboard months later. That guarantee has to be a declared event contract consumed by leaderboard and reporting ('monetary and stage history survive de-identified; Earnings unchanged'), plus a regression test — not a sentence in a value justification.
- NO `export.requested` / `export.completed` EVENTS. The mass-export security alert, the audit entry, and the expiring-link notification all depend on them; the export_job entity exists with no event surface.
- NO `events_consumed` SECTION AT ALL. This module writes the audit log, so it must consume the mutating events of every other module — and the spec must state that audit_entry is DERIVED FROM THE EVENT BUS, not written by ad-hoc calls at each call site, or the log will have holes precisely where someone bypassed it. At minimum it must consume: lead.created (write consent record + evaluate intake gate), aloware sms.stop_received, email.unsubscribed, call.disposition_logged (verbal opt-out disposition), opportunity.stage_changed, opportunity.value_changed, appointment.* — each producing an audit entry with the originating correlation_id.
- `user.role_changed` lists its consumer as 'all modules (authorization cache invalidation)'. That is not a consumer, it is a shrug. Needs a concrete mechanism: a permission_epoch integer on the user that every request revalidates, or a named session-invalidation event with an ordering guarantee. A demoted admin holding a warm session in a compliance module is not an acceptable race.
- `pipeline.closed_flags_changed` is correctly identified as the highest-blast-radius event in the product and has NO paired `leaderboard.recompute_requested` and no ordering contract against concurrent kanban drags. Who recomputes, in what order, and what the board shows mid-recompute is undefined for the one event that can move a public standings display in front of 50 people.
- `user.book_transferred` carries `closed_won_attribution_kept` but never declares the LEADERBOARD CONTRACT: historical Closed-Won must NOT be reattributed to the receiving seller. Left implicit, the public all-time board silently rewrites history the day someone quits — and the departing seller's replacement inherits their podium spot. State it as a consumer contract and test it.
- `user.credential_expiring` is present-tense and is a scheduled derivation, not a state change — it should be `credential.expiry_warning_raised` under a `credential.*` prefix, with a companion `credential.expired` that actually enforces the Closed-Won block. As written the block is 'optional' and nothing emits when the license is already dead.
- NO `lead_source.created` / `lead_source.updated` EVENT — the ping-post intake endpoint must reload field mapping, consent policy and posting keys without a deploy. Also missing `webhook.signature_invalid` and `integration.degraded` events to feed notifications and the config-health widget; today the Aloware token can expire and the only surface is a timestamp somebody has to go look at.
- NAMING DRIFT: `user.aloware_mapping_changed`, `user.credential_expiring`, `user.book_transferred`, `pipeline.stage_updated`, `pipeline.published` mix entity granularity and tense. Standardize on `<entity>.<past_tense_verb>` and give credentials, config, consent, suppression and exports their own entity prefixes instead of hanging them off `user.*` — otherwise every future consumer subscribes to `user.*` and receives compliance events it has no business seeing.

### 🚨 Silo violations (10)
- `lead_source` has `default_pipeline_id` but NO target seller and no per-seller posting key — which means posted ping-post leads land in a shared pool that something must then distribute. That something is a routing engine, which is explicitly forbidden. Every source key must be seller-scoped (each seller gets their own posting URL/key), or the vendor payload must carry the seller. This is the primary intake path and it currently assumes shared leads.
- Book-of-business transfer lets the admin 'choose destination seller(s)' — plural, with preview counts. That is a lead-distribution console with a different label, and it will be used as one. Constrain to a SINGLE destination seller or an admin holding account, with a mandatory reason, and make splitting a book require a second explicit confirmation naming it as an exception.
- Supervisors get scoped CSV/JSON EXPORT of their sellers' books. That hands the middle tier a bulk extract of up to 20 private books — the exact insider-exfiltration vector this same spec names as the reason exports must be gated. Supervisors get aggregates and counts; row-level export stays admin-only, reason-required, and alerted.
- The Audit Log Explorer shows supervisors 'entries for sellers in scope' INCLUDING before/after diffs. Before/after diffs of contact and opportunity records are the lead data itself — full cross-seller lead content through the audit backdoor. Audit rows visible to supervisors must redact PII-classified fields and show actor/action/entity/timestamp only.
- 'Supervisors may generate an evidence pack for sellers in scope if enabled' — an evidence pack is a complete contact dossier with recordings, transcripts, SMS bodies and the full timeline. That is the single most invasive artifact in the product. Admin-only, no toggle.
- Supervisor is defined as 'global read + COACH ACTIONS across assigned sellers' and coach actions are never enumerated. Unbounded, that becomes editing or reassigning another seller's opportunities. Enumerate them explicitly (view timeline, listen to a recording where the recording-consent policy permits, leave a coaching note visible to the seller, flag for review) and forbid all writes to the seller's own records.
- Impersonation is described as a READ-ONLY 'view as' session, but `user.impersonation_ended` carries `actions_taken_count`. That contradiction, shipped, becomes an admin writing into a seller's silo with ambiguous attribution — and a seller who sees a stage change they did not make. Resolve it: read-only and drop the counter, or permit writes and dual-stamp every single one in the seller's own visible history.
- Dedupe: 'ownership conflicts route to admin' is correct, but nothing specifies what the LOSING seller sees. If the message names the other seller, shows their activity, or reveals that the contact is being worked, the silo has leaked at exactly the moment sellers are most motivated to probe it. The seller-facing copy must be 'This contact is already in the system and has been sent to admin review' — no name, no owner, no dates.
- `permission_rule.scope` includes a `public` value but NO feature declares the leaderboard as public-scope. Without an explicit exemption, supervisor scope filtering will get applied to leaderboard queries by whoever implements the row filter, and the board silently shows each viewer only their own team — quietly destroying the one module the client called motivational, in the one place nobody will think to test.
- Inconsistent enforcement of the same rule: `import_batch` correctly carries `target_user_id` ('an import lands in exactly one seller's book'), while `lead_source` — the primary, high-volume, real-time intake path — carries no owner at all. The silo rule is enforced on the rare path and dropped on the main one.

## Risks & dependencies
- **Configuration is the highest-blast-radius surface in the product.** One edit to a stage's closed flag silently rewrites Earnings and re-ranks a leaderboard that 50 people look at every day. Mitigation: closed flags are org-locked, blast-radius preview before save, atomic publish, versioned config with one-click rollback, and a system note explaining rank changes. Without this discipline the leaderboard loses credibility, and a leaderboard nobody trusts is worse than no leaderboard.
- **Audit volume versus a sub-$100/month infrastructure budget.** 50 sellers generating call, SMS, stage and field events produce a large append-only table fast. Mitigation: batch-level audit for imports, lightweight audit for high-frequency comms events with the raw payload kept only briefly in webhook_event, tiered retention with cold storage for old entries, and no full before/after diff on unchanged fields.
- **True immutability is hard without a WORM store.** An append-only table is only as immutable as the database credentials. Mitigation: no application-level UPDATE/DELETE path, DB-level revocation for the app role, periodic hash-chaining of entries, and export with a hash manifest. Being honest with the client: this is tamper-evident, not tamper-proof, and full WORM archival is a later cost decision.
- **The silo model plus admin global export is the system's insider-exfiltration vector.** The entire product value is a proprietary book of business, and an admin can export all of it. Mitigation: reason required above a threshold, every export a first-class audited object, and same-hour anomaly alerts to all admins. Accepted residual risk: an admin can still exfiltrate — we make it loud, not impossible.
- **Aloware contract drift.** We are not a native partner; we depend on token-authenticated single-purpose APIs and outbound webhooks whose payloads can change without notice. Mitigation: signature validation, schema-tolerant parsing, dead-letter queue with replay, health monitor with a hard alert. Dependency: Aloware must reliably deliver call disposition, recording, transcription, AloAi summary and SMS events, including STOP.
- **Fail-closed suppression creates an availability dependency.** If the suppression lookup is down and we block sending, sellers are blocked; if we allow sending, we risk statutory penalties. Decision taken: fail closed and show explicit copy. Mitigation: a locally cached suppression bloom filter so the check survives a brief outage.
- **Regulatory drift.** TCPA one-to-one consent interpretation and state-level privacy laws keep moving; CCPA is only the first of several US state regimes. Mitigation: consent is modeled as evidence (source + disclosure text + certificate) rather than a boolean, and the privacy request console is request-type-driven so new rights are new rows, not new code.
- **CCPA identity verification paradox** — verifying a deletion requester may require collecting more personal data than we hold. Mitigation: a checklist-based verification with documented evidence, and explicit refusal-with-reason as a valid, audited outcome.
- **Deletion versus the permanent leaderboard.** A naive hard delete months after a sale would silently alter historical Earnings and demote a seller for reasons nobody can see. Decision: anonymize-and-retain by default, preserving de-identified monetary history. Needs client sign-off — it is a legal interpretation, not just a design choice.
- **Impersonation is a trust liability in a product whose selling point is privacy between sellers.** Mitigation: read-only, time-boxed, outbound comms blocked, persistent banner, dual-actor stamping, and visible in the impersonated user's own history. If the client objects, this feature is cuttable without breaking anything else.
- **i18n must be structural from the first screen.** Retrofitting Spanish across 40 screens of hardcoded en-US copy is a rewrite, and timezone handling is worse — mis-stamped call and appointment times corrupt speed-to-lead and no-show metrics for a nationally distributed team.
- **Dependency on module ownership boundaries.** The contact entity is owned by lead-intake but consent, suppression and deletion are owned here. If that boundary is not settled early, both modules will write to the same rows. Proposed rule: lead-intake owns contact identity and attributes; admin-audit owns consent, suppression, holds and deletion, and exposes a synchronous can_contact(identifier, channel) check that comms MUST call.
- **Deliberate CUTS (declared so Phase 3 does not re-add them):** (a) any lead-routing / round-robin / queue / supervisor-assignment configuration — it contradicts the per-seller silo architecture and the client explicitly rejected it; the only bulk reassignment is book transfer on deactivation; (b) a custom-role builder and a per-field per-user ACL matrix — three fixed tiers plus supervisor scope plus a PII flag covers every real case at 50 seats, and anything more is the 'brutal to administer' anti-pattern; (c) a generic blank-canvas condition/workflow builder in admin — automation stays a short plain-English trigger vocabulary owned by the automation module; (d) funnels, websites, membership sites, form builders — GHL agency bloat, zero value to a life insurance seller; (e) in-app billing, plans and usage metering in MVP — no second customer exists and usage-billing surprises are a named anti-pattern; (f) SIEM-grade forensic tooling — the audit log serves disputes and compliance evidence, not threat hunting.

## Open questions
- Deletion policy: does the client accept anonymize-and-retain as the default for CCPA deletions (preserving de-identified Earnings history and leaderboard integrity), with hard delete reserved for explicit demands? This is a legal interpretation and needs the client's counsel, not ours.
- Source of truth for opt-outs: Aloware maintains its own DNC/STOP suppression. Do we mirror it one-way (Aloware -> us) or push our manual do-not-contact entries back into Aloware? One-way leaves manual suppressions unenforced on Aloware-originated sends; two-way needs an API path we must confirm exists.
- Supervisor scope default: does a supervisor see all 50 sellers, or only an assigned subset? Affects every global list in every module and should be decided before the first roll-up view is built.
- How much pipeline personalization is genuinely wanted? Per-seller stage divergence makes supervisor roll-ups and leaderboard comparability fuzzy. Proposal: sellers may reorder and add personal intermediate stages but the closed stages and their flags are always shared — needs confirmation.
- Audit retention window and storage tier: how long must audit entries, call recordings and transcripts be kept? This is simultaneously a legal answer, a two-party-consent-state answer, and the dominant line item in the infrastructure budget.
- Entity ownership boundary between lead-intake (contact identity) and admin-audit (consent, suppression, holds, deletion) — confirm the synchronous can_contact() check as the enforcement contract before comms is built.
- Is admin impersonation acceptable to the client at all, given that the product is sold on the promise that sellers' books are private? If not, it is cuttable with no downstream breakage — but support becomes blind.
- Should sellers be allowed to export their own book? It is fair (it is their book of business) and it is also the departing-agent exfiltration path. Recommend allowing it with full audit and per-seat thresholds — needs an owner decision.
- Leaderboard fairness mitigation: research says revenue-only never-reset boards demote most of the team. The client chose all-time + real-time for v1. Which secondary recognition category ships (Most Improved, activity-based, streak) and who defines its formula?
- Multi-tenancy implementation: row-level security on a shared schema (cheap, fits the budget, riskier isolation) versus schema-per-tenant (stronger isolation, more operational overhead). No billing in MVP, but this choice is effectively permanent and should be made now.
- Do we need an admin-facing API/token management surface for the client's own integrations, or is Aloware plus lead vendors the whole integration universe for the foreseeable future? Building a general API key manager before anyone asks would be exactly the bloat we are avoiding.
- Verbal consent capture: when a seller gets consent on a recorded call, what constitutes storable evidence — a link to the Aloware recording plus a timestamped flag, or a transcript excerpt? Affects how much of the AloAi transcript we must retain and for how long.
