# 10. Reporting & Dashboards (Metrics Layer)

> **Module id:** `reporting` · part of [`docs/02-functional-map.md`](../02-functional-map.md)

## Verdict
RESHAPE — keep the module, but re-cut its boundary in two decisive ways. (1) Promote it from "a set of charts" to the tenant's **canonical metrics layer**: Reporting OWNS the append-only `earnings_ledger_entry` and the derived fact/rollup tables, is the ONLY writer of Earnings, and emits `earnings.updated`. The Leaderboard module becomes a *presentation consumer* of that same ledger. This is the only structural way to guarantee the brief's requirement that "Earnings math must be consistent with the leaderboard's" — one writer, many readers, no second implementation of the same SUM. (2) Reporting does NOT own "My Day": the Activity object belongs to the Activities/Calendar module; Reporting's seller home merely *composes* that list next to My Funnel and My Earnings. Everything shipped is a **named single-purpose report** (Attio/Pipedrive pattern) defined as config (`report_definition`), never a generic drag-and-drop BI builder — the builder is CUT outright as the exact "simple to use, brutal to administer" anti-pattern this product exists to avoid. Also CUT from scope: multi-touch attribution, commission/payout reporting (client deferred commission), client-facing/white-label reports (agency bloat), and per-user widget rearrangement. Non-negotiable design rule inherited from Phase 0: **every number on a seller's screen is scoped to that seller's silo, and the ONLY cross-seller figures a seller ever sees are the public leaderboard's Earnings and rank** — no aggregate on a seller screen may allow inferring another seller's book of business.

## Purpose & value
It answers the only two questions a seller asks all day — "who do I call right now?" and "how much have I earned?" — and the only three an owner asks — "who is producing, where are deals dying, and which lead vendor is worth paying for?" — using one shared, auditable Earnings number that can never disagree with the leaderboard.

## Screens & states (11)

### My Dashboard (seller home)
The screen a seller lands on after login: what to do now (My Day), how the book looks (My Funnel), what it earned (My Earnings), and current rank.

**Key actions:** `Call now on the top-priority lead` · `Open an activity from My Day` · `Drill into any funnel stage` · `Open My Earnings detail` · `Open the public leaderboard` · `Change the funnel date range (Earnings stays all-time)` · `Cmd+K`

- **Empty:** Headline: 'Your book is empty — let's fix that in 60 seconds.' Body: 'Add your first lead or import a list. As soon as a lead lands here, we'll start tracking your funnel, your response time, and your Earnings.' Primary button: 'Add lead'. Secondary: 'Import leads'. Below, a dimmed preview of the three panels labeled 'This is what you'll see.'
- **Loading:** Skeleton tiles matching final layout; last known values shown dimmed with an 'Updating…' chip on live counters; My Day list resolves first because it is the actionable panel.
- **Error:** Inline per-panel, never a blank page: 'We couldn't load your Earnings right now. Your deals are safe — this is a display problem. [Retry]'. Panels that did load stay usable.
- **No permission:** Not applicable to a seller's own dashboard. If the account has no active seller workspace: 'Your account isn't set up as a seller workspace yet. Ask your admin to activate it, and your board and numbers will appear here.'
- **Mobile:** Reordered to My Day → My Earnings + rank → at-risk count → funnel as a compact stacked list. Charts collapse to single-figure tiles; 'Call now' is a persistent bottom action.

### My Funnel
Where the seller's own deals are stuck, what they're worth, and what's about to go cold.

**Key actions:** `Drill through a stage to its opportunity list` · `Filter by source, product, date range` · `Sort stage list by value or days-since-touch` · `Jump to the kanban filtered to a stage`

- **Empty:** 'No opportunities in your pipeline yet. Move a lead onto your board and this funnel fills itself — no data entry required.' Primary: 'Open my board'.
- **Loading:** Stage bars render as gray placeholders at proportional widths; conversion and days-in-stage figures appear as shimmer until computed.
- **Error:** 'We couldn't build your funnel. This usually clears on retry. [Retry] — if it keeps happening, tell your admin the funnel report is failing.'
- **No permission:** Not reachable for other sellers' funnels; a seller who follows a link to another seller's funnel sees: 'This funnel belongs to another seller. You can only see your own book of business.'
- **Mobile:** Horizontal funnel becomes a vertical list of stages with count, value and a right-aligned at-risk badge.

### My Earnings
Proof of production: what the seller has earned all-time and what produced it.

**Key actions:** `View closed-won list behind the total` · `Break down by product (FE/IUL) and source` · `See month-to-date trend and personal best` · `Open a specific closed deal`

- **Empty:** 'No Earnings yet. Your first Closed-Won deal starts the counter. Earnings = the annual premium on every deal you move into a Closed-Won stage.' Primary: 'Open my board'.
- **Loading:** Total renders first from the live counter; breakdowns and trend fill in after; the total carries a LIVE indicator.
- **Error:** 'Your Earnings total is temporarily unavailable. Nothing has changed in your deals. [Retry]'
- **No permission:** Another seller's Earnings detail is never accessible to a seller: 'Only Earnings totals and rank are public. The deals behind them stay private to each seller.'
- **Mobile:** One large total, product split as two chips, trend as a sparkline; closed-deal list is infinite-scroll.

### Team Overview (supervisor/admin)
The owner's morning screen: production, pipeline, activity and the exceptions that need action today.

**Key actions:** `Filter by seller, source, product, date range` · `Open the exception rail items` · `Drill into any seller's coaching card` · `Open aggregate funnel or forecast` · `Export`

- **Empty:** 'No team activity to report yet. Once sellers start adding leads and logging calls, production, pipeline and exceptions appear here. Start by inviting your sellers.' Primary: 'Invite sellers'.
- **Loading:** Header KPI row loads first (live counters), then the exception rail, then charts; a freshness timestamp is visible from the first paint.
- **Error:** 'Some team metrics failed to load (last good data: {timestamp}). Showing what we have. [Retry]' — plus a persistent stale-data banner if the rollup job is failing.
- **No permission:** For a seller who reaches the URL: 'Team reporting is available to supervisors and admins. You can see your own dashboard and the public leaderboard.' with a link back to My Dashboard.
- **Mobile:** Exception rail first, KPI row second, charts last; comparison tables become swipeable seller cards.

### Report Library
Catalogue of named, single-purpose reports grouped by the question they answer — plus the viewer's saved views.

**Key actions:** `Search reports` · `Open a report` · `Save/rename/delete a view` · `Subscribe to a report` · `Pin a report to the dashboard`

- **Empty:** For a new tenant with no data: 'Reports are ready — they're just waiting for data. Each one answers one question, like "Which lead source actually closes?" Add leads and log a few calls, then come back.' Saved-views area: 'No saved views yet. Filter any report and choose Save view to keep it here.'
- **Loading:** Report cards render immediately from config with metric previews shimmering; the library itself never blocks on data.
- **Error:** 'We couldn't load your saved views. All standard reports are still available. [Retry]'
- **No permission:** Team-scope reports are listed but disabled for sellers with a lock affordance and the tooltip: 'Team report — visible to supervisors and admins.'
- **Mobile:** Single-column card list with the question as the card title.

### Named Report Detail (shared shell)
One report = one question. Standard shell: title as a question, filter bar, primary visual, data table, drill-through, export, definition link.

**Key actions:** `Apply filters (seller/source/product/stage/date)` · `Group by a supported dimension` · `Drill through any figure to records` · `Export CSV/PDF` · `Save view` · `Open 'How is this calculated'` · `Subscribe`

- **Empty:** Data-shaped, not generic: 'No results for these filters. Widest match found: {n} records with source = any. [Clear filters]' — and for genuinely empty datasets: 'Nothing to report yet. This report fills in as soon as {leads are created / calls are logged / deals are closed}.'
- **Loading:** Filter bar interactive immediately; chart and table skeletons; slow queries show 'Still crunching {n} records…' after 3 seconds with a cancel option.
- **Error:** 'This report failed to run. [Retry] · [Reset filters]. If it keeps failing, the report name and time are already logged for your admin.'
- **No permission:** 'You don't have access to this report. Team-wide reports are limited to supervisors and admins — your own version of this data is in My Funnel.' with a link.
- **Mobile:** Filters collapse into a bottom sheet; wide tables scroll horizontally inside their own container; charts degrade to top-N lists.

### Seller Drill-down / Coaching Card
Everything about one seller in one place, for supervisor coaching — the only place the silo is deliberately opened.

**Key actions:** `Switch seller` · `Change window (7/30/90 days)` · `Open a call recording, transcript or AI summary` · `Open a specific opportunity` · `Export the card` · `Set or adjust a goal`

- **Empty:** '{Seller} has no recorded activity in this window. Try a wider range, or check whether their dialer connection is active.' Secondary: 'Check integration status'.
- **Loading:** Identity and headline KPIs first, then activity series, then the recent-calls list (which depends on webhook data and can lag).
- **Error:** 'We couldn't load {Seller}'s full card. Activity metrics are shown; call recordings are unavailable right now. [Retry]'
- **No permission:** 'Seller drill-down is available to supervisors and admins. Sellers see only their own book of business.' Access by supervisors/admins is written to the audit log.
- **Mobile:** Vertical stack: KPI tiles → exceptions → recent calls; recording playback via native audio element.

### Forecast
Expected annual premium by period end: weighted, commit and best case, team and per seller.

**Key actions:** `Switch period` · `Toggle weighted/commit/best case` · `Adjust stage probabilities (admin)` · `Drill into the deals behind each band` · `Compare last period's forecast vs actual`

- **Empty:** 'Not enough closed history to forecast yet. We need deals to have moved through your stages at least once. In the meantime, set stage probabilities manually.' Primary: 'Configure stage probabilities'.
- **Loading:** Bands render sequentially (commit → weighted → best case) with the underlying deal counts appearing last.
- **Error:** 'Forecast couldn't be computed. Pipeline values are unaffected. [Retry]'
- **No permission:** 'Forecasting is a supervisor and admin report.' Sellers see their own weighted pipeline inside My Funnel instead, linked here.
- **Mobile:** Three stacked figures with delta vs last period; probability editing is desktop-only.

### Data Health & Compliance
The hygiene and TCPA/CCPA exposure list — everything that silently breaks the numbers or creates legal risk.

**Key actions:** `Open each issue group as a record list` · `Assign/notify the owning seller` · `Open the dedupe review queue` · `Export the consent-coverage list` · `Mark an issue reviewed`

- **Empty:** 'Everything checks out. No missing annual premiums, no duplicate candidates, no contacts missing consent. We re-check continuously and will flag anything here.'
- **Loading:** Issue groups appear as they are evaluated, each with its own count spinner, so a slow check never blocks the others.
- **Error:** 'One or more health checks didn't run ({check names}). The results below may be incomplete. [Re-run checks]'
- **No permission:** 'Data health and compliance reporting is limited to supervisors and admins.' Sellers get a personal subset — their own records with missing premium or consent — surfaced on their dashboard instead.
- **Mobile:** Issue groups as an accordion list with counts; record lists open full-screen.

### Metric Dictionary
The published definition of every number in the product, so numbers can be argued with facts instead of opinions.

**Key actions:** `Search a metric` · `Read the plain-English formula and source events` · `See which module owns it and its refresh cadence` · `Jump to the report that uses it`

- **Empty:** Never empty (definitions ship as config). If a tenant filters to nothing: 'No metrics match "{query}". Try "earnings", "conversion" or "speed to lead".'
- **Loading:** Static config, renders instantly; only usage examples load asynchronously.
- **Error:** 'Definitions couldn't be loaded. [Retry]'
- **No permission:** Open to all roles by design — a public leaderboard requires public math.
- **Mobile:** Searchable list with expandable definitions.

### Digests, Goals & Subscriptions settings
Control what gets delivered, when, and against what target.

**Key actions:** `Enable/disable the daily seller digest and set local send time` · `Subscribe to a named report at a cadence` · `Set a personal Earnings goal` · `Set team or per-seller goals (supervisor/admin)` · `Choose delivery channel (email/in-app)`

- **Empty:** 'No scheduled reports yet. Get your numbers without logging in — most sellers start with the 7:00 AM daily digest.' Primary: 'Turn on my daily digest'.
- **Loading:** Form skeleton; existing subscriptions list loads underneath.
- **Error:** 'We couldn't save that schedule. Your existing deliveries are unchanged. [Try again]'
- **No permission:** A seller attempting to set another seller's goal: 'You can set your own goal. Team and per-seller goals are set by supervisors and admins.'
- **Mobile:** Full-width form controls, native time picker, timezone shown explicitly next to the send time.

## Typical automations
- Every weekday at each seller's local 7:00 AM → send the daily digest: today's activities, overdue items, leads going cold in 48h, yesterday's Earnings, and current leaderboard rank.
- Every Monday at each supervisor's/admin's local 7:00 AM → send the weekly team scorecard: Earnings, closes, funnel movement, top and bottom performers, and open exceptions.
- When an opportunity moves into a stage flagged Closed-Won → append the Earnings credit, emit earnings.updated, refresh My Earnings and the leaderboard live, and fire the celebration.
- When an opportunity leaves a Closed-Won stage, is deleted, or its annual premium is edited after close → post a reversal plus corrected credit and emit earnings.updated so no consumer celebrates a reversal.
- When a seller has logged zero calls by 11:00 AM local on a working day → raise performance.anomaly_detected and notify their supervisor.
- When a seller's rolling 7-day first-touch median exceeds the speed-to-lead SLA → emit speed_to_lead.degraded and add a coaching item to the supervisor's exception rail.
- When a lead source's 30-day close rate falls below the configured fraction of its 90-day baseline → emit source.decay_detected and notify admins before the next lead invoice.
- When any opportunity within one stage of a Closed-Won stage has no annual premium → raise data_health.issue_detected and notify its owning seller (the stage-gating rule blocks the close, this catches it earlier).
- When a contact is missing a consent record or a vendor consent certificate → raise data_health.issue_detected on the TCPA compliance report.
- When team no-show rate over 14 days exceeds the configured threshold → surface on Team Overview and notify supervisors.
- When a rollup job fails → emit metrics.rollup_failed, notify admins, and display the stale-data banner on every affected report until it succeeds.
- At the close of each day, week and month → seal a period_snapshot per seller (Earnings, counters, rank) so a future period-based leaderboard needs no redesign or backfill.
- Every Monday → recompute Most Improved and emit seller.improvement_computed for the leaderboard's secondary recognition category.
- Nightly → recompute the forecast snapshot and emit forecast.updated; when a period ends, write actual_value_usd back onto the snapshot to score accuracy.
- When an active goal's projected finish falls short of target beyond tolerance → emit goal.pace_at_risk to the owner and their supervisor; when it is reached → emit goal.achieved and celebrate.
- When pipeline stage configuration changes → version the definition, mark affected rollups for recompute, and annotate reports with the date the definition changed.
- When any report is exported → write report.exported to the audit log with row count and PII flag.

## KPIs

## Insurance specifics (Life / FE / IUL) & vertical-agnostic core
Genuinely Life/FE/IUL-specific in this module: (1) The money unit is ANNUAL PREMIUM, not invoice revenue — so 'Earnings' is a forward-looking production figure and the ledger must survive premium edits after close via reversals. (2) FE and IUL are two different businesses wearing one pipeline: FE is a low-premium, minutes-to-hours, phone-closed sale off ping-post leads; IUL is a higher-premium, multi-week, underwriting-heavy sale. Every velocity, close-rate and average-premium metric is therefore segmented by product_type by default, because a blended average describes no real deal. (3) Speed-to-lead is measured in SECONDS AND MINUTES, not days — purchased FE leads decay within minutes, so first-touch latency is a first-class KPI with its own SLA, breach list and coaching loop; no generic CRM treats response time this way. (4) The post-sale Life funnel is real and reportable: application submitted → approved → issued/placed → not-taken/declined/withdrawn, because a Closed-Won that never gets issued is not production. (5) TCPA and DNC are reporting objects, not just guardrails: consent-certificate coverage per lead vendor, opt-out (STOP) rates by source and seller, and attempts against non-consented contacts are standing reports, because the exposure is per-attempt and the evidence must exist before a complaint arrives. (6) Loss reasons are life-shaped (doesn't qualify on health/age, already insured, no funds, affordability), which makes loss analysis a lead-vendor quality signal rather than a sales-skill signal. HOW THE CORE STAYS VERTICAL-AGNOSTIC: none of the above is hardcoded. The engine computes over generic primitives — stage flags (is_closed / is_won / is_lost), a numeric deal_value with a configurable label, a configurable product/category field on the opportunity, a source dimension, activity and communication event types, and typified loss-reason codes. 'Annual premium' is a display label on deal_value; 'FE vs IUL' is values in a configurable product field; the underwriting funnel is an optional post-close status dimension that a tenant either populates or leaves empty. Named reports are rows in report_definition, and metric semantics live in metric_definition — so adding Medicare, P&C or a non-insurance vertical means new configuration and possibly new report definitions, not new query code. The only vertical-specific concession is the choice of DEFAULT report set and DEFAULT thresholds shipped for a Life tenant.

## ⚔️ Adversarial review (sales-ops critic)

**Verdict:** NEEDS WORK — the ledger-as-single-writer spine, silo-at-query-build, speed-to-lead in seconds and the loss-reason/TCPA reporting are genuinely right, but the module credits Earnings on SUBMITTED premium with placement deferred and no carrier, lead-cost, persistency, roster or licensing dimension, so the public all-time leaderboard becomes gameable on business that never issues — and a forecast/goals/report-library layer of pure CRM boilerplate has to come out to pay for fixing it.

### Must fix — missing for real operation (15)
- PLACEMENT / ISSUED PREMIUM IS ABSENT FROM MVP — this is the single biggest defect. Earnings = annual premium the moment a card lands in Closed-Won, with the application/placement funnel deferred to non-MVP and explicitly 'CUT rather than faked'. In Life, a written app that is declined, not-taken, or never drafted is worth zero. A public, all-time, never-reset leaderboard ranked on SUBMITTED premium is a gaming machine: the floor learns within a week to write anything with a pulse, ranks high, and the owner pays lead spend and advances on production that never places. The ledger needs is_placed / policy_status and the leaderboard needs at minimum a 'written vs placed' toggle from day one, even if placement status arrives via manual entry.
- NO CARRIER DIMENSION ANYWHERE. Not in earnings_ledger_entry, not in stage_transition_fact, not in metric_rollup_daily. FE agents rotate Mutual of Omaha / Americo / Foresters / SBLI / Gerber by health condition and age; IUL producers pick by illustration. 'Which carrier declines my clients', 'which carrier issues in 3 days vs 6 weeks', 'which carrier has my worst not-taken rate' are daily questions and none of them are answerable. Add carrier_id to the ledger and all fact grains.
- PERSISTENCY / EARLY LAPSE AND CHARGEBACK ARE UNMENTIONED. Commission is post-MVP, fine — but persistency is not a commission concept, it is the number that decides whether an agency survives. A seller whose book lapses at month 4 is a liability ranked as a hero. At minimum: policy draft date, first-draft success, and a 3/6/9-month persistency figure per seller and per lead source.
- LEAD COST IS 'AN OPTIONAL cost_per_lead FIELD WHEN THE TENANT SUPPLIES IT'. For a US FE agency, cost per acquisition and premium-per-lead-dollar are THE reason the owner buys software. It also cannot be a static per-source constant: cost varies per order and per lead type (real-time/live-transfer vs aged vs direct mail vs Facebook), so cost must live on a lead batch/purchase record and roll up. As specified, the per-vendor report can show close rate but not whether the vendor is profitable.
- NO lead_type DIMENSION (real-time ping-post vs live transfer vs aged vs inbound vs referral vs recycled). Speed-to-lead SLA on a 90-day aged lead is meaningless, and blending aged into a source's median first-touch corrupts the single metric the whole speed thesis rests on. source_id alone does not carry this.
- THE SPEED-TO-LEAD CLOCK STARTS AT lead.created (our row insert) INSTEAD OF THE VENDOR'S POST TIMESTAMP. Any latency in ping-post intake, webhook queueing, or retry is therefore invisible and the seller absorbs the blame. Store vendor_posted_at and measure posted_at → first outbound touch, with our own intake latency broken out as a separate system metric.
- NO DIAL-ATTEMPT DEPTH REPORTING. The floor lever in FE is not only the first touch — it is 6-8 attempts across the first 72 hours at varied hours. There is calls_dialed and connect rate, but no attempts-per-lead distribution, no 'leads in your book with fewer than 3 attempts', no list penetration ('you worked 40% of your own book this week'). Because there is no routing and no queue, list penetration against the seller's OWN book is the only meaningful workload metric and it is missing.
- NO WORKING-ROSTER / PTO / SHIFT SOURCE, yet the spec fires 'zero calls by 11:00 AM on a working day' and silent-seller anomalies. Across ~50 sellers there is always someone on vacation, in a licensing exam, or newly hired and not yet appointed. Without a roster the exception rail cries wolf on day one and supervisors mute it in week two — which kills the entire Team Overview value proposition.
- NO STATE LICENSING / CARRIER APPOINTMENT AWARENESS. A seller can only write in states where they hold a license and with carriers where they are appointed. 'Leads received in states where the owning seller is not licensed' is pure wasted lead spend and should be a first-class data-health issue with a dollar figure attached. Close-rate-by-state without licensing context actively misleads.
- NO ALOWARE WEBHOOK RECONCILIATION. The entire activity scorecard, connect rate, talk time and the silent-seller alert are built on webhooks with no gap detection and no periodic reconciliation against Aloware's API. A dropped webhook batch means a producing seller is reported as silent and coached on fiction, and the ledger side would be equally blind. data_health covers 'calls not linked to a contact' but not 'call event stream gap between 14:00 and 16:00'.
- ALOWARE SEQUENCE ACTIVITY IS INVISIBLE. sequence enroll/disenroll is a contracted API and a legitimate way to work leads, but no sequence event is consumed and no sequence touch counts as activity. A seller working leads via sequences shows as inactive.
- NO 'DISTANCE TO NEXT RANK' ON THE SELLER DASHBOARD. Showing current rank without the gap ('$1,430 behind #4') wastes the one motivational mechanic that actually moves behavior on an all-time board where most sellers can never reach #1.
- NO MODEL FOR MULTI-POLICY SALES. An FE appointment routinely writes husband + wife, or a policy plus a child rider. One opportunity carrying one deal_value forces either one opportunity per policy (which destroys funnel counts and appointment math) or a blended value (which destroys product-mix, carrier and placement reporting). The ledger needs policy-level rows under an opportunity.
- NO CALL-RECORDING CONSENT REPORTING BY STATE. The coaching card serves recordings, transcripts and AloAi summaries across a national floor including all-party-consent states (CA, WA, FL, PA...). TCPA and CCPA are covered; recording consent is not, and it is the exposure most likely to surface in an employment or consumer dispute.
- NO RETENTION / DATA-SUBJECT PATH IN THE FACT TABLES. CCPA deletion requests must reach metric_rollup_daily, stage_transition_fact and the append-only ledger. 'Earnings history is never deleted' (user.deactivated reaction) and CCPA erasure are on a collision course and the spec never resolves it.

### Too generic / boilerplate (11)
- Forecast (weighted / commit / best case + stage win-probabilities + forecast_snapshot + forecast.updated + a whole Forecast screen) is lifted straight from B2B SaaS pipeline management. An FE deal off a ping-post lead is quoted and closed in the same call — there is no 'commit vs best case' band and no multi-week negotiation to weight. The only forecast that means anything in Life is EXPECTED ISSUED PREMIUM: submitted applications sitting in underwriting × historical placement rate by carrier and product, plus the draft dates they will hit. Replace weighted-pipeline forecasting with an underwriting-pipeline projection, or cut it.
- 'Cohort report by lead week' is growth-analytics boilerplate. On a floor where leads decay in MINUTES and FE closes in days, a weekly cohort grain is meaningless and the interesting cohort is not the week — it is the lead ORDER/BATCH from a specific vendor at a specific price. Cut it or re-cut it as 'lead batch performance' keyed to the purchase order.
- The whole goals sub-system (goal entity, personal + team targets, pace-to-target, projected finish, goal.pace_at_risk, goal.achieved, a settings screen) is generic CRM gamification bolted on. This product already bought a public all-time leaderboard as its motivational engine; adding a second parallel motivation system in the reporting module before the first has shipped is scope creep, not a feature.
- 'Anomaly / silent-seller alerts' defined as 'deviates materially from its own baseline' with 'anomaly sensitivity' as a config knob is statistical hand-waving that could appear in any product. A telesales floor needs three hard rules, not a detector: zero dials by 11:00 local, no first-touch on a fresh lead within N minutes, no Closed-Won in 14 days. Anything fancier produces noise nobody reads.
- The Named Report Library + saved_view + shareable presets + subscriptions to ANY named report + pin-to-dashboard + admin enable/disable of reports per tenant is a small BI product re-entering through the back door — precisely the 'simple to use, brutal to administer' anti-pattern the verdict claims to have cut. One seller home, one team overview, one report list, two digests. Everything else is administration burden for a 50-seller single-agency tenant.
- Metric Dictionary as its own searchable screen with usage examples is over-built. The defensible thing is metric_definition config powering an inline 'How is this calculated' popover on the tile; a destination screen nobody navigates to is documentation theatre.
- 'Digest engagement: open rate and click-through' as a product KPI is email-marketing boilerplate — and it directly contradicts the spec's own decision that email open/click analytics are out of scope. Pick one.
- 'Universal drill-through', 'freshness banner', 'timezone-correct today', 'export with audit trail' are correct but they are platform hygiene rules, not features with a business case. Stating them as line-item features inflates the module and hides the fact that only about eight things here are actually FE/IUL-shaped.
- PDF export of dashboards is generic enterprise garnish. CSV serves the owner rebuilding numbers in Excel; PDF rendering is infra cost and a maintenance surface for zero decision made differently.
- 'Command-palette report access' is owned by the palette module, not Reporting. Declaring it here creates duplicate ownership of the same behavior across module specs.
- Product-mix (FE vs IUL) is marked mvp_candidate:false while insurance_specifics claims 'every velocity, close-rate and average-premium metric is segmented by product_type BY DEFAULT'. Either segmentation is a default of the fact tables (in which case product-mix is free and is MVP) or the vertical-specific claim is decoration. As written, the spec contradicts itself.

### Bloat to cut (13)
- Forecast feature + Forecast screen + forecast_snapshot entity + forecast.updated event + admin stage-probability configuration + 'Forecast accuracy' KPI. Nothing in an FE cycle breaks without it, and IUL volume at launch is too thin to fit probabilities to. Replace later with underwriting-pipeline expected-issued premium.
- Cohort report by lead week — the spec itself calls it the first candidate for deferral. Make it a hard cut, not a deferral; a shipped module should not carry a feature its own author ranks last.
- Entire goals sub-system: goal entity, goal.pace_at_risk, goal.achieved, pace/projection UI, and the goals half of the Digests/Goals/Subscriptions screen. Nothing breaks: the leaderboard, Most Improved and the daily digest already provide the horizon.
- funnel_stage_snapshot as a nightly per-seller × per-stage table. 'Was Quoted always this clogged?' is not a question a 50-seller FE floor asks in year one, and the row count is a real cost against the sub-$100/month envelope. Derive stage occupancy history from stage_transition_fact if it is ever asked for.
- Metric Dictionary as a standalone searchable screen with usage examples. Keep metric_definition as config and the inline 'How is this calculated' popover; cut the destination screen and its four key_actions.
- saved_view sharing to roles, subscriptions to arbitrary named reports, and pin-a-report-to-dashboard. Ship exactly two deliveries (seller daily digest, supervisor weekly scorecard) and personal filter memory. Everything else is administration surface for a single-agency tenant.
- report_definition.is_enabled — per-tenant enabling/disabling of individual reports, plus the admin permission row that guards it. One agency, ~50 sellers; nobody will ever curate this.
- PDF dashboard export. CSV covers the owner's Excel workflow; PDF is rendering infrastructure and layout maintenance for no additional decision.
- 'Source-decay alert' as a separate feature with its own event. Fold it into thresholds on the per-source report; a standalone decay engine before there are 90 days of baseline data is unusable anyway.
- Command-palette report access — delete from this module and let the palette module own it.
- 'Digest engagement: open rate and click-through' KPI, and 'Time from login to first action' KPI. Both are product-analytics instrumentation, not module KPIs, and the second contradicts the stated out-of-scope stance on open/click tracking.
- 'Team no-show rate over 14 days exceeds threshold' automation. The per-source and per-seller no-show numbers on the weekly scorecard already surface this; a dedicated alert adds another mutable notification for a slow-moving metric.
- The 'no_permission_state' copy for My Dashboard ('If the account has no active seller workspace...') describes a provisioning state that should not exist — sellers are created as sellers. Cut the state rather than designing UI for an impossible account shape.

### Event gaps (15)
- NO policy.status_changed (or application.status_changed) IS CONSUMED, yet the Application & Placement Leakage report is declared to 'depend on post-issue policy status being captured'. The dependency is asserted and never wired — no producing module is named, no event shape is proposed, and nothing in the ledger reacts to an issued or declined policy. This is the module's central unwired dependency, and because Earnings is credited at Closed-Won it means the canonical money number can never be corrected when the policy does not place.
- NO call.recording_ready / call.disposition_set CONSUMED. Only call.completed and call.summary_ready are handled, but Aloware delivers disposition, recording URL and transcript as separate, later webhooks. The coaching card promises 'links to recent call recordings, transcripts and AloAi summaries' with no event that ever supplies a recording reference, and connect-rate derived from call.completed alone will be wrong whenever disposition arrives after the fact.
- NO call-channel DNC / suppression EVENT CONSUMED, yet the TCPA report claims to show 'DNC suppression hits'. Only sms.opted_out is consumed. Add consumption of dnc.suppressed / contact.opted_out (voice) or the metric is fabricated.
- NO PER-LEAD REAL-TIME BREACH EVENT. speed_to_lead.degraded fires off a rolling 7-day median — that is a post-mortem. On a ping-post floor the moment that matters is 'this specific fresh lead has been sitting untouched for 5 minutes'. Emit speed_to_lead.lead_untouched (lead_id, seller, source, age_seconds) so the seller — not the supervisor, next week — gets the nudge while the lead is still alive.
- earnings.updated CARRIES earnings_total_all_time_usd AND policies_closed_all_time IN THE PAYLOAD. Running totals inside an event are order-dependent: two concurrent closes, a retry, or an out-of-order delivery makes the public leaderboard show a stale total, and the leaderboard's whole legitimacy is that it never disagrees. Ship a monotonic ledger_sequence / version in the payload and make consumers read the total, or drop the totals from the event entirely.
- NO ledger.recomputed / earnings.backfilled EVENT. Admins can 'trigger rollup recompute / backfill' per the permissions table, but nothing tells the leaderboard, notifications or celebration consumers that history moved underneath them. After a backfill the public board silently disagrees with the ledger — the exact Sev-1 the module defines for itself.
- THE idempotency_key = (opportunity_id + closed_stage_id + closed_at) IS BROKEN IN BOTH DIRECTIONS. If closed_at is date-granular, a legitimate reopen-and-reclose on the same day is silently dropped as a duplicate; if it is timestamp-granular, a double-fire of the same drag creates two credits whenever the timestamps differ by a millisecond. Reversal rows are given no idempotency key at all, so a replayed reversal double-debits. Key on the transition event id, not on business fields.
- meeting.* vs appointment.* NAMING IS INCONSISTENT WITH THE REST OF THE PRODUCT. This module consumes meeting.scheduled / completed / no_show / canceled while the Phase-1 commitments and automations talk about 'appointment status changed' and no-show follow-up. Two names for one object guarantees a missed subscription. Standardize on appointment.*.
- NO appointment.reminder_sent CONSUMED, yet the no-show report promises 'recovery rate after no-show follow-up' and the KPI set implies reminders can be evaluated. Without the reminder event you cannot attribute any change in no-show rate to the reminders.
- data_health.issue_detected HAS AN AMBIGUOUS SHAPE: it carries entity_type + entity_id AND count + sample_entity_ids. One is a single-issue event, the other is a batch digest. Consumers cannot be written against both. Split into data_health.issue_detected (single) and data_health.scan_completed (batch summary).
- NO lead.import_completed / lead_batch.received CONSUMED. Bulk imports and ping-post batches are how leads actually arrive; without a batch event, per-order lead cost, dedupe backlog spikes and source attribution have no anchor.
- lead.merged's REACTION IS UNDER-SPECIFIED AND DANGEROUS: 'Re-points facts and any ledger entries from the losing record to the surviving record'. Re-pointing contradicts append-only — a merge must post a reversal under the losing seller and a credit under the surviving one. And the spec never acknowledges that in ping-post the same consumer is routinely sold to two sellers, so a merge can MOVE EARNINGS BETWEEN SELLERS AND RE-RANK THE PUBLIC LEADERBOARD. That path needs an explicit rule, an admin-only surface, and its own event (earnings.reattributed).
- NO sms.delivery_failed / sms.blocked CONSUMED. A2P 10DLC carrier filtering silently eats messages; sms_sent counts optimistically and the activity scorecard overstates outreach.
- NO user.reactivated (only user.deactivated) AND NO pipeline.stage_deleted. Both leave frozen counters and orphaned stage_ids with no defined recovery path.
- goal.pace_at_risk IS ROUTED 'to the owner AND THEIR SUPERVISOR' EVEN FOR SELF-SET PERSONAL GOALS. A private target becoming a supervisor notification is a behavior change nobody agreed to; sellers stop setting goals. Split personal-goal events from supervisor-assigned-goal events.

### 🚨 Silo violations (9)
- Loss-reason analysis is declared 'available to sellers for their own book' AND 'sliceable by source, product AND SELLER' in the same sentence. A seller-visible seller dimension is a direct silo breach. Strike 'seller' from the seller-scoped variant.
- Most Improved is computed here and published to the public leaderboard carrying baseline_value, current_value and delta_pct in seller.improvement_computed. Publishing another seller's baseline and percentage change lets anyone reconstruct that seller's book trajectory — far more than the sanctioned 'Earnings total and rank'. Publish name + improvement RANK only; keep the deltas internal to supervisor scope.
- period_snapshot.improvement_delta_pct and policies_closed are written per seller and the leaderboard is a declared consumer of period.snapshot_created (which also carries total_earnings_usd and top_seller_user_id). Policy count is not a sanctioned public figure: policies_closed plus public Earnings reveals another seller's average premium, i.e. what kind of business they write. Define exactly which snapshot fields are public and enforce it at the event boundary, not in the leaderboard UI.
- My Funnel and the seller's Speed-to-Lead scorecard are 'split by source'. Combined with any team-level source figure a seller ever sees (the digest, a shared view, a celebration message), per-source seller metrics are a differencing attack in a 50-seller tenant. Rule needed: no seller screen may show any figure whose denominator includes another seller's records — and today nothing in the spec states it at the query level, only report_definition.scope_mode which governs record scope, not aggregate scope.
- saved_view.shared_with_role lets a supervisor share a view to the seller role. If that view's report is team-scope, a seller inherits a team filter set; if scope_mode blocks it, the seller gets a broken artifact. Sharing a view to the seller role is a silo hazard with no upside — remove it.
- The Seller Drill-down / Coaching Card is correctly role-gated and audited, but 'Switch seller' plus 'Export the card' plus a supervisor account is one compromised login away from exfiltrating every seller's book. Access is logged; it is not rate-limited, not alerted on, and export of a coaching card is not distinguished from a normal report export in report_export_audit.
- 'Speed-to-lead compliance report (team)' is marked mvp_candidate:true while the seller-facing coaching loop it feeds is described as flowing through the supervisor. Correct on permissions, but the automations then push breach coaching items to supervisors without ever notifying the seller who could still save the lead — operationally this converts a silo-respecting real-time lever into cross-seller surveillance with no seller-side remedy.
- Permissions say supervisors may view their own dashboard 'if they carry a book' — the player-coach case is real on FE floors and completely unresolved. Does a producing supervisor's Earnings appear on the PUBLIC leaderboard alongside the sellers they coach and whose books they can read? Either answer has consequences and the spec picks neither.
- The drill-through rule 'a filter resolving outside own scope returns empty and IS LOGGED' will log ordinary navigation (a stale bookmark, a shared link) as an attempted breach, and the Silo-integrity KPI ('0 successful cross-seller accesses, verified continuously against the audit log') will drown in false positives — making the one control that proves the silo works unreadable.

## Risks & dependencies
- OWNERSHIP CONFLICT (highest risk): the Leaderboard module will likely also claim the Earnings calculation. There must be exactly ONE writer. This spec asserts Reporting owns earnings_ledger_entry and emits earnings.updated, with Leaderboard as a read-only consumer. If Phase 3 assigns it the other way, this module must be rewritten to consume rather than emit earnings.updated — this must be resolved before either module is built.
- Double-counting on reopen/re-win/merge/value-edit is the classic failure mode of any earnings ledger. Mitigated by append-only rows, idempotency keys, and compensating reversals — but it demands explicit test scenarios for: win → reopen → re-win, merge of two records where one is won, premium edited after close, and webhook replay.
- Silo leakage through aggregates: any team-shaped number rendered on a seller screen (team averages, 'top source overall', benchmarks) can leak another seller's book. Rule: sellers see ONLY their own data plus the public leaderboard's Earnings and rank. Comparison against 'team average' is deliberately excluded from seller views until legally and culturally cleared.
- Timezone correctness across ~50 US sellers is a silent-corruption risk. Facts stored UTC, bucketed per-user local day, and every team report must state which timezone its day boundary uses. Fiscal week start is an unresolved tenant setting.
- Aloware webhook fidelity is a hard dependency: activity metrics, connect rates and speed-to-lead are only as true as the disposition/recording/summary webhooks. Missing or delayed webhooks silently undercount. Requires a reconciliation job against the Aloware API and an explicit 'activity data may be incomplete' state — never a plausible-looking wrong number.
- Pipeline reconfiguration (stage renamed, reordered, deleted, or re-flagged as closed) can retroactively rewrite historical conversion and velocity reports. Requires stable stage_ids, versioned stage configuration, denormalized labels on facts, and a visible annotation on reports covering the change date.
- Real-time everywhere would blow the sub-$100/month infra target. Deliberate hybrid: live push only for Earnings, rank and today's counters; everything historical served from rollups. If a stakeholder demands live for all reports, cost re-opens.
- Forecast credibility risk: stage win-probabilities are configured, not learned, until enough closed history exists. Shipping a confident-looking forecast on invented probabilities damages trust in every other number. It must be labeled as configuration-based and its accuracy tracked publicly from day one.
- The application/placement leakage report depends on post-issue policy status being captured somewhere in the system. If no module owns policy status, the report is CUT rather than approximated.
- Cost-per-acquisition and true source ROI depend on a cost_per_lead input the client has not confirmed exists. Without it, source performance ships as conversion-and-premium only, and CPA is CUT.
- CCPA: reports and exports carry PII. Requires the export audit log, no PII in URLs or query strings, retention limits on rollups and snapshots, and a defined behavior for deletion requests (facts must be de-identified without corrupting historical aggregates — deletion vs anonymization is unresolved).
- Deactivated or departed sellers: their Earnings history must persist for reporting and audit while disappearing from active denominators and, per policy, from the live leaderboard. The policy is not yet decided and affects both modules.
- EXPLICIT CUTS, recorded here so they are not re-litigated later: generic drag-and-drop BI/report builder (administration burden, blank-canvas anti-pattern); multi-touch attribution (single-source model is sufficient at this volume); commission and payout reporting (client deferred commission to post-MVP); client-facing/white-label report portals (agency bloat); per-user dashboard widget rearrangement (maintenance cost with no seller value); deep email open/click analytics (we are not an ESP); predictive lead scoring (no training data at launch and it would quietly conflict with the silo model).

## Open questions
- Who owns the Earnings ledger — Reporting (this spec's position: single writer, emits earnings.updated) or Leaderboard? Must be decided before either module is built; a duplicated SUM is the fastest way to destroy trust in the leaderboard.
- Does the tenant have cost-per-lead data per vendor? Without it, source ROI/CPA is cut and source performance ships as conversion + premium only.
- Is post-issue policy status (submitted / approved / issued / placed / not-taken / declined) captured anywhere? Without an owner for that field, the application-placement leakage report cannot exist.
- Who sets seller goals — the seller themselves, the supervisor, or admin only? This changes both the permission matrix and the motivational dynamic of an all-time leaderboard.
- Is forecasting actually wanted by the client, or is it demo candy? It is the highest-complexity, lowest-confidence feature in the module at launch.
- Should supervisors drill through aggregates into a seller's actual lead and contact records (PII), or only into de-identified counts? This spec assumes full drill-through with audit logging, but it is the one place the silo is deliberately opened and it needs explicit client sign-off.
- Fiscal calendar: does the week start Sunday or Monday, and does the month follow the calendar month? Period keys for snapshots, goals and any future leaderboard reset all depend on this.
- Retention: how long are daily rollups, snapshots and stage-transition facts kept? Affects both CCPA posture and infra cost.
- What happens to a departed seller's Earnings on the public all-time leaderboard — retained, archived, or hidden? Unresolved and jointly owned with the Leaderboard module.
- Are seller-visible team benchmarks (e.g. 'your close rate vs team average') acceptable, or does that violate the spirit of the silo? Currently excluded by default.
- Should the kiosk/TV view include an aggregate funnel or team-goal thermometer alongside the leaderboard podium, or does the TV belong entirely to the Leaderboard module? Assumed the latter.
- Should the daily seller digest go by SMS as well as email? It is outbound messaging to employees, not leads, so TCPA does not apply, but channel cost and preference are unconfirmed.
