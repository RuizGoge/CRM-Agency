# 1. Lead Intake & Ownership Origin

> **Module id:** `lead-intake` · part of [`docs/02-functional-map.md`](../02-functional-map.md)

## Verdict
KEEP — but RESHAPE the mandate. In a system with per-seller silos and NO routing engine, "intake" is not a form-builder module: it is the **ownership-binding + consent-provenance layer**. Every lead must acquire (a) exactly one owner_user_id, (b) a consent record, and (c) a source attribution, at the moment of birth — because there is no downstream engine to fix any of the three later. So the module is reshaped around one question: *how does a lead land in ONE silo deterministically?* Answer: ownership is a property of the SOURCE, resolved by one of five deterministic binding modes (endpoint token, payload field map, importer identity, creator identity, inbound Aloware number), never by an algorithm. Everything that does not serve speed-to-lead, ownership determinism, dedupe, or TCPA provenance is CUT from this module (see open_questions for the cut list). Do NOT merge into lead-management: intake is write-once, machine-facing, security-sensitive (public webhook endpoints, tokens, PII payload logs) and has a completely different failure mode (a lost payload = a lost purchased lead = real money), while lead-management is human-facing CRUD over an already-owned record.

## Purpose & value
It gets every new lead — typed by hand, posted by a lead vendor in milliseconds, imported from a CSV, or arriving as an unknown inbound call — into the right seller's own book, deduplicated, with its TCPA consent certificate attached, and callable with one tap before it goes cold.

## Screens & states (9)

### Fresh Leads (speed-to-lead strip)
The first thing a seller sees: newly arrived, never-touched leads in their own silo, ordered by age, each with a one-tap call.

**Key actions:** `Call now (one tap, Two-Legged Call via Aloware)` · `Send first SMS (disabled with reason when consent absent)` · `Open lead` · `Dismiss to pipeline (mark as worked)` · `Filter by source`

- **Empty:** No fresh leads right now. New leads from your vendors, forms, and imports land here the second they arrive — and you can call them with one tap. Add one yourself with Quick Add, or connect a lead source to start receiving automatically. [Quick Add Lead] [Set up a lead source]
- **Loading:** Skeleton cards preserving the strip height so the layout does not jump; the live age timers start only once real data is bound. If the realtime channel is reconnecting, a thin 'Reconnecting — leads may appear with a delay' bar sits above the strip.
- **Error:** 'We could not load your fresh leads. Your leads are safe — this is a display problem. [Retry]' If realtime is down but fetch works: 'Live updates are off. New leads will appear when you refresh. [Refresh]'
- **No permission:** Not applicable to sellers (always own-scope). Supervisors/admins opening another seller's Fresh Leads see a read-only banner: 'Viewing {seller}'s fresh leads. Calling and texting are disabled in oversight mode.'
- **Mobile:** This screen IS the mobile home. Full-width cards, thumb-reachable primary 'Call now' button, native push notification with a Call action that deep-links straight into the dial. Must be usable one-handed and survive spotty coverage (optimistic UI + queued action).

### Quick Add Lead (modal / mobile sheet)
Capture a referral or callback in under 15 seconds with live duplicate detection.

**Key actions:** `Paste-anything parse (phone, name, email from clipboard)` · `Live duplicate check on phone/email blur` · `Pick product interest and source` · `Save & Call` · `Save only`

- **Empty:** The form itself is the empty state. Helper text under the phone field: 'Paste a phone number — we will format it and check for duplicates as you type.' Under source: 'Where did this lead come from? This is how you find out which sources are worth paying for.'
- **Loading:** The duplicate check shows an inline spinner chip next to the phone field ('Checking…'); Save stays enabled — we never block typing on a lookup. Save shows a button-level spinner and disables double-submit.
- **Error:** Field-level validation inline ('Enter a 10-digit US phone number'). On save failure: 'We could not save this lead. Your input is still here — [Try again]. If this keeps happening, your entry is saved as a draft on this device.'
- **No permission:** Not reachable without lead-create permission; the Quick Add button and its Cmd+K command are hidden, not disabled.
- **Mobile:** Bottom sheet, numeric keypad by default on the phone field, sticky primary action bar above the keyboard, and the duplicate warning rendered as a colored inline row rather than a modal so it never blocks the flow.

### My Lead Sources
Per-seller self-service: see and manage the doors leads come through — webhook endpoint, hosted form, imports.

**Key actions:** `Copy my post URL / token` · `Send a test ping and inspect the received payload` · `Copy the sample payload to email my vendor` · `Rotate token` · `Enable/disable a source` · `Open the hosted form / copy embed code` · `View last received and 24h volume`

- **Empty:** You have no lead sources connected yet. Right now leads only arrive when you add them by hand. Connect a source and they arrive on their own — instantly. 'My vendor posts leads' [Create a post URL] · 'I run my own ads' [Create a web form] · 'I have a spreadsheet' [Import a CSV]
- **Loading:** Row skeletons for each source card; the health metrics (last received, 24h volume) load in a second pass and show '—' until resolved.
- **Error:** 'We could not load your sources. Existing sources keep receiving leads even while this page is broken. [Retry]' Test-ping failure shows the raw HTTP status and body returned so it can be forwarded to the vendor.
- **No permission:** A seller never sees another seller's sources. Supervisor view is read-only with tokens masked: 'Tokens are hidden. Only the source owner or an admin can reveal or rotate a token.'
- **Mobile:** Copy-to-clipboard buttons must be large and confirm with a toast; the payload inspector collapses to a scrollable code block with horizontal scroll contained. Token rotation requires a confirm step on mobile to prevent fat-finger revocation.

### CSV Import Wizard
Get a spreadsheet into the book without creating duplicates or importing non-consented contacts.

**Key actions:** `Upload file` · `Map columns (save/reuse mapping template)` · `Choose duplicate policy (skip / update existing / create anyway)` · `Attest consent basis` · `Run dry run` · `Commit import` · `Download error report`

- **Empty:** Step 1 with a drop zone: 'Drop your CSV here, or browse. First time? Download our template so the columns line up. Every row you import becomes a lead in YOUR book — nobody else sees it.'
- **Loading:** Parsing shows row-count-so-far; the dry run shows a determinate progress bar with the four running counters (new / duplicate / cross-owner / invalid); commit shows rows-committed with a Cancel that stops cleanly and reports what was already created.
- **Error:** Unparseable file: 'We could not read this file. It should be a .csv with a header row (max {n} MB). [Try another file]'. Partial commit failure: 'Import stopped after {n} of {m} rows. Nothing after row {n} was created. [Download what failed] [Resume]'
- **No permission:** If the user lacks import rights: 'Importing leads is turned off for your role. Ask an admin to import this file for you, or add leads one at a time with Quick Add.' The 'import for another seller' owner-picker is hidden entirely for sellers.
- **Mobile:** Import is a desktop-first workflow. On mobile we show step 1 and a clear message: 'Importing works best on a computer — the column mapping needs a wide screen. We can email you a link to finish this on desktop.'

### Duplicate Review
Resolve duplicate candidates: seller-scoped for same-owner matches, admin/supervisor-scoped for cross-owner ones.

**Key actions:** `Compare side by side` · `Keep field-by-field` · `Merge` · `Not a duplicate (dismiss)` · `Cross-owner only: release to new owner / keep with existing owner / discard`

- **Empty:** 'No duplicates to review. We check every new lead against your book by phone and email before it is created — anything we are not sure about shows up here.'
- **Loading:** Two-column compare skeleton; the timeline union preview loads last with its own inline spinner.
- **Error:** 'We could not load this comparison. Nothing has been merged. [Retry]' On merge conflict (record changed since load): 'This lead changed while you were reviewing. [Reload the comparison]' — we never merge stale state.
- **No permission:** A seller opening a cross-owner item sees the shielded view only: 'This person already exists in the agency book. To protect other sellers' privacy we cannot show you their record. An admin has been asked to review it.' No name, no owner, no history.
- **Mobile:** Side-by-side collapses to stacked cards with a per-field toggle (left/right) and a sticky merge summary bar. Merge always requires an explicit confirm on mobile.

### Unassigned Intake (admin / supervisor)
Quarantine for leads with no resolvable owner — the only place ownership is decided by a human.

**Key actions:** `Inspect raw payload` · `Assign owner` · `Bulk assign selection` · `Fix the source's owner mapping (deep link)` · `Discard with reason`

- **Empty:** 'Nothing waiting. Leads land here only when we cannot tell who they belong to — an unmapped vendor field, a disabled seller, or a source with no owner. An empty queue means every source is mapped correctly.'
- **Loading:** Table skeleton with the age column rendered first, since age is what drives urgency.
- **Error:** 'We could not load the queue. Incoming leads are still being captured and nothing is lost. [Retry]'
- **No permission:** 'Only supervisors and admins can see unassigned leads.' Sellers get no entry point in navigation.
- **Mobile:** Assignment is a common on-the-go action for a supervisor: single-row assign via a bottom-sheet seller picker with search. Bulk assign is desktop-only.

### Lead Sources & Performance (admin)
The taxonomy of every source in the tenant plus what each one actually produces.

**Key actions:** `Create/edit a source and its binding mode` · `Maintain the payload→owner map` · `Set cost per lead` · `View leads / contact rate / speed-to-lead / duplicates / closed-won / Earnings / cost per policy` · `Pause a source`

- **Empty:** 'No lead sources defined yet. Add the vendors, forms and campaigns you buy from — then this page tells you which ones actually turn into policies, and what each policy costs you. [Add a source]'
- **Loading:** Metric cells show shimmer placeholders while the attribution rollup resolves; the source list renders immediately from config so the page is usable before the numbers land.
- **Error:** 'Performance numbers are unavailable right now. Source settings below are current and safe to edit. [Retry metrics]' — config and analytics fail independently.
- **No permission:** 'Source performance is available to admins.' Supervisors see volume, speed-to-lead and duplicate rate but not cost fields, unless granted.
- **Mobile:** Table becomes a ranked card list ordered by cost-per-closed-policy; the full grid scrolls horizontally inside its own container so the page never scrolls sideways.

### Consent & Compliance panel (embedded on the lead record)
Show, at a glance and under audit, why we are allowed to contact this person.

**Key actions:** `View certificate (Jornaya/TrustedForm link, verbatim disclosure, IP, timestamp)` · `See per-channel grants` · `Record a verbal consent with note` · `Mark do-not-contact` · `View consent history`

- **Empty:** 'No consent record on file. Calls are allowed only if you have your own basis — texting is turned off for this lead. [Record how you got consent]'
- **Loading:** Compact skeleton for the badge row; the certificate iframe/link resolves separately and never blocks the rest of the lead page.
- **Error:** 'We could not load the consent record. Until it loads, texting stays blocked for this lead.' — fail closed, always.
- **No permission:** Sellers see status badges and the disclosure text but not the raw payload or IP; 'Full certificate details are visible to admins.'
- **Mobile:** Badges must be legible at a glance before a call: a single row of high-contrast chips (Call OK / SMS blocked / DNC) pinned above the fold on the lead screen.

### Intake Health & Delivery Log (admin)
Prove nothing is being lost, and recover it when it is.

**Key actions:** `Filter by source/status/date` · `Inspect a raw payload and its parse result` · `Replay one or many payloads` · `Edit and re-run the mapping` · `Acknowledge an alert`

- **Empty:** 'No intake traffic yet. Once a vendor or form starts posting, every request shows up here — including the ones that fail, so nothing you paid for disappears silently.'
- **Loading:** Virtualized log rows stream in newest-first with a live tail toggle.
- **Error:** 'The log is unavailable. Intake itself is unaffected — payloads are still being stored and will appear here once this recovers. [Retry]'
- **No permission:** 'Raw intake payloads can contain personal data and are restricted to admins.' Supervisors see counts and status codes with payload bodies redacted.
- **Mobile:** Read-only on mobile: status, counts and alerts. Replay and mapping edits are desktop-only because they are destructive-ish and need the payload diff view.

## Typical automations
- When a payload arrives on a seller's endpoint and passes dedupe → create the lead, push a notification to that seller's phone and desktop with a one-tap Call now, and place the card at the top of Fresh Leads.
- When a lead is created from a real-time purchased source → optionally add the contact to that seller's Aloware Power Dialer list so it is dialed agent-paced without manual queue building.
- When a lead is created and enrollment is configured on the source → enroll it in the owner's speed-to-lead sequence, which auto-pauses the moment the lead replies or books.
- When a fresh lead has had no first touch for 5 minutes → nudge the owner; at 30 minutes → notify the supervisor with the lead's source and age (never the lead's PII beyond what oversight allows).
- When an incoming lead exactly matches an existing lead in the SAME seller's book on phone and email with no conflicting fields → merge automatically, append the new source touch to the timeline, and tell the seller what happened.
- When an incoming lead matches a lead owned by a DIFFERENT seller → do not create it in the new silo; open a shielded cross-owner duplicate for admin review and tell the receiving seller only that the person already exists in the agency book.
- When a lead is created without a consent record or certificate → block SMS on that lead, mark it call-with-your-own-basis, and badge the card.
- When a STOP reply or a DNC match is received → revoke consent, add a tenant-wide suppression entry, remove the lead from every sequence and dialer list, and badge it do-not-contact for all sellers forever.
- When a lead's state is outside the owner's licensed jurisdictions → flag license_gap, soft-block outbound comms with an override-with-reason, and notify the supervisor.
- When a seller is deactivated → suspend their endpoints and forms, divert further vendor traffic to Unassigned Intake instead of returning errors, and alert the admin that a source now needs a new owner.
- When a source stops delivering for longer than its configured silence threshold, or its rejection rate spikes → alert the admin with last-received time and the top rejection reason.
- When a CSV import finishes with invalid rows → email the importer the annotated error CSV and keep the job resumable.
- When a mapping is corrected after a batch of rejections → offer one-click replay of every affected vaulted payload from the last N days.
- When an admin assigns a quarantined lead to a seller → the lead behaves exactly like a brand-new intake for that seller: it appears in Fresh Leads with a one-tap call and its own speed-to-lead clock starting at assignment.
- When an opportunity is closed-won → attribute the annual premium back to the originating source so cost per closed policy stays current without anyone maintaining a spreadsheet.

## Insurance specifics (Life / FE / IUL) & vertical-agnostic core
**What is genuinely Life/FE/IUL-specific here:** (1) *Ping-post economics* — purchased FE leads are sold in real time to multiple buyers and decay in minutes, so intake must be an always-on, sub-second, idempotent HTTP endpoint with retry/replay, not a nightly sync; a 502 on our side is a lead we paid for and lost. (2) *Consent certificates* — US life-lead vendors ship a Jornaya LeadiD and/or TrustedForm certificate URL plus the verbatim opt-in language, IP and timestamp; storing that blob is the agency's only defense in a TCPA suit, and the resulting `tcpa_consent_status` must travel to Aloware on every call and SMS. (3) *State licensing* — a seller may only solicit in states where they hold a resident/non-resident life license; a lead whose `state` is outside the owner's license set is a compliance event, not a sales opportunity, and intake is the only place to catch it. (4) *Underwriting knockouts at intake* — FE lead payloads carry date_of_birth (FE issue-age band is roughly 50–85), tobacco use, height/weight, existing coverage and requested face amount; IUL payloads carry income, investable assets and retirement horizon. These decide whether the lead is even workable before the first dial. (5) *Product intent* — FE and IUL are different sales motions (need-based vs accumulation), so `product_interest` must be captured at intake to pick the right pipeline and the right first script. **How the core stays vertical-agnostic:** none of the above is a column in the core schema. The engine knows only three primitives — `intake_profile` (a named, versioned field schema with typed fields + validators), `field_mapping_template` (vendor payload/CSV column → intake_profile field), and `intake_rule` (a declarative validator/gate such as "required", "range", "licensed_state_check"). "Final Expense v1" and "IUL v1" ship as seeded intake profiles, exactly as "Auto P&C v1" or a non-insurance vertical would later. `custom_fields` is a JSONB payload validated against the profile; the licensing check is a generic `owner_jurisdiction_check` rule bound to a generic `user_jurisdictions` table that happens to be seeded with life-insurance license states. Zero insurance vocabulary in table or column names.

## ⚔️ Adversarial review (sales-ops critic)

**Verdict:** NEEDS WORK — the ownership-binding thesis, the payload vault and the shielded-duplicate instinct are the right spine, but the module ships a lead it cannot legally or practically work: no live-transfer path, no vendor credit/return loop, no calling-window or intake-side DNC, TrustedForm stored but never retained, cost modeled per source instead of per lead, quarantine with no entry event, and dedupe/notification payloads that leak the exact cross-silo facts the UI copy promises to hide.

### Must fix — missing for real operation (15)
- LIVE / WARM TRANSFER INTAKE — absent entirely, and it is a large share of FE volume. The vendor posts the lead and simultaneously transfers a live caller. Intake must bind the post to the concurrent inbound Aloware call (by phone, within a seconds-wide window), screen-pop the record BEFORE the transfer connects, and mark the lead already-contacted so speed-to-lead is not computed as a failure. Without this the seller is talking to a stranger while hunting a lead that hasn't parsed yet.
- VENDOR CREDIT / RETURN WORKFLOW — the daily money loop of every FE floor. Bad number, wrong person, deceased, never-inquired, already-insured-at-intake, DNC, resold duplicate — all are returnable to the vendor within a contractual window. Needs a `credit_claim` record (lead, vendor, reason_code, evidence, status), a one-tap 'Return this lead' from the lead card and from the call disposition, a per-vendor claim export, and dedupe auto-generating duplicate-resold claims. Nothing in this spec recovers a dollar of bad lead spend.
- TRUSTEDFORM CERT RETENTION — storing certificate_url is legally worthless. TrustedForm certificates must be claimed/retained via API within their short lifetime or the evidence evaporates; Jornaya LeadiD needs verification too. Intake must fire the retention call within seconds of receipt, record the retained-copy id, and emit a failure event. This is a paid dependency (per-cert cost) that must appear against the <$100/mo infra target.
- CALLING-WINDOW / CURFEW ENFORCEMENT — completely missing, and intake is the only place it can be established. `timezone` is a field with no stated derivation (must be zip→tz, DST-aware) and no rule. Federal 8am-9pm lead-local plus stricter state regimes (FL, OK, WA mini-TCPA) must be computed at intake and travel on `lead.created` so the Fresh Leads 'Call now' button and the Power Dialer are gated. Today the spec hands a seller a one-tap call button on a lead in a state where it is 6:40am.
- DNC SCRUB AT INTAKE, not only at the dialer. The spec delegates DNC to Aloware, but the lead still lands in Fresh Leads with a primary Call button, and the Aloware Talk Chrome extension / a personal cell bypasses that backstop. Federal DNC + state registries + the tenant's internal suppression must be checked before the card is presented, and a suppressed lead must arrive already badged and un-callable.
- PHONE LINE-TYPE + DISCONNECT CHECK at intake. Aged FE lists run heavy on dead and reassigned numbers. A sub-cent line-type/activity lookup at intake removes the largest single time sink on the floor, and wireless-vs-landline is TCPA-relevant. Also: reassigned-number risk on aged leads has no handling at all.
- FE/IUL UNDERWRITING KNOCKOUTS are described in prose but never land anywhere operational — they are not in the seeded profile field list, not in the `lead.created` payload contract, and not on the Fresh Leads card. A seller dialing an FE lead needs age/DOB band, tobacco, height/weight, existing coverage, requested face amount and bank-draft date visible BEFORE the dial; IUL needs income/investable assets/retirement horizon. As specced the seller dials blind and re-asks everything the vendor already sold us.
- NO user↔Aloware number/user mapping entity, yet `inbound_number` is one of the five ownership binding modes and inbound-unknown-caller capture depends on it. Binding mode 5 has no table. Add `user_channel_identity` (user_id, provider, provider_user_id, phone_e164) — it is also what makes call/SMS webhooks resolve to a silo without a lookup.
- NEVER STATED whether `lead.created` produces an opportunity. The entire Earnings→leaderboard chain starts at a kanban card; if intake does not declare the policy (FE: opportunity at intake; IUL: opportunity on qualification), two modules will each assume the other does it and the board will be empty on day one.
- BULK OWNERSHIP TRANSFER IS mvp_candidate:false — wrong. FE telesales turnover is brutal; a seller will leave in the first month and their entire book becomes invisible to everyone including the owner who paid for it. This is the only sanctioned way to recover a book and it must ship in MVP. Trade it against auto Power-Dialer enrollment.
- IDEMPOTENCY ON vendor_lead_id WILL FAIL — a large share of FE vendors post no unique id. Needs a composite idempotency key (source_id + phone_e164 + payload content hash + time window) with vendor_lead_id as an override when present. As written, one vendor retry creates two paid-looking leads and inflates volume metrics.
- RE-POST / RE-INQUIRY BEHAVIOR IS UNDEFINED. When the same person submits again 3 weeks later, that is the single highest-intent event in FE. The spec auto-merges it into the existing record and emits `lead.merged` — with no statement of whether the lead re-enters Fresh Leads, resets the speed-to-lead clock, un-cools a cold lead, or re-triggers the speed sequence. As written the best lead of the week is silently absorbed into a row nobody is looking at.
- MOBILE NOTIFICATION HAS NO DELIVERY MECHANISM. This is responsive web, not a native app; reliable push with an actionable Call button does not exist on iOS Safari without PWA install, and that is where half the floor is. The entire speed-to-lead promise ('under 10 seconds to ringing, from any device') rests on a channel that will not deliver. Real options: an Aloware SMS/voice alert to the seller's own cell containing a deep link, plus a persistent audible in-browser alert on desktop. Decide it here or the module's north-star metric is unreachable.
- MULTIPLE PHONE NUMBERS AND BEST-TIME-TO-CALL. FE payloads routinely ship 2-3 numbers plus a stated callback window; the schema has exactly one `secondary_phone_e164` and no best_time field, so the dialer works the worst number first.
- QUARANTINE GAMES THE NORTH-STAR METRIC. Speed-to-lead restarts at assignment, so an unmapped vendor field converts a 4-hour delay into a perfect score. Report quarantine dwell time as part of speed-to-lead, not outside it.

### Too generic / boilerplate (9)
- utm_source / utm_medium / utm_campaign as first-class columns on `lead` is B2B-SaaS attribution copied wholesale. A purchased FE lead does not arrive with UTMs — it arrives with vendor account, sub_id/campaign_id, lead_type (fresh real-time / aged / live-transfer / direct-mail response), price paid FOR THAT LEAD, original opt-in timestamp, and cert URLs. Replace the UTM triple with `vendor_account`, `lead_type`, `price_paid_usd`, `original_optin_at`; keep UTMs inside custom_fields on the hosted-form path only.
- `cost_per_lead` on `intake_source` is generic marketing-spend modeling and it makes the CPA screen mathematically wrong. FE pricing varies per lead inside the same vendor and the same day (fresh $25-40, aged $0.25-3, live transfer $60-125). Cost must be `price_paid_usd` on the lead, read from the payload or set per import batch, and rolled up — not a constant on the source.
- The 'Source performance & cost-per-acquisition view (admin)' feature and its screen are a reporting module wearing an intake badge. Intake's job is to emit `lead.created` + attribution + `price_paid_usd`; dashboards-reporting renders CPA. As written, two modules will both claim the funnel rollup.
- `intake_rule` as a declarative validator engine plus `match_confidence` fuzzy scoring is the blank-canvas data model this project explicitly refuses. Two rules actually exist (required, jurisdiction). That is two hardcoded validators, not a DSL. It also contradicts the committed pattern of short plain-English vocabulary and ready-made templates over generic condition builders.
- Every screen carries a `no_permission_state` paragraph even where it is declared 'not applicable' — spec-template filler that inflates the module and hides the two permission states that actually matter (shielded duplicate, masked token).
- 'Nudge owner at 5 min, escalate to supervisor at 30 min' is a generic SLA ladder. In FE a real-time lead untouched at 30 minutes is dead, and the supervisor has NO lever — reassignment is forbidden. An escalation to someone who can only nag is theater. Either give it a sanctioned action (admin bulk transfer, or 'return to vendor for credit') or cut the second rung.
- HMAC signature verification + IP allowlist + optional per-source signing is enterprise webhook boilerplate. No FE lead vendor's dial-in-a-URL delivery form supports HMAC. Token-in-URL + per-source rate limit + payload vault is the real-world control set.
- The consent model is a generic per-channel grant object. It omits the two facts that actually decide a US life TCPA case: whether THIS agency/seller is a named party in the verbatim disclosure (the 'marketing partners' list problem), and whether the TrustedForm certificate was retained via API before it expired. Storing a certificate_url is the classic mistake.
- 'Dismiss to pipeline (mark as worked)' on Fresh Leads invents a lifecycle state inside a module that just declared lead-management owns status. Fresh Leads should be a query (first_touch_at IS NULL), not a status a seller can toggle.

### Bloat to cut (11)
- 'Source performance & cost-per-acquisition view (admin)' feature + its screen — belongs to dashboards-reporting. Intake keeps the attribution fields and the events; nothing breaks.
- 'Intake Health & Delivery Log (admin)' as a separate ninth screen — fold it into My Lead Sources with an admin scope toggle. Two screens answering 'is my source delivering?' in a one-admin agency is the 'brutal to administer' anti-pattern this project bans.
- Hosted web forms with embeddable script, honeypot, and two-to-three layouts. Already post-MVP, but even then it is the first inch of the GHL funnel-builder slope. The seller's Facebook form + a per-seller post URL does the same job with zero surface. Cut to one non-embeddable hosted layout or drop.
- HMAC signature verification and IP allowlist — no FE lead vendor will implement either. Token + rate limit + vault.
- Configurable soft-match secondary key (last_name + zip + dob) and `match_confidence`. MVP dedupe is exact E.164 and exact lowercased email. Fuzzy matching in v1 generates a review queue nobody empties and gives the cross-silo oracle more surface.
- Reversible merges with a configurable undo window. Expensive to build, near-never used. Keep the field-level review queue and the audit trail; drop undo.
- The `intake_rule` engine. Hardcode `required` and `owner_jurisdiction_check` inside `intake_profile`.
- Owner-jurisdiction check's full apparatus (soft-block + override-with-reason + supervisor notification). Keep the `license_gap` flag and the badge; the enforcement ladder is post-MVP.
- CCPA per-record purge UI with tombstoning as a shipped feature. Keep the suppression tombstone (it is required for correctness anyway); the purge itself is a rare, admin-run, audited operation, not a screen.
- `auto_sequence_id` on `intake_source` duplicates the automation module's stage/event trigger binding. One owner for 'what happens when a lead is created' — the automation engine. Intake emits, it does not configure.
- Nine screens is too much module for one thing entering one silo. Fresh Leads, Quick Add, My Lead Sources, CSV Wizard, Duplicate Review, Unassigned Intake are the six that survive.

### Event gaps (15)
- NO `lead.quarantined` EVENT. Unassigned Intake has an exit event (`lead.owner_assigned`) but no entry event. The KPI 'queue depth and median age of the oldest item' has nothing to start a clock, and no admin notification can fire. Paid leads sit silently — the exact failure the quarantine was invented to prevent.
- NO `intake.payload_replayed`. Replay is a headline feature and a headline KPI ('recovered-payload count — money literally saved'), yet a replay is indistinguishable from a normal `lead.created`. Needs its own event carrying replay_of_intake_event_id and the resulting lead.
- `lead.duplicate_detected` IS THE SILO LEAK. Its payload carries existing_lead_id, existing_owner_user_id and match_keys, and its consumers include notifications and lead-management — both seller-facing. Split into two events: `lead.duplicate_detected` (admin/supervisor scope, full ids) and `lead.cross_owner_blocked` (seller scope, carries the incoming payload id and nothing about the existing record).
- `lead.created` BROADCASTS FULL PII (first_name, last_name, phone_e164, email, custom_fields) to eight consumers including dashboards-reporting. Events should carry ids, owner, source, consent status and non-PII routing facts; consumers that need PII read the record under their own permissions.
- `call.inbound_unknown_number` INVERTS RESPONSIBILITY. Only intake owns the contact index, so comms cannot know a number is unknown without querying intake — a coupling loop. The event must be `call.inbound_received`; intake decides unknown-ness and creates the lead.
- `dnc.match_found` HAS NO NAMED EMITTER. No module in the spec owns it. If it is Aloware-webhook-derived it belongs to comms-aloware and must be named as such; otherwise it is a phantom event.
- `lead.first_touch_recorded` IS CONSUMED BY 'leaderboard' with no justification. The leaderboard is Earnings = closed-won deal_value. Either drop the consumer or declare speed-to-lead as the explicit secondary recognition category the fairness research demands ('Most Improved' is currently a promise with no data feed — first-touch could actually be it, but say so).
- `import.started` FIRES AT COMMIT — misnamed. And the dry run, which is the step that produces the numbers a human acts on, emits nothing, so an import's decision trail is unauditable.
- AUTO-MERGE EMITS `lead.merged` BUT NOTHING SAYS A RE-INQUIRY OCCURRED. Downstream Fresh Leads, sequences and the cold-lead timer have no defined reaction. Needs `lead.reinquiry_received` (or an explicit was_reinquiry flag with declared consumer reactions) or the highest-intent event in FE is swallowed.
- NO CONSENT LIFECYCLE EVENTS beyond captured/revoked: no `consent.certificate_retained`, no `consent.certificate_retention_failed`, no `consent.expired`. Without them the compliance dashboard shows green badges over evidence that silently never existed — the exact failure mode the spec's own risk section names.
- NO `intake.endpoint_resumed` / `intake.token_rotated` counterpart to `intake.endpoint_suspended`. Suspension is observable; recovery is not.
- NO QUIET-HOURS / CURFEW FACT ON `lead.created`. comms-aloware has nothing to gate a call or SMS on, so the calling-window rule cannot be enforced anywhere.
- `lead.owner_changed` IS CONSUMED BY leaderboard WITH NO STATED RULE. Does a book transfer move historical closed-won Earnings to the new owner? As written an offboarding silently re-ranks the public leaderboard. Declare it: Earnings stay permanently with the seller who closed; ownership transfer moves only open work.
- SYNCHRONOUS/ASYNCHRONOUS CONTRADICTION. The endpoint is specced write-first, respond 2xx in under one second BEFORE parsing, but the cross-owner rule says 'do not create it in the new silo.' The vendor was told success for a lead that will never exist, and no event records that loss — it is invisible in both the intake success rate KPI (2xx/total) and the lead count. Needs an explicit `intake.lead_suppressed` outcome and a reconciliation of the two designs.
- NO CONCURRENCY CONTRACT ON DEDUPE. Two ping-posts of the same consumer hitting two different sellers' endpoints in the same second, processed async in parallel, both pass dedupe and both create. Ownership determinism — the module's entire thesis — requires a stated locking/uniqueness key on normalized phone at the tenant level.

### 🚨 Silo violations (8)
- QUICK ADD'S LIVE DUPLICATE CHECK IS AN UNRATE-LIMITED CROSS-SILO ORACLE. The spec rate-limits webhooks but says nothing about the typeahead lookup, and never states the check is same-owner-scoped or that response timing and wording are constant. A seller can paste a competitor's prospect list into the phone field and enumerate the agency book from the fastest, least-audited path in the product.
- THE SHIELDED MESSAGE STILL CONFIRMS EXISTENCE. 'This person is already in the agency's book' IS the leak — it answers the only question the attacker asked. To be a real shield the accepted-lead response and the blocked response must be indistinguishable to the seller (accept it into their view as pending-review, or return identical copy and timing), with the truth visible only in the admin queue.
- `lead.duplicate_detected` SHIPS existing_owner_user_id AND existing_lead_id TO SELLER-FACING CONSUMERS (notifications, lead-management). The shield exists in the UI copy and is undone in the event bus.
- SUPERVISOR 'may create on behalf of a named seller' (permissions table) IS MANUAL ROUTING, and it contradicts both 'ownership is a property of the SOURCE' and the CSV rule that only admin may set target_owner. Remove it, or reclassify it as an audited assignment action with a required reason and a `lead.owner_assigned` emission.
- SUPERVISORS HOLD THREE ASSIGNMENT LEVERS AT ONCE — resolve cross-owner duplicates by 'release to new owner', assign from quarantine, and bulk-transfer books. That is a hand-cranked routing engine sitting in the supervisor role, and it is exactly how round-robin gets added in v2 'to save the supervisor time'. Constrain: cross-owner resolution and bulk transfer are admin-only, both require a typed reason, both emit `lead.owner_changed`, and the codebase must make any owner-selection function that takes a list of sellers structurally impossible.
- INTERNAL CONTRADICTION ON SUPERVISOR SCOPE. The KPI section says the untouched-lead metric is 'the only oversight signal that does not require seeing another seller's leads', while the permission table grants supervisors read on ALL leads in the tenant. Pick one — and note that the project brief grants supervisors global visibility, so the KPI's justification is the part that is wrong.
- TENANT-WIDE SUPPRESSION IS A JUSTIFIED CROSSING BUT ITS SIDE EFFECT IS NOT SPECIFIED. 'Mark do_not_contact on every matching lead across all silos' means seller B's card changes state because of seller A's STOP reply. Correct behavior, but the badge and any notification must not reveal that another seller was contacting the same person.
- NO RULE FOR A PRODUCING SUPERVISOR. In a 50-seat FE agency the supervisor usually carries a book and appears on the public leaderboard while holding global read access to every competitor's leads and sources. Either supervisors are excluded from the leaderboard, or oversight read must be audited and visible. Unaddressed.

## Risks & dependencies
- OWNERSHIP IS THE WHOLE BALL GAME: if binding_mode resolution has any hole, the product either drops paid leads or breaches the silo. The only sanctioned fallback is quarantine — the codebase must make round-robin/random/least-loaded impossible to add accidentally, because that is how a routing engine gets born by stealth.
- Many US life-lead vendors post to ONE URL per buyer account and will not maintain a URL per seller. If that is true for this agency's vendors, binding depends entirely on payload_field_map + a correctly maintained owner map — an admin data-entry error then silently misroutes paid leads into the wrong silo. Mitigation: map coverage report, unmapped-value alerting, and quarantine-by-default.
- Cross-silo dedupe is a privacy oracle by construction. The shielded response must be identical in timing and wording whether or not the match reveals anything sensitive, or a seller can enumerate the agency book with phone numbers. Needs rate limiting on duplicate checks per user.
- Public webhook endpoints are attack surface: token leakage (vendors paste URLs into shared docs), replay, and payload-stuffing to inflate a seller's lead count. Requires hashed tokens, optional HMAC, IP allowlist, per-source rate limits, and easy rotation with grace.
- Sub-second response with a raw-payload write means intake must be write-first / process-async. A synchronous dedupe-then-respond design will blow the latency budget under a ping-post burst and cost real leads.
- TCPA exposure is concentrated at intake. If certificate capture is optional in practice (sellers skipping attestation, vendors not sending LeadiD), the compliance story collapses while the UI still shows green badges. Consent status must fail CLOSED everywhere.
- Aloware dependency: no embeddable softphone means 'Call now' is a Two-Legged Call API round trip plus the Chrome extension as fallback. Sub-second perceived latency depends on Aloware's API responsiveness, which we do not control; needs an optimistic UI, a visible failure path, and per-minute overage awareness (~$0.02–0.04/min).
- Inbound-unknown-caller capture depends on Aloware webhook coverage and on a reliable number→seller ownership map; a shared main line has no deterministic owner and must quarantine.
- PII at rest: raw payload vault + import files are the densest PII in the system. Retention limits, encryption, admin-only access with access auditing, and CCPA purge that reaches the vault are mandatory, not nice-to-have.
- Suppression must be tenant-wide to be legally meaningful, which is the one deliberate exception to the silo model besides the leaderboard. It must be hash-based so it never becomes a browsable contact list.
- Infra budget < $100/month while running an always-on, burst-tolerant public endpoint with payload retention: needs a cheap queue and object storage tiering, plus retention caps on raw bodies. A naive log-everything-forever design breaks the budget.
- Depends on: comms-aloware (call/SMS events, opt-out, DNC, Power Dialer, Two-Legged Call), lead-management (lead lifecycle after creation), opportunities-pipeline (conversion and closed-won attribution), automation-engine (sequence enrollment and auto-pause), notifications (push for speed-to-lead), admin-settings (users, roles, jurisdictions, tenant config), compliance-audit (immutable audit log).
- Timezone correctness: FE calling-hour restrictions are enforced against the LEAD's local time. If timezone is not derived at intake from state/postal code, the first automated touch can be an after-hours call — a TCPA violation created by our own automation.

## Open questions
- CUT — predictive/automatic lead ASSIGNMENT of any kind (round-robin, load-based, skill-based). Explicitly forbidden by the silo decision; quarantine + manual assignment is the only path. Recorded here so a future agent does not 'helpfully' reintroduce it.
- CUT — lead scoring / AI prioritization at intake. We have no outcome history, no model, and FE speed-to-lead makes 'call the newest one' a better policy than any score. Revisit only after a year of closed-won data.
- CUT — fuzzy/ML identity resolution. Deterministic keys (E.164 phone, normalized email, optional last_name+zip+dob) plus a human review queue beat a probabilistic matcher that silently merges two different people. Nothing breaks without it.
- CUT — landing-page / funnel / drag-and-drop form builder. This is precisely the GHL agency bloat we refuse. Two or three fixed, profile-generated form layouts solve the real need; anything more is a website product.
- CUT — third-party data enrichment/append vendors at intake. Per-record cost, drift-prone snapshots, and no evidence it moves FE conversion. Revisit only if a specific vendor shows lift.
- CUT — business-card scanning / OCR intake and email-parsing intake. This is a telesales operation working purchased and referred leads; nobody is scanning cards. Cut with no loss.
- CUT — bulk phone/email verification service at intake. Aloware already handles DNC and deliverability concerns at send time; paying twice adds latency to the one path that cannot afford it.
- OPEN — Do this agency's actual lead vendors support per-buyer-seller post URLs, or only one URL per account? This single answer decides whether binding is endpoint_token (safe) or payload_field_map (needs a maintained map and alerting). Blocks final intake design; ties to unresolved OQ-2 on real channels and volume.
- OPEN — Which certificate provider(s) do the vendors ship: Jornaya LeadiD, TrustedForm, both, or neither? If neither, the agency's TCPA posture rests on vendor contracts alone and we should surface that risk in the UI rather than showing a green badge.
- OPEN — What is the true monthly/daily volume and burst shape of ping-post traffic? Drives the queue, rate limits, and whether the raw vault's retention fits the infra budget.
- OPEN — Same-owner exact-match policy: auto-merge silently, auto-merge with notification, or always ask? Recommendation is auto-merge with a timeline entry and a dismissible notice, but the client should confirm because it touches trust in the data.
- OPEN — For a cross-owner duplicate, what is the house rule: does the ORIGINAL owner keep the lead (protects the book, discourages poaching) or does the FRESHEST paid lead win (protects the money just spent)? This is a commercial policy decision, not an engineering one, and it must be settled before Duplicate Review can ship a default.
- OPEN — Should a seller be allowed to create their own intake endpoints self-service, or must an admin provision every source? Self-service is faster and fits the silo philosophy; admin-provisioned is safer for token hygiene and cost control.
- OPEN — Is a shared/main inbound number in play? If yes, inbound-unknown capture has no deterministic owner and every such call quarantines, which may be operationally annoying enough to need a different answer.
- OPEN — Retention period for raw payload bodies and import files (PII vs recoverability vs storage cost). Suggest 30–90 days for bodies with metadata kept longer.
- OPEN — Does the client want the untouched-lead escalation to reach the supervisor at all? It is the only oversight mechanism that does not require reading another seller's leads, but it can read as surveillance to a commission sales floor.
