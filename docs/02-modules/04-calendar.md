# 4. Calendar & Scheduling (Appointment Engine)

> **Module id:** `calendar` · part of [`docs/02-functional-map.md`](../02-functional-map.md)

## Verdict
KEEP as a first-class MVP anchor, but RESHAPE its identity: this is NOT "a calendar", it is the **appointment engine** of the CRM. Three reshapes. (1) It owns exactly one entity family — `meeting` and everything that keeps a meeting alive (reminders, availability, external calendar sync, booking links) — and it does NOT own the generic Activity/task object or the "My Day" list; it *feeds* them via `meeting.*` events. Building a second activity model here is the #1 way this module rots. (2) Its center of gravity is the **phone appointment**, not the grid: the highest-value surfaces are the 2-click booker launched from a kanban card, the Today strip with tap-to-call, and the outcome/no-show capture — the month grid is the least important screen and must never be the default view on mobile. (3) It must be **timezone- and TCPA-aware at the core**, because a life/FE seller books across four to six US timezones and every reminder and every dial the calendar triggers is a regulated contact. A calendar that can schedule a 7:00 AM lead-local call is a legal liability, not a feature. Everything that smells like a scheduling-SaaS product (round-robin pools, team/group events, room & resource booking, hosted video, payment-at-booking, multi-host) is CUT — round-robin in particular directly contradicts the per-seller silo model, since there is no routing engine and no shared pool of leads to route.

## Purpose & value
It makes sure that every promise a seller makes on a call — "I'll call you back Thursday at 3" — actually happens: it books the appointment from the lead card in two clicks, reminds the lead by SMS and email, puts the call one tap away at the right minute in the lead's own timezone, and refuses to let the seller finish the day without saying whether the lead showed up.

## Screens & states (9)

### My Calendar (Day / Week / Month / Agenda)
The seller's own appointment grid — their book of business in time. Week is the desktop default; Month exists for planning, never as the default.

**Key actions:** `Switch Day/Week/Month/Agenda` · `Book an appointment` · `Drag to reschedule` · `Open a meeting drawer` · `Start call from a row` · `Filter by appointment type or status` · `Jump to today / date picker`

- **Empty:** Shows the empty time grid (never a blank page) with a centered card: **"Nothing on your calendar yet."** / "Appointments start on the board. Open a lead in your pipeline and hit Schedule — or book one right here." Buttons: [Book an appointment] [Go to my pipeline]. Footer hint: "Tip: press N anywhere to book."
- **Loading:** The time grid, day headers and current-time line render immediately from local state; appointment blocks fade in as skeleton chips in their slots. No full-screen spinner — the seller can already orient in time. Google-sourced busy blocks arrive second and animate in with a subtle 'Syncing Google Calendar…' chip in the toolbar.
- **Error:** Grid stays visible with a top banner: **"We couldn't load your appointments."** / "Nothing was lost and no reminders were affected. [Retry]". If only the external sync failed, appointments render normally and a separate amber chip reads "Google Calendar isn't syncing — [Reconnect]".
- **No permission:** A seller opening another seller's calendar URL sees: **"That calendar isn't yours."** / "You can only see appointments in your own book of business. [Go to my calendar]". Supervisors and admins are routed to the Team Calendar instead of being blocked.
- **Mobile:** Day and Agenda only — Week and Month are hidden below 768px. Vertical scroll snapped to hours, current time pinned, sticky 'Today' bar, floating Book button in thumb reach, swipe-left on an appointment to log an outcome.

### Today (My Day agenda strip)
Answer 'who am I calling next and what do I know about them' in under two seconds. Mobile home screen; a strip on the desktop seller dashboard.

**Key actions:** `Call now` · `Expand pre-call prep card` · `Confirm / mark no-show / mark held` · `Reschedule` · `Send reminder now` · `Open the linked lead or opportunity`

- **Empty:** **"Nothing scheduled today."** / "An empty day is a cold pipeline. Pick a lead from your board and book a callback — it takes two clicks." Buttons: [Open my pipeline] [Book an appointment]. If the seller has unresolved past meetings, that counter renders above the empty state instead of being hidden.
- **Loading:** Three skeleton rows the height of a real appointment row so nothing shifts when data lands; the 'Needs outcome' counter renders last.
- **Error:** **"We couldn't load today's appointments."** / "Your appointments and reminders are unaffected. [Retry]" — with a link to the full calendar as a fallback path.
- **No permission:** Not reachable for another user; the strip only ever renders the signed-in seller's own day. Supervisors see their own (usually empty) strip plus a link to the Team Calendar.
- **Mobile:** This is the mobile landing screen. Each row is one tap to call and one long-press to log an outcome; lead-local time is always shown when it differs from the seller's.

### Quick Schedule sheet (2-click booker)
Create or reschedule an appointment without leaving the pipeline card, the lead timeline, or the call screen.

**Key actions:** `Pick a preset slot chip` · `Pick a custom date/time` · `Choose appointment type (pre-selected by stage)` · `Adjust duration` · `Toggle/preview the reminder ladder` · `See lead-local time` · `Save`

- **Empty:** Not a list screen, but it has a no-slots case: **"No open slots left today."** / "You're at your 8-appointment limit and your day ends at 6:00 PM. Try tomorrow, or book anyway." Buttons: [Show tomorrow] [Book anyway]. And a no-consent case: **"No SMS consent on file for this lead."** / "We'll send the email reminder and skip the text. You can still call."
- **Loading:** The sheet opens instantly with contact, type and duration already filled; only the preset slot chips shimmer while availability and conflicts resolve. Save stays disabled for that fraction with the label 'Finding your next open slot…'.
- **Error:** Inline above the Save button: **"We couldn't save this appointment."** / "Nothing was sent to the lead and nothing was added to your calendar. [Try again]" — the sheet never closes on failure and never loses typed input.
- **No permission:** **"This lead isn't in your book of business."** / "Only the seller who owns a lead can schedule with them." Shown if a stale card or deep link points at another seller's lead. Supervisors get the same message — read-only means read-only.
- **Mobile:** Full-height bottom sheet, preset chips first and thumb-sized, native date/time picker for the custom case, Save pinned above the keyboard.

### Meeting detail drawer
Everything about one appointment: who, when, why, what happened, and what it produced.

**Key actions:** `Start call` · `Log outcome (Held / No-show / Canceled)` · `Reschedule` · `Cancel with reason` · `Send a reminder now` · `Edit notes against the type's notes template` · `Open linked opportunity` · `Play recording / read transcript / read AI summary` · `View reminder and audit history`

- **Empty:** For a freshly booked meeting: **"Nothing has happened yet."** / "Reminders go out 24 hours and 1 hour before — we'll show them here as they send. Hit Start call when it's time."
- **Loading:** Header (contact, time, type) renders from the row the user clicked; timeline, reminders and call artifacts load beneath as skeleton lines. Recording/transcript blocks show 'Fetching from Aloware…' rather than appearing missing.
- **Error:** **"Some of this appointment couldn't load."** / "The appointment itself is fine — we couldn't reach the call recording. [Retry]" Partial failure never blanks the drawer.
- **No permission:** **"You can't open this appointment."** / "It belongs to another seller's book of business." Supervisors and admins see the full drawer with every write control hidden and a 'Read-only — oversight view' label.
- **Mobile:** Full-screen sheet; Start call and Log outcome are a fixed bottom action bar; transcript is collapsed by default with the AI summary shown first.

### Needs Outcome queue
Close the loop on every past appointment so show-rate is real and no-show automations can fire.

**Key actions:** `Mark Held` · `Mark No-show` · `Mark Canceled` · `Add outcome notes` · `Move the opportunity stage inline` · `Reschedule from the same row` · `Bulk-mark a selection`

- **Empty:** **"All caught up — every appointment has an outcome."** / "When an appointment ends it lands here until you say whether the lead showed. It takes one tap."
- **Loading:** Rows shimmer in reverse-chronological order, oldest unresolved first; the header count animates from the cached value rather than flashing zero.
- **Error:** **"We couldn't load your unlogged appointments."** / "[Retry]" — plus a reassurance line: "Nothing has been marked or discarded."
- **No permission:** Sellers only ever see their own. A supervisor sees a read-only cross-seller version labeled 'Outcome coverage' with the message **"You can see who hasn't logged outcomes, but only the owning seller can log them."**
- **Mobile:** Swipe right = Held, swipe left = No-show, tap = full drawer. This screen is designed to be cleared standing up at the end of the day.

Oversight of appointment load, confirmation and outcome discipline across all sellers — read-only by design.

**Key actions:** `Filter by seller, appointment type, status, date range` · `Toggle a per-seller lane view vs a merged view` · `See per-seller counts, confirm rate, show rate and unlogged-outcome count` · `Open a meeting read-only` · `Export the range`

- **Empty:** **"No appointments booked in this range."** / "Widen the date range, or check whether your sellers have started using the calendar — nobody has booked anything yet."

- **No permission:** **"The team calendar is for supervisors and admins."** / "Your own calendar is here. [Go to my calendar]" — shown to sellers, who must never see another seller's appointments.
- **Mobile:** Collapses to a per-seller summary list (name, appointments today, confirmed, unlogged outcomes); the lane grid is desktop-only.

Let a lead pick their own time and end phone tag; the public page is the only unauthenticated surface this module owns.

**Key actions:** `Create a booking link (slug, appointment type, duration, buffer)` · `Add qualifying questions` · `Activate/deactivate` · `Copy the link / insert into an SMS or email template` · `See submissions and the meetings they created`

- **Empty:** Manager: **"No booking links yet."** / "Create one and drop it into your follow-up texts — leads who won't pick up will still pick a time. [Create a booking link]". Public page with no availability: **"No times available right now."** / "Call or text us and we'll find a time that works."
- **Loading:** Public page shows the seller's name and the appointment type immediately, with the slot list skeletonized while availability is computed; the manager list uses row skeletons.
- **Error:** Public page: **"We couldn't book that time."** / "Someone may have just taken it. Pick another slot — nothing was charged and no details were saved." Manager: standard retry banner.
- **No permission:** Sellers manage only their own links: **"You can only manage your own booking links."** Admins can deactivate any link but cannot edit another seller's availability.
- **Mobile:** The public page is mobile-first by definition — large slot buttons, minimal fields (name, phone, email), explicit consent checkbox with TCPA/CCPA notice text, no account creation.

## Typical automations
- When a lead is created from a purchased/ping-post source and the seller hasn't reached them within X minutes → create a same-hour callback micro-appointment on that seller's day and push-notify them.
- When an appointment is booked → immediately send the confirmation (SMS + email), then queue reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before, all in the lead's local time.
- When a reminder would send outside the 8:00 AM–9:00 PM lead-local window → hold it and re-time it to the window edge instead of dropping it.
- When the contact has no TCPA consent, has opted out, or is on DNC → suppress the SMS reminder, send the email instead, and show the seller a 'no texting — confirm by voice' badge on the appointment.
- When the lead replies to a reminder with a confirmation keyword → mark the appointment confirmed and cancel every remaining reminder.
- When the lead replies asking to move it → cancel remaining reminders, flag the appointment, and put a pre-filled reschedule on the seller's Today list.
- When an appointment starts in 15 minutes → notify the seller with the pre-call prep card and a one-tap call button.
- When a call completes within ±30 minutes of a scheduled phone appointment with the same contact → attach the recording, transcript and AI summary to the appointment and pre-fill the outcome as Held.
- When a phone appointment's window passes with N unanswered attempts → propose the No-show outcome to the seller (never auto-final it silently).
- When an appointment is marked No-show → send the no-show SMS + email, create a follow-up task, and surface one-tap reschedule on the card.
- When a lead no-shows three appointments in a row → flag the opportunity for recycle-or-drop review by the owning seller.
- When an appointment ends and no outcome is recorded within 30 minutes → nudge the seller; after 24 hours, raise it in the Today header and the supervisor's outcome-coverage view.
- When a card moves to Quoted → prompt (or auto-create) a Quote Review call two business days out at the seller's next open slot.
- When a card moves to Application / Underwriting → schedule the carrier phone interview and a Policy Delivery appointment placeholder.
- When an opportunity is Closed-Won → cancel remaining prospecting appointments and schedule the Annual Policy Review 12 months out (IUL statement review).
- When an opportunity is Closed-Lost → hold reminders on its upcoming appointments and offer the seller one-click cancel-all.
- When a booking is attempted for a contact who already has an upcoming appointment → warn and offer to reschedule the existing one instead of creating a second reminder ladder.
- When a seller's Google Calendar sync fails or its token expires → show the reconnect banner in-app and email the seller; keep in-app scheduling and reminders fully operational.
- When a seller has a block of confirmed phone appointments today → offer to build the Aloware Power Dialer list for that block, in appointment order.
- When a contact's phone number or state changes → re-resolve their local timezone, re-time pending reminders, and warn if an existing appointment now falls outside the legal calling window.
- When a seller is deactivated → hold reminders on their future appointments and queue those appointments for transfer with the book of business.

## Insurance specifics (Life / FE / IUL) & vertical-agnostic core
"Genuinely Life/FE/IUL-specific: (1) The **phone appointment is the product**, not a fallback — FE and IUL are sold over the phone to seniors and pre-retirees, so channel defaults to phone, 'location' is a phone number, and the calendar's most important control is a dial button. (2) **Lead-local time and the TCPA calling window are load-bearing**, not polish: sellers work leads across four to six US timezones, the FE demographic (roughly 50–85) answers in the morning, and a dial or text outside 8:00 AM–9:00 PM lead-local is regulated exposure. (3) **Purchased ping-post leads decay in minutes**, so the calendar must support a same-hour, reminder-less callback micro-appointment — a horizon most scheduling tools don't even model. (4) The **vendor consent certificate and TCPA consent flag are snapshotted onto the meeting** and gate every reminder and every dial the calendar triggers; a suppressed reminder must be visible with its reason so the seller switches to voice instead of assuming the lead was reminded. (5) The **appointment vocabulary is the FE/IUL sales process**: Discovery Call, Quote Review, Application / Tele-app, **Carrier Phone Interview** (a meeting whose counterparty is the carrier's underwriting line, not the lead — so lead reminders must be suppressible per type), Policy Delivery / draft-date confirmation, and the **Annual Policy Review** that is the IUL statement conversation and the FE lapse-prevention touch. (6) **No-shows are the dominant outcome**, so no-show logging, reschedule chains and consecutive-no-show detection get first-class UI rather than living in a dropdown. (7) An IUL Quote Review is worthless without the **illustration attached to the meeting**, which is why the meeting carries attachments and a type-specific notes template.\n\nHow the core stays vertical-agnostic: none of the above is hardcoded. `meeting_type` (name, duration, channel, color, required fields, notes template, reminder set, counts_toward_show_rate) and `reminder_template` are tenant config rows; the Life pack ships as **seed data an admin can rename or delete**. The calling-window rule lives in `contact_window_policy` as a generic 'when may we contact a person' policy with warn/block enforcement and per-channel scope — a US regulatory concern, not a life-insurance one, and the same mechanism serves any future vertical or a stricter state rule. The stage→appointment-type mapping is configuration over the pipeline module's stages, so a second vertical means new stages and new types, not new code. The only non-negotiable primitives are: a meeting owned by exactly one seller, linked to a contact and optionally an opportunity, with a resolved counterparty timezone, a consent snapshot, a materialized reminder ladder, and a mandatory outcome."

## ⚔️ Adversarial review (sales-ops critic)

**Verdict:** NEEDS WORK - the compliance/timezone/outcome/auto-logging spine is genuinely right, but this is a spec for scheduling phone calls in general, not for selling Final Expense and IUL: the carrier tele-app three-way, the bank draft date, the persistency window, the spouse on the line, and per-number consent are all absent, while a Calendly clone, a month grid and a manager lane view are carried as first-class scope - and it ships a reminder ladder with reply-handling deferred, which is how you earn a STOP.

### Must fix — missing for real operation (15)
- CARRIER TELE-APP / PHONE INTERVIEW IS UNBUILT. This is the single biggest miss. Channel enum has carrier_interview and the starter pack has 'Carrier Phone Interview', and that is all. In FE the sale closes on a live three-way call to the carrier's tele-app line with a voice signature. There is no carrier entity, no carrier tele-app phone number, no carrier underwriting call-center HOURS (which differ per carrier, are stated in the carrier's own timezone, and are the actual booking constraint), no expected hold time, no three-way conference behavior from Aloware. Booking a 5:30pm ET tele-app for a carrier whose intake closes 5:00pm CT is the most common real-world scheduling failure in this business and this spec cannot even represent it.
- NO DRAFT-DATE SCHEDULING. FE premium drafts are set against the client's Social Security deposit date (1st/3rd of the month, or the 2nd/3rd/4th Wednesday by birth date). The agent picks the draft date at application and must touch the client before the first draft. There is no draft_date, no 'call before first draft' scheduled touch, and no first-draft-failed reaction. A failed first draft is a chargeback. This is the highest-dollar scheduled event in a Final Expense agent's life and it is absent.
- NO PERSISTENCY / CHARGEBACK-WINDOW CADENCE. Agents are charged back on lapses inside the 9-12 month advance window. The only post-sale scheduling is an annual policy review 12 months out - which fires AFTER the money is already clawed back. Needs day-30 / day-60 / day-90 retention touches auto-scheduled on Closed-Won, driven by the policy, not by a generic meeting_series.
- NO SECOND ATTENDEE / SPOUSE OR DECISION-MAKER. meeting has exactly one contact_id. 'Thursday at 3 when my husband gets home' is the most common booking sentence in FE, and IUL almost never closes with one spouse alone. There is no second attendee, no second phone number to conference, no 'both parties required' flag on the appointment type. Reminders therefore go to one person for a two-person decision.
- NO PER-NUMBER CONSENT OR MULTI-NUMBER HANDLING. TCPA consent attaches to a phone NUMBER, not a person. FE seniors routinely have a landline and a cell. meeting stores one phone_number_or_location and tcpa_consent_snapshot as a single scalar. The system therefore cannot answer 'may I text THIS number' and cannot pick which number the reminder or the Start-call action uses.
- TIMEZONE RESOLUTION METHOD IS UNSAFE. The spec resolves contact_local_timezone from 'state + phone NPA'. Cell NPAs lie constantly for this demographic (a Michigan area code living in Florida). NPA must be the last fallback behind state/ZIP, and the resolved timezone needs a confidence flag surfaced in the booker ('timezone guessed from area code - confirm'). As written, the module will confidently print a wrong lead-local time, which is worse than printing none.
- NO STATE-LEVEL CALLING RULES OR SUNDAY/HOLIDAY BLOCKING. Several states are stricter than the federal 8am-9pm window and some restrict Sunday solicitation. Selling to seniors, Sunday-morning dials and federal-holiday dials are also commercially dead. The policy entity has no state, no day-of-week, and no holiday calendar.
- SMS REPLY HANDLING IS mvp_candidate FALSE WHILE THE REMINDER LADDER IS mvp_candidate TRUE. That combination ships a system that texts a lead 24h and 1h before an appointment and ignores 'cant do it, call me Friday'. That is exactly the behavior that earns a STOP and permanently kills the SMS channel for that lead - and it is the anti-pattern the spec itself names elsewhere. Either reply-matching ships with the ladder or the ladder does not ship.
- NO NO-SHOW ATTEMPT PROTOCOL. Real FE practice at appointment time is three back-to-back dials, then a text, then a next-morning attempt. The spec has attempt_count and a threshold but no attempt ladder, no defined spacing, no auto-queued second attempt. 'Propose No-show after N attempts' with no mechanism to generate those attempts leaves the seller doing it manually.
- NO LEAD-VENDOR RETURN/CREDIT SLA LINKAGE. The speed-to-lead micro-appointment exists but never connects to the ping-post vendor's bad-lead return window (typically 24-72h). A lead that is unreachable after the callback ladder should feed the vendor-return workflow before the window closes. That is real money for the owner and it is nowhere.
- PURCHASED-LEAD 'BEST TIME TO CALL' IS IGNORED. FE/IUL lead posts commonly carry a best-time-to-call field. The Quick Schedule preset chips are generated purely from seller availability and never bias toward the lead's stated window or the seller's historical answer-rate by hour.
- IN-PERSON PATH IS DECLARED AND THEN ORPHANED. Channel enum includes in_person, but the spec deliberately collapses location to 'the contact's phone number, not an address field'. Some FE agencies still run field appointments. Either cut in_person from the enum or define address, travel time and a map link. A half-defined channel becomes a support ticket.
- NO CALLING-WINDOW RE-CHECK FOR THE APPOINTMENT ITSELF AT DST BOUNDARIES. contact_local_timezone is stored as a resolved value on the row (correct instinct), but nothing re-validates a meeting booked in October for a date after the DST switch, so a legal 8:05am booking silently becomes a 7:05am dial.
- NO CCPA RETENTION/DELETION STORY FOR SCHEDULING DATA. booking_submission hoards consent_ip, consent_user_agent and answers with no retention period, and the CCPA erasure path is a single permission row. Recordings/transcripts attached to meetings inherit the same gap.
- THE FAIRNESS MITIGATION THE CLIENT'S OWN RESEARCH DEMANDS IS mvp_candidate FALSE. The show-rate / appointments-set metrics feed is the designated secondary recognition category that offsets an all-time revenue-only leaderboard - and it is marked non-MVP. So v1 ships the exact demotivating board the research warned against, with the antidote deferred.

### Too generic / boilerplate (10)
- Day/Week/Month/Agenda as the flagship screen is Google Calendar boilerplate. The spec's own verdict says the month grid is the least important screen, then ships four views as MVP screen #1 with full empty/loading/error/no-permission states. An FE seller dialing 150 numbers a day never opens a month grid. MVP surfaces should be exactly two: Today (list) and Needs Outcome. Week grid = v1.1, Month = v2.
- The availability_profile (buffer_minutes, min_notice_minutes, daily cap, working hours) is a Calendly object model, not a telesales one. A life seller's day is not bookable gaps between meetings; it is Aloware Power Dialer blocks interrupted by appointments. There is no dial_block concept anywhere (availability_exception.kind = blackout | extra_availability only), so the system cannot know 9-11am is a dial session, and meeting.starting_soon has no way to tell the dialer to stop feeding. Replace buffer/min-notice with dial blocks vs appointment slots.
- Booking links + public booking page + booking_submission + qualifying questions + redirect_url is an entire second product (Calendly) carried in this spec's schema, screens, permissions and events. Final Expense buyers are 55-85, largely landline, responding to direct-mail/Facebook leads. They do not self-book from a slug. This is at best an IUL-prospect surface. It belongs in one future-scope sentence, not two entities, a screen, an event and four permission rows.
- Team Calendar with per-seller lanes, merged-view toggle and range export is the generic manager calendar every CRM ships. A life agency owner does not scan lanes. His questions are exception questions: who has zero appointments booked for tomorrow, who has aged leads with no next appointment, who has 11 unlogged outcomes. Ship an exception list. The lane grid is the most expensive screen in the module for the least behavior change.
- contact_window_policy is written as a single tenant-wide 8am-9pm rule with a free-text jurisdiction_notes field, and the notes claim stricter state rules can be layered later without touching the scheduler. That is false: there is no state/jurisdiction dimension on the entity, so adding state rules IS a schema change plus a scheduler change. This is generic compliance theater, not a US life-telesales calling-window model.
- Appointment types with name/duration/color/sort_order plus an admin CRUD screen with a merge-field editor, preview panel and test-send is heavy generic config for five seeded rows. The vertical-agnostic seam is real, but the admin UI should be a settings list, not a screen with its own four-state design.
- Conflict/double-book detection is framed generically ('two Final Expense seniors at 10 AM'). In phone telesales overlapping appointments are rare; the real conflicts are appointment-lands-inside-a-dial-block, appointment-lands-outside-carrier-underwriting-hours, and appointment-lands-when-the-spouse-is-at-work. None of those are modeled.
- meeting.updated with a changed_fields array consumed by activity-timeline is generic CRUD noise. Nobody wants 'duration changed 30 to 45' threaded into the lead timeline next to a call recording.
- The reschedule flow assumes a grid: drag-to-reschedule is mvp_candidate true, but mobile is Day/Agenda only and the spec itself says the grid is the least important surface. Drag is desktop sugar, not MVP.
- Read-only iCal subscription feed and the sync_direction push_only|two_way user choice are generic calendar-app features. Give one sync mode and drop the .ics entirely from the spec until a seller complains.

### Bloat to cut (13)
- Month view (and arguably Week) from MVP. Nothing breaks: a seller who lives in Today + Needs Outcome loses nothing, and the spec already concedes the grid is the least important surface.
- Booking links, public booking page, booking_submission, qualifying questions, redirect_url, and the entire booking-links manager screen. Cut to a one-line future note. Nothing breaks in MVP: an FE senior was never going to self-book.
- Read-only iCal subscription feed. Nothing breaks - the Outlook minority can be told to use email invites.
- meeting_series as a general recurrence entity. Replace with a policy-driven retention touch generator (day 30/60/90/365) owned by the post-sale flow. A generic series engine with interval_months and end_condition solves a problem this business does not have.
- external_busy_block.opaque_title plus the full-detail import toggle. One privacy default (opaque), no setting. Nothing breaks.
- sync_direction push_only|two_way as a user-facing choice. Pick two-way with opaque inbound busy blocks. A seller who has to choose a sync direction will choose wrong and blame the product.
- availability_profile.min_notice_minutes and buffer_minutes. When 90 percent of bookings are preset chips, these knobs only produce 'why can't I book in 20 minutes' support tickets. Keep working hours and daily cap.
- Cmd+K natural-language time parsing ('book maria tomorrow 3pm'). NLP date parsing is an unbounded rabbit hole with a long tail of wrong-year/wrong-timezone bugs. Cmd+K should open the pre-filled sheet on the contact; chips finish the job.
- meeting.updated as a timeline event. Keep the audit entry, drop the broadcast.
- Team Calendar lane grid, merged-view toggle and range export. Replace with a supervisor exception list. Nothing breaks: no supervisor decision depends on seeing appointment blocks laid out in time.
- The dual knobs contact_window_policy.enforcement (warn|block) AND allow_override AND calling_window_override on the meeting. Three ways to express the same thing, and a logged override on a federal calling window is a document that helps a plaintiff, not the owner. Federal window = hard block, no override. Stricter internal rules = warn.
- Drag-to-reschedule as an MVP item. Downgrade to post-MVP desktop polish; the one-tap reschedule chip already covers the real path.
- Appointment type color and sort_order, and the send-test-SMS/email affordance on the admin screen. Cosmetic surface on a five-row config table.

### Event gaps (15)
- No meeting.reminder_scheduled / meeting.reminder_canceled. The data model claims rescheduling cancels pending rows and materializes new ones (the correct anti-zombie design), but emits nothing, so communications and the timeline cannot reconcile a send that was cancelled a second before dispatch. This is the exact mechanism that produces a 'see you tomorrow' text for a cancelled appointment.
- No meeting.reschedule_requested. The sms.received reaction says it 'flags the meeting and creates a reschedule prompt' with no event, so notifications and automations have nothing to subscribe to. A lead asking to move an appointment is a first-class business event.
- No meeting.needs_outcome (state entered). Only meeting.outcome_overdue exists, at +30 minutes. My Day, the Today header counter and notifications need the entry event, not just the escalation.
- No event for the recycle signal. 'Three consecutive no-shows -> flag for recycle-or-drop' is listed as an automation but consecutive_no_shows is only a payload field on meeting.no_show. Pipeline and automations need an explicit threshold event (e.g. contact.appointment_abandoned) or every consumer has to re-implement the counting.
- Event naming is incoherent for the module's own headline KPI. meeting.completed (lifecycle verb) vs meeting.no_show (outcome noun) means any consumer computing show rate must subscribe to two differently-shaped events. Use meeting.outcome_recorded with outcome in the payload, or held/no_showed consistently. Also, the spec insists outcome is deliberately separate from status, then emits only lifecycle events - the sales outcome of a held meeting is never broadcast.
- status includes in_progress but no event or consumed signal ever transitions into it. call.started / call.answered are not consumed.
- No consumption of delivery failure. meeting_reminder.status has 'failed' and the KPI list includes reminder delivery rate by channel, but nothing consumes an sms.delivery_failed / email.bounced signal from Aloware or the email provider. As written that status can only ever be set by a local send exception, and the KPI is unmeasurable.
- No consumption of Aloware DNC / suppression webhooks. The module consumes contact.opted_out and contact.consent_updated (internal) but never the carrier-side suppression Aloware applies. Suppression reason 'dnc' is in the enum with no producer.
- Sequence auto-pause is not contracted. Research pattern 6 (sequences auto-pause the moment a lead books) requires communications to disenroll the contact from the Aloware sequence on meeting.scheduled. 'communications' appears in expected_consumers but the required behavior is never stated, so it will not get built and a booked lead will keep receiving cadence texts.
- SYNC ECHO LOOP. meeting.scheduled lists source = external_sync as a valid origin and fires the full consumer fan-out. Our own event pushed to Google, re-imported, then re-emitted as meeting.scheduled will re-trigger automations, timeline entries and reminder materialization. There is no echo-suppression / external_event_id round-trip rule anywhere. This is a real, shipped-in-week-one bug.
- No compliance event for the calling-window override. meeting.reminder_suppressed goes to compliance-audit, but calling_window_override - the higher-risk act, a human deliberately scheduling regulated contact outside the legal window - is only a boolean on the row. It needs its own audited event with actor and reason.
- meeting.starting_soon carries phone_number in the payload and lists 'dashboard' as a consumer. Regulated PII should not be fanned out to a dashboard consumer; pass ids and let the owner-scoped surface resolve the number.
- user.deactivated produces 'orphaned' meetings with no emitted event, so the admin transfer UI has nothing to react to and no notification fires. Orphaned meetings with held reminders will sit silently until someone notices.
- availability.updated lists 'calendar' (itself) as a consumer - self-subscription noise that signals the event list was written per-module rather than per-contract.
- meeting.confirmed exists but there is no corresponding 'confirmation attempted and no reply' signal, so the confirmation-rate KPI has a numerator with no defined denominator event.

### 🚨 Silo violations (8)
- Admin may 'create a meeting on any lead (data repair only)'. Creating a meeting on another seller's lead is functionally assignment - an admin can materially direct a seller's day and put regulated outbound contact on their calendar. Data repair is editing an existing record, not creating a new obligation. This should be denied, or narrowed to edit/cancel only.
- automation.action_requested executes schedule/cancel/reschedule 'against the owning seller's calendar' with no stated constraint on who authored the automation or which records can trigger it. An admin-authored automation that can materialize meetings on any seller's calendar is a lead-routing engine through the back door. Constrain explicitly: automations may only act on the owning seller's own records, and never move ownership.
- EXISTENCE ORACLE - Quick Schedule no_permission_state reads 'This lead isn't in your book of business. Only the seller who owns a lead can schedule with them.' That confirms to seller A that the record exists and is owned by someone else. Combined with a guessable id or a stale link this leaks the shape of other sellers' books. Return a not-found message, not an ownership message.
- Same leak in the Meeting detail drawer: 'It belongs to another seller's book of business' confirms both existence and cross-seller ownership to a seller.
- The duplicate-appointment guard needs an explicit scoping statement. If it checks 'does this contact already have an upcoming meeting' tenant-wide rather than owner-scoped, it will surface another seller's appointment with that contact ('Maria already has a Quote Review Thursday 2:00 PM'). Nothing in the spec scopes it to owner_user_id.
- Booking-link slugs are per-seller and public. Nothing states that slugs must be non-enumerable or that the public page must not reveal the seller's full availability pattern. A public page is a cross-tenant read surface and needs an explicit rule.
- Supervisor permissions are internally contradictory in a way that will get built wrong: 'View another seller's individual calendar = Read-only, full detail' but the Needs Outcome supervisor view is described as 'Outcome coverage' (counts). Pick one. If supervisors see full detail, say so once; if oversight is counts-only, the calendar row must say counts-only.
- Note (not a violation, credit where due): there is no round-robin, no pool, no queue, no reassignment on booking. The core silo instinct is intact. The leaks above are edges, not architecture.

## Risks & dependencies
- **No embeddable Aloware softphone.** 'Start call' must go through the Two-Legged Call API (server-initiated, calls the seller's phone then the lead) or the Aloware Talk Chrome extension. The API path has a perceptible connect delay and the extension path requires an install on every seller's browser and only works on pages Aloware can parse. Decide which is the primary path before building the UI around it; the fallback is a tel: link, which loses attribution.
- **Call ↔ meeting auto-linking is heuristic.** Matching on contact + phone + a ±30-minute window is wrong often enough to matter in Final Expense, where a household shares one landline and a spouse may be a separate contact. Mitigation: require a contact-id match (not just a phone match), expose a confidence level, never finalize an outcome automatically below the threshold, and always allow manual unlink. Getting this wrong corrupts show-rate, which then corrupts any leaderboard category built on it.
- **Google Calendar integration is the module's largest technical risk.** OAuth verification for the app, per-user consent, token expiry and revocation, API quotas, DST edge cases, and sync loops (our write echoing back as an inbound change). Mitigation: our system is the source of truth for meetings we create; inbound events are imported as opaque busy blocks only; every synced event carries our meeting id; sync failure degrades to local-only and never blocks scheduling or reminders.
- **The reminder scheduler needs durable, timezone-correct, at-least-once job execution** with idempotency (a retried job must not text a lead twice) — inside a sub-$100/month infra budget. Materializing one row per planned send makes this auditable, but the runner is real infrastructure, not a cron loop, and it is a shared dependency with the Automations module. Decide early whether both modules share one scheduler.
- **SMS cost and A2P 10DLC throughput.** Three reminders per appointment across 50 sellers is a real per-message spend and a real throughput ceiling on the registered campaign. Reminder ladders must be tunable per appointment type, and the module should never send a reminder for an appointment starting in less time than the offset (a common source of duplicate/late texts).
- **Timezone correctness depends on the Contacts module** supplying reliable state and/or a parseable phone NPA. Bad or missing data silently produces an appointment in the wrong local time, which is both a lost sale and a compliance exposure. Mitigation: show lead-local time everywhere it differs, require an explicit timezone when it can't be resolved, and warn on the calling window rather than assuming.
- **Silent reminder suppression would be worse than no reminders.** Aloware gates SMS on TCPA approval and handles STOP/DNC natively; if that suppression isn't surfaced back into our meeting record, sellers will believe leads were reminded. This requires reliable inbound webhook handling and a visible suppression_reason on the meeting.
- **Booking links open the only unauthenticated surface in the product** — spam and bot submissions, PII collected from strangers (CCPA notice-at-collection obligations), consent-artifact retention, and a dedupe path that must reuse the intake module's rules rather than inventing a second one. This is why booking links are scoped as a later capability, not an MVP anchor.
- **Cross-module ownership boundary risk.** This module must not build its own task/activity object or its own 'My Day' list — it emits meeting.* and the Activity/Timeline module renders. If that boundary blurs, we end up with two competing definitions of 'next activity' on the kanban card. Requires an explicit contract with the pipeline and timeline modules.
- **Scope cuts made deliberately, all of which someone will ask for:** round-robin and pooled booking (directly contradicts the per-seller silo and the no-routing-engine decision), team/group and multi-host meetings, room and resource booking, hosted video conferencing (we store a link, we don't host), payment-at-booking, a blank-canvas availability rule builder, a full RRULE recurrence engine, and cross-seller meeting reassignment outside the book-of-business transfer flow. Each fails the 'what breaks for this seller or this owner' test in a 50-seat phone-sales shop.
- **Also cut:** meeting-level agendas/attendee-invite RSVP mechanics (leads don't run Google Calendar invites — they answer a phone), and calendar-based 'availability sharing' between sellers, which would leak silo boundaries.
- **Supervisor read-only global calendar is deliberately an exception to the silo.** It must be enforced server-side per query, not by hiding UI, and it must never become a write path — the moment a supervisor can book on a seller's behalf, we've reintroduced assignment through the back door.

## Open questions
- Do sellers actually work leads across multiple US timezones, and how many? This decides whether lead-local time is a prominent everywhere-visible element or a secondary detail, and how aggressive the calling-window enforcement default should be (warn vs block). Ties to OQ-2.
- Are the 50 sellers on Google Workspace accounts under one domain, or personal Gmail? A Workspace domain enables a much simpler admin-consented OAuth path; personal accounts mean 50 individual consent screens and a full app verification process.
- What is the real FE/IUL appointment sequence today — is there a Policy Delivery step, a carrier tele-app/phone interview, a draft-date confirmation call? The seeded appointment-type pack should mirror how this team actually sells, not an industry-generic guess. Ties to OQ-4.
- Reminder cadence default: is 24h + 1h right for a senior FE audience, or is 24h + 2h + morning-of better? Over-texting invites STOP replies, which permanently kills the SMS channel for that lead.
- Should a no-show automatically move the opportunity to a follow-up stage, or leave the stage untouched? Auto-moving creates stage churn and distorts time-in-stage; leaving it risks a stalled card. Recommendation: leave the stage, fire the follow-up, let the board's stale alert do its job.
- Should show rate or appointments-set become the secondary recognition category on the leaderboard (the fairness counterweight to an all-time Earnings board)? The calendar can supply the data; the leaderboard module owns the decision.
- Do we need Outlook/Microsoft 365 sync at all, or does a read-only iCal feed cover the non-Google minority?
- Booking links: MVP or V1.1? Phase 0 explicitly deprioritized them, but they are the single strongest answer to 'the lead won't pick up'. Recommendation: V1.1, immediately after Google sync stabilizes.
- When a seller leaves, who inherits their future appointments — and do the leads get notified of the change before the new seller dials? There is a real trust and TCPA-consent question in handing a booked appointment to a different human.
- How long do we retain recordings, transcripts and consent artifacts attached to meetings? CCPA erasure requests must reach them, and storage cost sits inside the sub-$100/month target.
- Naming normalization for cross-module events: this spec consumes `contact.opted_out`, `contact.consent_updated`, `call.no_answer`, `automation.action_requested`, `user.book_transferred` and `pipeline.settings_updated`. If the communications, automations and admin modules named these differently, the integration map must reconcile them — the calendar will adopt whatever the catalog settles on rather than defending its own spelling.
- Is the ±30-minute call↔meeting matching window right, or should it be per-appointment-type? A Quote Review that runs 20 minutes late is normal; a 15-minute speed-to-lead callback is not.
