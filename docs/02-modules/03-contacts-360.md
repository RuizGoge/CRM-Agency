# 3. Contacts 360° — The Lead Record

> **Module id:** `contacts-360` · part of [`docs/02-functional-map.md`](../02-functional-map.md)

## Verdict
KEEP as-is, and RESHAPE its internal center of gravity. This is the load-bearing module of the product: every other module (pipeline, telephony, sequences, calendar, leaderboard) writes to or reads from this record, and the per-seller silo is enforced here first (owner_user_id on the contact is the root of all visibility). Two reshapes are mandatory. (1) This module is NOT a form — it is a TIMELINE with a form attached. Build the unified timeline + the Aloware auto-log pipeline first; the field editor is secondary. A CRM that costs the seller typing dies of data rot, and auto-logged calls with recording/transcript/AloAi summary are the cheapest way to keep the record true without asking the seller for anything. (2) Compliance is not a side panel, it is a GATE: TCPA consent, opt-out and DNC live on the contact and physically disable the Call/Text buttons. Nothing in the system reaches a phone number without asking this module first. Two boundary corrections vs the naive scope: duplicate DETECTION at intake/import belongs to lead-intake (it happens before a record exists) — this module owns the MERGE, its audit and its reversibility; call/SMS/email artifacts (recording file, transcript, AloAi summary body) belong to the telephony/comms module — this module owns the timeline_entry that references and renders them. Nothing to merge, nothing to cut at module level.

## Purpose & value
One screen per person that tells the seller everything that ever happened with this lead — every call with its recording and AI summary, every text, every note, every stage move — plus the insurance facts (age, state, health, existing coverage, draft date) and whether it is legally safe to dial or text them right now.

## Screens & states (7)

### Contact Record (360°)
The single working surface for one lead: identity and consent on top, unified timeline in the center, insurance profile and opportunities in the rails — everything needed before, during and after a call.

**Key actions:** `Call now (Aloware two-legged / click-to-call)` · `Send SMS` · `Send email` · `Log an activity manually` · `Book a meeting` · `Edit insurance profile fields` · `Add note / pin note` · `Add tag` · `Create task` · `Open an opportunity or change its stage inline` · `View consent and certificate` · `Flag duplicate / start merge` · `Archive contact` · `Reassign owner (supervisor/admin)`

- **Empty:** New contact with no history — headline: 'Nothing has happened yet with Maria.' Body: 'The clock started 0:42 ago. Purchased leads go cold in minutes.' Primary button: 'Call now'. Secondary: 'Send a text' and 'Log an activity'. Footnote: 'Every call, text and email logs itself here automatically once you start.' If consent is missing instead — headline: 'You cannot contact Maria yet.' Body: 'No consent on file for calls or texts. Add the consent certificate, or capture verbal consent on a recorded call.' Primary button: 'Add consent record'.
- **Loading:** Header and rails render as skeletons immediately from cached identity; the timeline streams the newest 25 entries first with a thin progress bar and loads older entries on scroll. Recording and transcript panes load independently so a slow Aloware fetch never blocks the record.
- **Error:** Record fails to load: 'We could not open this contact. Nothing was changed.' with Retry and 'Back to my book'. Enrichment fails: amber strip on the affected call entry — 'Transcript not available yet. Aloware usually delivers it within a few minutes.' with Retry. Save fails: the field keeps the typed value, turns amber, shows 'Not saved — retrying…' then 'Could not save. Copy your text before leaving this page.'
- **No permission:** 'This contact is not in your book.' Body: 'Leads are private to the seller who owns them. If you think this one is yours, ask your supervisor.' No name, phone, email or field value is rendered. The attempt emits contact.access_denied.
- **Mobile:** Header collapses to name + consent badge + a sticky two-button bar (Call, Text). Timeline is the default and only initial tab; profile and opportunities behind chips. Notes support voice dictation. When the Aloware call API is unavailable, tap-to-call hands off to the device dialer and prompts 'Log this call?' on return.

### Timeline (tab / full-height panel of the record)
Read the entire relationship in one scroll, filtered to what the seller is looking for.

**Key actions:** `Filter by type chips` · `Jump to date` · `Play recording / open transcript` · `Expand the AloAi summary and create a follow-up from it` · `Mark entries reviewed` · `Add a note inline` · `Copy entry link`

- **Empty:** 'No activity of this type yet.' Secondary line: 'Showing: Texts. Clear filters to see everything.' with a 'Clear filters' button. On a truly empty record it defers to the record-level empty state.
- **Loading:** Newest 25 entries stream in first with a 'Loading older activity' spinner pinned to the bottom. Changing a filter re-queries without clearing the visible list until results arrive.
- **Error:** 'We could not load the rest of this timeline.' with Retry; already-loaded entries stay on screen and remain readable.
- **No permission:** Recordings and transcripts are hidden from anyone who is neither the owner nor supervisor/admin: 'Recording not available to you.' Call metadata (time, duration, disposition) remains visible only to owner/supervisor/admin.
- **Mobile:** Filter chips scroll horizontally; the recording player docks to the bottom of the screen and keeps playing while scrolling.

### My Book of Business (contact list)
Work the people who are not currently on the kanban: no-answers, cold leads, past clients, the recycling pool.

**Key actions:** `Filter and sort` · `Save and switch views` · `Select multiple records` · `Bulk tag / enroll in sequence / push to Power Dialer list / assign task / archive` · `Create contact` · `Import (hands off to lead-intake)` · `Export own book (logged)`

- **Empty:** 'Your book is empty.' Body: 'Leads you buy or add land here, and they stay yours — nobody else sees them.' Primary button: 'Add your first contact'. Secondary: 'Import a list'. Empty filtered view: 'No contacts match this view.' with 'Clear filters'.
- **Loading:** Table skeleton with 10 shimmer rows; filter chips and the saved-view selector are usable immediately; row counts show 'Counting…' until resolved.
- **Error:** 'We could not load your book.' with Retry. A failed bulk action reports partial results explicitly: '48 of 60 updated. 12 skipped: 9 opted out, 3 on DNC.' with a 'See skipped' link.
- **No permission:** Sellers have no scope selector at all — the owner filter is absent, not disabled. If a supervisor's scope is revoked mid-session: 'Your access to team data changed. Showing only your own contacts.'
- **Mobile:** Table becomes a card list showing name, phone, days since last touch, next activity and consent badge; bulk selection is limited to tag and task actions.

### Consent & Compliance panel
Show in one place whether this person may legally be called, texted or emailed — and prove it.

**Key actions:** `View consent per channel with source and date` · `Open the TrustedForm/Jornaya certificate` · `Attach a consent certificate` · `Record verbal consent (linked to the call recording that captured it)` · `View opt-out history` · `View DNC/suppression status and last check` · `Request an admin opt-out reversal`

- **Empty:** 'No consent on file.' Body: 'Calls and texts are blocked until consent is recorded. Add the lead vendor's certificate, or capture verbal consent on a recorded call.' Primary button: 'Add consent certificate'. Secondary: 'How consent works'.
- **Loading:** Channel rows render greyed with 'Checking…' badges and the action buttons stay disabled while status is unknown — never optimistically enabled.
- **Error:** 'We could not verify consent right now.' Body: 'Calling and texting stay blocked until we can confirm. This protects you.' with Retry. Fails closed by design.
- **No permission:** Sellers see status and certificates for their own contacts, but the 'Clear opt-out' control is absent with the note 'Only an administrator can undo an opt-out.' No role, at any level, sees a DNC override.
- **Mobile:** Renders as a bottom sheet with one row per channel and a full-width red banner whenever an opt-out is active.

### Insurance Profile editor
Capture the facts that decide whether and at what price this person can be insured.

**Key actions:** `Enter DOB (age and rate band compute live)` · `Set state, tobacco, height/weight` · `Toggle health knockout flags` · `Add medications` · `Add beneficiaries with allocations` · `Add existing coverage / mark replacement` · `Set draft date and bank-on-file flag` · `Mark profile complete`

- **Empty:** 'No insurance details yet.' Body: 'Add date of birth and state first — that is all you need to start quoting.' Primary button: 'Add date of birth'. Remaining pack fields appear progressively as prerequisites are filled.
- **Loading:** Field-pack definitions load before any input renders, so the seller never types into a half-built form and loses it; a compact skeleton holds the space.
- **Error:** Inline per-field validation ('Beneficiary allocations must total 100% — currently 90%'). On save failure: 'Could not save the profile. Your entries are still here.' with Retry.
- **No permission:** Read-only rendering with 'You can view this profile but not edit it.' for view-only supervisors; sellers never reach another seller's profile at all.
- **Mobile:** Grouped accordions (Basics · Health · Beneficiaries · Coverage · Payment); numeric and date keyboards forced; age visibly recomputes on DOB entry.

### Duplicate Review & Merge
Kill duplicates without losing history, and let the agency see when it paid twice for the same person.

**Key actions:** `Review candidate pairs with match reasons and score` · `Dismiss as not a duplicate` · `Open side-by-side merge` · `Choose the surviving record and resolve field conflicts` · `Preview the merged timeline and opportunities` · `Merge` · `Undo merge within the reversible window`

- **Empty:** 'No duplicates to review.' Body: 'We check phone and email every time a lead lands or a list is imported. Anything suspicious shows up here.' No primary button — this is a good empty state and should read like one.
- **Loading:** Candidate cards stream in scored order; the merge preview shows a two-column skeleton before field values resolve.
- **Error:** 'The merge did not complete. Nothing was changed and both contacts are intact.' with Retry and 'Contact support'. Merges are transactional — a partial merge is never presented as success.
- **No permission:** Sellers see only same-owner candidates inside their own book. Cross-owner candidates are entirely invisible to sellers — not greyed, not counted. Supervisors and admins get a separate 'Same lead in two books' section.
- **Mobile:** Merging is desktop-only; on mobile the queue is viewable and dismissible, and the merge action shows 'Finish this on a computer — merging needs the side-by-side view.'

### Field & Tag Library (admin)
Define what a contact is for this tenant without touching code — the mechanism that keeps the core vertical-agnostic.

**Key actions:** `Create and edit typed field definitions` · `Group fields into packs (Life FE v1, Life IUL v1)` · `Set required/visible rules and stage-gating hooks` · `Reorder fields on the record layout` · `Manage the tag library: create, recolor, rename, merge` · `Archive a field (never hard-delete while values exist)`

- **Empty:** 'Only the standard fields are active.' Body: 'Add the fields your team actually asks on the phone — date of birth, tobacco, existing coverage. Or start from the Final Expense pack.' Primary button: 'Install the Final Expense pack'. Secondary: 'Create a field'.
- **Loading:** Definition list skeleton first; the record-layout preview renders last.
- **Error:** 'Could not save this field. No records were changed.' Deleting a field in use is refused: 'This field has values on 412 contacts. Archive it instead — the data stays and it stops appearing on the record.'
- **No permission:** Not reachable by sellers or supervisors; the settings entry is absent from navigation. Direct URL access shows 'Only administrators can change what a contact record looks like.'
- **Mobile:** Read-only on mobile; editing requires desktop.

## Typical automations
- When a contact is created from a purchased/ping-post lead → open the speed-to-lead countdown on the record and put a 'Call now' action at the top of the owner's My Day.
- When a purchased lead has had no outbound attempt after 5 minutes → notify the owner on mobile and emit contact.speed_to_lead_breached (the supervisor sees the aggregate, never the lead).
- When a call ends with a no-answer or voicemail disposition → create a follow-up task for the owner at a spaced interval, capped at a configurable number of attempts before the contact is marked for recycling.
- When an AloAi summary arrives → post it in the timeline, mark it 'Needs review' for the owner, and offer a one-tap follow-up task pre-filled with the extracted next step.
- When the contact replies by SMS, answers a call, or books a meeting → pause every active sequence, clear the cold state, and notify the owner immediately.
- When an inbound STOP is received → revoke SMS consent, remove the contact from all sequences and Power Dialer lists, and show a permanent red banner on the record.
- When a suppression check returns a DNC hit → disable the calling action, tell the seller why, and keep the record workable only on channels that remain consented.
- When no activity of any kind occurs for 7 days (configurable) → mark the contact cold, badge it on the kanban card, and create a recycle task for the owner.
- When a seller tries to move an opportunity into a Closed-Won stage while annual premium, state or date of birth are missing → block the move and deep-link to the exact missing field on the contact.
- When a date of birth is entered or an age band is crossed → recompute age, flag any quote older than the birthday as stale, and notify the owner.
- When an opportunity is won → unlock the post-sale field group, and two days before the recorded draft date create a 'Confirm first draft' task for the owner.
- When a manual creation matches an existing phone or email in the same book → show the existing record inline before saving; if the match is in another seller's book, create the contact and raise a cross-owner duplicate visible only to the supervisor.
- When an import finishes → raise duplicate candidates instead of creating duplicates, and post one 'Imported from <list name>' entry per contact.
- When a supervisor reassigns a contact → move open opportunities, tasks and future meetings to the new owner, leave won-deal Earnings attributed to the original closer, and write the transfer into the timeline.
- When a seller is deactivated → flag their whole book for reassignment and notify admins; the leads stay invisible to other sellers until an explicit reassignment.
- When a contact is anonymized under CCPA → scrub identity and endpoints, retain de-identified timeline counts, and leave every historical Earnings and leaderboard figure unchanged.

## KPIs
- Auto-log coverage: % of Aloware calls that land on a contact timeline automatically (target >95%) — its inverse is the data-rot rate.
- Enrichment completeness: % of logged calls that end up with recording + transcript + AloAi summary attached, and median lag from call.completed to summary_ready.
- Median time from contact.created to first outbound attempt (speed-to-lead), and % of purchased leads breaching the 5-minute threshold.
- % of contacts with a valid, non-expired TCPA consent on at least one channel, and % with a stored vendor consent certificate.
- Blocked-attempt count: actions prevented by the consent/DNC gate (proof the gate is working) and opt-out rate per 1,000 messages sent.
- Insurance profile completeness at quoting (DOB + state + tobacco present) and at Closed-Won (annual premium present) — a Closed-Won with a blank premium is a leaderboard defect.
- Duplicate rate: candidates raised per 100 contacts created, median age of the merge queue, and cross-owner duplicate count (money the agency spent twice).
- Contacts going cold per seller per week and reheat rate after the recycling task.
- Average touches (calls + texts + emails) per contact before Closed-Won vs before Closed-Lost — the number that tells a supervisor whether a seller quits too early.
- Record freshness: % of active contacts with last_activity_at within 7 days, per seller.
- Mobile share of note and activity creation — a proxy for whether the record stays true away from the desk.

## Insurance specifics (Life / FE / IUL) & vertical-agnostic core
Genuinely Life/FE/IUL-specific here: (1) date of birth is not decoration — it computes age and the FE rate band, and crossing a birthday invalidates an outstanding quote, so age is a live computed attribute with its own change signal; (2) state drives both carrier licensing and state-level DNC, so it is a first-class field, not part of an address blob; (3) health knockout flags (dialysis, oxygen, CHF, recent cancer, stroke), tobacco use and height/weight decide whether a level, graded or guaranteed-issue product is quotable at all — capturing them on the qualification call is what avoids a wasted application; (4) beneficiaries with allocations summing to 100 and existing in-force coverage with a replacement-intent flag, because 'already insured' is one of our typified loss reasons and replacement carries a disclosure obligation; (5) the monthly bank draft date plus a bank-on-file flag (flag only — no bank or card numbers stored here), because a failed first draft in Final Expense means a commission chargeback, making the pre-draft reminder a direct earnings-protection feature; (6) IUL-specific parameters (funding intent, target premium, illustration date, annual review) that live in a different pack from FE; (7) TCPA consent modeled as evidence rather than a checkbox — vendor certificate (TrustedForm/Jornaya), capture timestamp and IP, or a pointer to the recorded call where verbal consent was given — because purchased life leads are exactly the scenario TCPA litigation targets. How the core stays vertical-agnostic: none of the above is a column. The contact table holds only identity, ownership, channel endpoints, activity clocks and compliance state — all true for any vertical. Every insurance attribute is a typed field definition inside a versioned field pack (life_fe_v1, life_iul_v1) that an admin installs, with declarative validation, layout order, per-stage required rules and its own timeline renderer for domain events. Adding Medicare or P&C later is installing a pack, not migrating a schema. Likewise the timeline is channel- and type-generic (entry_type + channel + source_module + source_ref_id), so a new domain event type or a new channel — WhatsApp is already reserved in the endpoint and consent enums — renders without touching the contact model.

## ⚔️ Adversarial review (sales-ops critic)

**Verdict:** NEEDS WORK — the strongest and most domain-aware module in the set, but four load-bearing defects: the premium unit is wrong for FE (monthly sold, annual stored, no mode field) and will corrupt the public all-time leaderboard by 12x; the compliance gate omits the two real US gates (local calling window, two-party recording consent) plus state licensing; 'touch' is never defined so automated sequence SMS and stage drags keep every record permanently warm and the cold rule never fires; and the record ends at Closed-Won with no policy/draft/chargeback lifecycle, which is precisely the economics an agency owner buys on.

### Must fix — missing for real operation (23)
- THE MONEY UNIT IS WRONG. Final Expense is sold and quoted MONTHLY ($47/mo), and deal_value is ANNUAL premium. There is no premium_mode field, no monthly input, no auto-annualization and no sanity guard. Sellers will type 47 into an annual field and the PUBLIC all-time leaderboard will be wrong by 12x, permanently, with no way to tell a typo from a small case. Required: premium_amount + premium_mode (monthly/quarterly/semi/annual) + computed annual_premium displayed side by side + a range guard (FE annual premium is typically ~$300-$2,400).
- IUL premium semantics are undefined. 'Annual premium' on an IUL can mean minimum, target, maximum non-MEC, or excess funding, and 1035 exchanges and single-premium cases exist. One field for FE and IUL on an all-time public board means one IUL case outranks twenty FE sales — exactly the fairness collapse research item 18 warned about, originating in THIS module's data model.
- No TCPA calling-window gate. Federal restriction is 8am-9pm in the CALLED PARTY's local time, several states restrict Sundays/holidays. The contact has a timezone field and nothing uses it. The action bar must show the lead's LOCAL TIME next to the Call button and hard-block outside the window — a national floor calling a 6am Pacific senior from an East Coast desk is a real, routine, four-figure exposure.
- No two-party recording-consent handling. We store, play and transcribe recordings with zero per-state disclosure state. CA/FL/PA/IL/WA/MA and others require all-party consent; the record must carry recording_consent_required (derived from state) and the disclosure moment must be evidenced. We are building a recording archive with no legal frame around it.
- No seller state-licensing / carrier-appointment gate. The spec offers a 'licensing hint'. A seller cannot legally sell in a state they aren't licensed in — that is a hard block at the top of the record, plus it is the ONLY legitimate business reason a lead should ever move between books, and the spec never connects the two.
- No attempt ledger: attempt_count, attempts by day-part, attempt cap, and a day-part rotation rule. FE floors work a purchased lead 6-8 times across mornings/afternoons/evenings over ~2 weeks. A 7-day inactivity timer is not a work plan.
- No structured callback window / do-not-call-before-X field. It exists only as a pinned note, so the Power Dialer list builder and every sequence will happily dial a night-shift worker at 10am.
- No language field. Spanish-language FE is a large share of this market. It determines script, sequence, and whether the owning seller can work the lead at all. Buried as a tag example.
- No post-sale policy lifecycle. Contact record ends at Closed-Won. Missing: carrier, product, face amount, underwriting class (level/graded/modified/GI), policy number, application status (submitted → UW → approved → issued → NTO → active → lapsed), and the chargeback window. In FE, Closed-Won is not money — placement is roughly 60-75% and the leaderboard we make public is computed off the optimistic end of that.
- Draft date exists but with no Social Security alignment, and it is marked non-MVP while three automations depend on it. FE clients are seniors on fixed income: SS pays 2nd/3rd/4th Wednesday by birth date, SSI on the 1st. Setting a draft date that misses the deposit is the single most common cause of a failed first draft. The field needs an SS-schedule helper, not a bare date picker.
- No NSF / failed-draft handling: no consumed event, no task, no timeline entry, no banner. First-year chargebacks are the agency owner's biggest leak and the CRM is blind to them.
- No household / contact-to-contact relationship (spouse, adult child as payer or responsible party). 'Multiple opportunities per contact' covers FE→IUL on one person but not the spouse sale, which is the cheapest second policy on the floor.
- SSN is completely unaddressed. Agents collect it for the carrier e-application. The spec carefully forbids card/bank numbers and says nothing about SSN — silence means it lands in a note field in plaintext. Decide explicitly: prohibited (collect in the carrier portal only, with a UI warning on notes) or encrypted, masked and access-logged.
- Health data (medications, height/weight, dialysis/oxygen/CHF/cancer flags) is 'sensitive personal information' under CPRA with its own limited-use and retention obligations. The spec cites CCPA generically and specifies no field-level encryption, no retention/purge policy, and no limited-use statement.
- No voicemail drop and no SMS quick-reply templates in the action bar. FE dialing is overwhelmingly voicemail; if a one-tap drop and 6 canned texts aren't in our record, the seller works out of Aloware and our timeline is a shadow copy at best.
- No lead cost at intake. lead_vendor is stored but not cost_per_lead, so vendor ROI, cost per issued policy, and 'stop buying from this vendor' are all uncomputable. This is the number that makes an agency owner renew.
- No fresh vs aged lead distinction. Purchased aged FE lists are real inventory with completely different cadence economics; the speed-to-lead countdown and the cold clock both misbehave on them.
- Speed-to-lead is implemented as a countdown BANNER on a record the seller is not looking at, with a 5-minute threshold. Ping-post life leads decay in seconds. It needs: push notification, automatic placement at the head of the owner's Power Dialer queue, optional auto two-legged dial on arrival, and a threshold expressed in seconds.
- 'Touch' is never defined. last_activity_at is updated by outbound sequence SMS, stage changes, task completion and system entries. A sequence texting every 6 days keeps a dead lead permanently warm and contact.went_cold never fires — which silently disables the cold rule, the recycle workflow, the kanban rot badge and the My Day list. Need counts_as_touch / is_human_touch on timeline_entry, with cold computed only from human outbound + any inbound.
- Nothing here feeds an activity-based recognition metric (dials, talk time, appointments set, first-attempt speed) to the leaderboard. Those counters live in THIS module. Without exposing them, the all-time revenue-only board demotes the bottom 60-70% of the floor exactly as our own research said it would, and the 'Most Improved' mitigation has no data source.
- contact_consent has expires_at with no sweeper and no event. Consent silently expires and either the gate keeps letting calls through or the seller finds out mid-dial.
- The unmatched-call quarantine has no owner scoping and no 'create a contact from this call' path. Inbound calls from unknown numbers are how FE callbacks actually arrive.
- The lead's local time is never displayed anywhere. On a national floor that is a per-call decision input, not a nice-to-have.

### Too generic / boilerplate (10)
- The 'Unified per-contact timeline' as written is the same timeline every CRM ships. An FE floor doesn't read a thread, it works an ATTEMPT MATRIX. It should be an attempt ledger with a timeline attached: 'Attempt 4 of 8 — last tried Tue 6:40pm local, no answer', attempts bucketed by day-part, Aloware disposition taxonomy as first-class, and best-time-to-call learned from answered dials. Reverse-chronological cards alone don't tell the seller what to do next.
- The 'Tags with a governed tag library' feature is doing work that domain fields should do, and it contradicts itself: it justifies tags as ad-hoc seller segmentation ('veteran', 'Spanish', 'callback Saturday') then makes the library admin-curated so sellers can't create them. Replace the three examples with real fields — language (ES/EN), callback_window, lead_vendor, day_part_preference — and let sellers create free tags with an admin merge/cleanup tool. As specified this is the 'simple to use, brutal to administer' anti-pattern we named.
- 'Custom field engine with vertical field packs' is a blank-canvas data model wearing a vertical costume. Nothing in it is Life/FE/IUL. Anchor it: ship Life-FE-v1 and Life-IUL-v1 as SEEDED, versioned packs with the hot fields promoted to typed/generated columns (dob, computed_age, state, tobacco, language, draft_date) so the book view, the cold sweeper and the age-band automation are not filtering JSON. The admin field EDITOR is not MVP; the packs are.
- 'My Book of Business with saved views, column chooser and shared views' is generic table chrome. A life seller does not build views, they work five lists: New/uncalled, Callbacks due today, No-answer ladder (attempt 2-8), Cold/recycle, Clients on persistency watch. Ship those five as seeded system lists; make user-authored views post-MVP.
- 'Notes with pinning and @mentions' is CRM boilerplate. The facts being pinned ('do not call before 6pm, works nights') are STRUCTURED data that should drive Power Dialer list building and the calling-window gate — as free text they are invisible to every automation. And the real escalation on a life floor is not a mention, it is 'get my supervisor on a three-way close', which this doesn't model.
- 'Record completeness meter' is a generic progress ring. It must be two named gates, not a percentage: QUOTABLE (DOB + state + tobacco + health knockouts + licensed-in-state) and CLOSEABLE (premium + mode + carrier + product + face amount). A percentage tells the seller nothing; 'you cannot quote this person until you have their state' does.
- The KPI list is analyst-shaped and skips the two numbers an FE agency owner actually runs on: contact rate and close rate BY LEAD VENDOR, and cost per ISSUED policy. Neither is computable because the contact stores lead_vendor but no lead cost and no issued-policy state.
- The 'Insurance profile panel' is the only genuinely vertical feature and it is still just labelled custom fields: a list of inputs with no logic. No carrier-specific knockout evaluation, no level/graded/modified/GI band output, no 'this combination is decline everywhere' signal. Without an output it is a notebook the seller must interpret themselves.
- The 'Consent-aware action bar' describes generic channel gating and misses the two gates that actually exist in US telesales: the 8am-9pm LOCAL-TIME calling window (the contact even has a timezone field that nothing reads) and two-party recording-consent states. As written the gate is a TCPA-consent checkbox, which is the easy third of the problem.
- Empty/loading/error state catalogue is well written but drifts into design-system filler in places ('Counting…', shimmer rows, skeleton counts). Every state should name the seller consequence the way the speed-to-lead empty state does.

### Bloat to cut (14)
- Jump-to-date rail on the timeline. Nothing breaks without it; filters plus scroll cover the real need.
- Transcript keyword search, click-to-seek and playback speed control. V1 is: play the recording, read the transcript. Search-in-transcript is a coaching feature, not a selling feature.
- Saved views with column chooser and is_shared_with_role. Replace with five seeded system lists. User-authored views are post-MVP and the shared-view flag is also a silo risk (see violations).
- The full Field & Tag Library admin UI — pack authoring, drag-reorder layout preview, per-field visibility rules. Seed the FE and IUL packs; the editor is high complexity delivering nothing on day one for a 50-seat single-tenant-in-practice deployment.
- 'Reserved channel slot for WhatsApp' as a FEATURE row. It is a schema decision inside contact_channel_endpoint and contact_consent. Keep the decision, delete the feature — it has no UI, no behavior, and nothing breaks without it because it isn't anything.
- CCPA subject-request workflow as a product screen. Expected volume for 50 sellers is near zero. An admin runbook plus the export and anonymization primitives already specified covers the obligation; a built flow does not.
- The merge preview 'union of timelines, opportunities and tasks'. Keep the transaction, the audit and the reversible window — cut the expensive side-by-side visual union down to conflict fields plus counts ('merging brings 214 timeline entries, 1 opportunity, 3 tasks').
- @mention notifications carrying body_preview. Cut the preview outright (it is a leak), and if mentions survive at all restrict them to seller→supervisor only.
- Voice dictation on mobile notes as a build item. Every mobile OS keyboard already does this; just don't block the field.
- The duplicate match SCORING model (name+DOB+state fuzzy scoring with a score). V1 should be exact normalized phone / lowercased email only. Fuzzy scoring generates a review queue nobody drains.
- Watermarked exports as a build. Logging plus a rate limit is the control that actually detects a departing seller; the watermark is theater.
- 'Needs review' as a concept distinct from unread inbound. Collapse into one unread state — two overlapping unread systems on the same thread will confuse the seller and neither will be trusted.
- Pinned-notes max-3 rule and the note edit version trail. Over-engineered for the value; a note is a note.
- Timeline PDF export. CSV/JSON for v1 — the recipient is a compliance officer or a carrier, both of whom accept structured data.

### Event gaps (18)
- call.completed is the ONLY call event consumed, so the headline 'sub-second Call now' has nothing to render and no correlation. Missing call.initiated / call.ringing / call.answered, and missing a correlation id returned by the Two-Legged Call API. Matching a webhook to a click by normalized phone alone is ambiguous the moment two sellers dial the same purchased lead — which is the exact scenario ping-post guarantees.
- Out-of-order webhook delivery is not handled. call.summary_ready or call.recording_ready can arrive before call.completed. The contract says 'patches the existing call entry, never creates a second entry' — with no entry to patch, the enrichment is dropped. Needs an upsert-by-provider-call-id placeholder rule, not just idempotency.
- No sms.opt_in_received / START / UNSTOP consumer, while permissions state only an admin may clear an opt-out. A contact who texts START is re-consented at the carrier and at Aloware but stays hard-blocked in our UI forever. The event surface and the permission model directly contradict each other.
- No contact.consent_expired event and no producer for it, despite contact_consent.expires_at existing and the action bar claiming to fail closed.
- No contact.endpoint_undeliverable event. sms.delivery_failed and email.bounced flag deliverable_status internally and emit nothing, so sequences, Power Dialer lists and the book view keep burning attempts on a disconnected number.
- No policy or persistency events whatsoever: policy.submitted, policy.issued, policy.not_taken_out, draft.failed, policy.lapsed, commission.charged_back. opportunity.won is treated as the end of the story, which is the FE-specific mistake this module of all modules should not make.
- contact.speed_to_lead_breached has no positive counterpart. Nothing emits first-touch success (contact.first_touch_recorded with elapsed_seconds), so coaching, the speed-to-lead KPI and any activity-based recognition category can only see failures.
- contact.archived has no contact.unarchived / contact.restored pair, even though archiving is described as reversible.
- contact.dnc_flagged is EMITTED by this module but suppression originates in Aloware and no suppression event is CONSUMED anywhere. The automation 'when a suppression check returns a DNC hit' has no source event and no scheduler behind it — the trigger is invented.
- contact.went_cold and contact.speed_to_lead_breached are elapsed-time events with no declared producer. Nothing in the spec runs the sweeper or the timer, and the infra budget (<$100/mo) makes the mechanism a real design decision, not an implementation detail.
- contact.exported carries scope and record_count but no record identifiers. It therefore cannot answer the one question it exists for: what left with the departing seller.
- contact.note_added ships body_preview and mentions to the notifications module. A note body about one seller's lead can reach a non-owner through a notification. Emit note_id + contact_id only and authorize at render time.
- Module-id vocabulary drifts: 'admin-audit', 'dashboards-my-day' and 'search-index' appear in consumer lists alongside 'reporting-analytics' and 'notifications'. Pick one canonical module vocabulary or half these subscriptions are wired to nothing.
- lead.created and contact.created are two births for one record with no stated ordering or idempotency contract, and the reaction says owner_user_id is 'set from the intake rule' — a mechanism that is nowhere defined and is the closest thing to a routing engine in the entire product (see silo violations).
- contact.merged does not state what happens to consent and suppression on the surviving record. Without an explicit strictest-state-wins rule in the contract, merging a consented record with an opted-out one is a consent-laundering path.
- Only timeline_entry declares an idempotency key. Every consumed Aloware webhook retries; each consumer reaction needs a dedup key, or a retried sms.opt_out_received re-emits contact.opted_out and re-fires downstream automations.
- No opportunity.reopened consumer, and moving an opportunity back out of Closed-Won is not handled. contact_status stays 'client', the post-sale field group stays unlocked, and the pre-draft reminder task survives a deal that no longer exists.
- contact.timeline_entry_added has no is_human_touch / counts_as_touch flag. Every downstream consumer (cold detection, My Day, board rot badge, activity metrics) will re-derive 'what counts as a touch' independently and disagree — the exact failure the last-activity engine feature was written to prevent.

### 🚨 Silo violations (10)
- Owner reassignment transfers the ENTIRE timeline — including the previous seller's call recordings, transcripts and private notes — into the new owner's readable book. That is a seller reading a colleague's work product, delivered by a supervisor action. Rule needed: the record and forward activity move; prior-owner recordings and notes become supervisor-only or are reduced to de-identified counts.
- 'Supervisor bulk actions execute as the owning seller for attribution.' That is impersonation writing into a silo. It inflates that seller's activity counters, which feed a PUBLIC leaderboard, and it blurs who is liable for a TCPA-relevant send. Attribute to the actor with an explicit on-behalf-of marker, and exclude on-behalf-of activity from any ranked metric.
- contact.note_added emits body_preview plus a mentions array, and nothing in the spec forbids a seller from @mentioning another SELLER. That is a direct channel for one seller's lead content to reach a non-owner. Restrict mentions to supervisors/admins and strip the preview from the event.
- saved_view.is_shared_with_role lets a supervisor publish a view to sellers. Unless every seller-scope query force-injects owner_user_id = current user AFTER the view filter, a shared view silently widens scope. No such invariant is stated anywhere in the spec.
- The unmatched-call quarantine has no owner scoping rule. An inbound call that actually belongs to another seller's lead, or a shared quarantine list, leaks the existence of leads across books. Quarantine must be partitioned per Aloware line / per seller, and resolution must be owner-scoped.
- Duplicate-safe manual creation hides cross-owner matches (correct) but the same-owner and cross-owner paths behave differently from the seller's point of view — one shows an inline match, the other saves silently and flags. The divergence is an observable side channel: a seller can infer a teammate holds the lead. Make both paths visually identical to the seller.
- 'search-index' is listed as a consumer of contact.created with phone_primary_normalized in the payload, with no stated partition or scoping key. A global search index is the classic accidental silo breach — one query and a seller finds everyone's leads.
- The speed-to-lead automation says 'the supervisor sees the aggregate, never the lead', while the permissions table gives supervisors full visibility of every contact. Directly contradictory. Ambiguity in the visibility contract is how a silo model rots in implementation.
- The 'intake rule' that sets owner_user_id on lead.created is undefined and is the only place a routing engine could enter the product. It must be declared explicitly as a static vendor/source/campaign → seller ownership mapping, with a written statement that there is no queue, no round-robin, no idle-reassignment and no supervisor pull.
- Cross-owner duplicate resolution 'executed as owner change + merge' merges two sellers' timelines into one book. Same exposure as the reassignment case, plus one seller's closed-won history attaches to another seller's record — which makes a PUBLIC all-time leaderboard contestable. Needs an explicit rule: won opportunities and Earnings stay attributed to the original closer, and the losing record's recordings/notes do not become readable by the surviving owner.

## Risks & dependencies
- Hard dependency on Aloware webhook reliability and ordering: call.completed can arrive after call.recording_ready, or twice. The timeline must be idempotent on the provider call id and tolerate out-of-order enrichment, or sellers see duplicate and headless call entries and stop trusting the record.
- Phone-number matching is the weak joint of auto-logging: extensions, +1 formatting, shared household numbers, and the same number in two books. Requires strict E.164 normalization, an unmatched-call quarantine, and a documented rule for whose timeline an ambiguous call lands on (proposal: the Aloware agent identity on the call decides, not the number).
- We are not an Aloware native partner — there is no embedded softphone. Click-to-call depends on the server-side Two-Legged Call API or the Aloware Talk Chrome extension being installed per seller: a per-machine setup dependency and a real mobile gap that must be designed around, not assumed away.
- Recording and transcript storage/egress is the main cost risk against the <$100/month infra target if we mirror media instead of deep-linking to Aloware. Default position: store references and stream from Aloware; mirror only if a retention policy forces it.
- Consent must fail closed. If the consent service is unavailable, action buttons stay disabled — this will occasionally block a legitimate call and sellers will complain. The alternative is TCPA exposure. Needs an explicit written product decision and clear microcopy.
- Cross-owner duplicate handling is the sharpest tension with the silo: the agency needs to know it bought the same lead twice while sellers must never see each other's books. Modeled as supervisor-only visibility; if the client later wants seller-visible duplicate warnings, the silo promise weakens and must be re-decided.
- EAV custom fields are flexible but hostile to filtering and reporting at scale. Mitigation: promote high-traffic pack fields (dob/age, state, tobacco, draft_date) to indexed generated columns while keeping the definition-driven UI.
- Boundary risk with lead-intake (who detects duplicates), telephony (who owns call artifacts) and pipeline (who owns the cold/rot rule). Proposal: intake detects, contacts-360 resolves and merges; telephony owns artifacts, contacts-360 owns the referencing timeline entry; contacts-360 owns contact-level contact.went_cold while pipeline owns opportunity-level rotting, both reading contact.last_activity_updated as the single source of truth. An auditor must confirm no other module emits these.
- CUT — third-party data enrichment/append (social, firmographic): no verified value for FE/IUL, per-record cost against the infra budget, and nothing breaks without it.
- CUT — AI predictive lead scoring: with per-seller silos and no routing engine there is no action to take on a score; it would be a number nobody uses. Revisit only if routing is ever introduced.
- CUT — household/relationship graph (spouse, adult child): genuinely useful for FE cross-sell but it multiplies the permission model (whose book owns the household?) and nothing breaks today without it. Deferred, not deleted; multiple opportunities per contact covers most cross-sell cases now.
- CUT — in-app document storage and e-signature: applications are submitted in carrier e-app portals; duplicating them creates a PII surface and a compliance obligation for zero added conversion. We store a link and the policy number instead.
- CUT — free-form conditional logic builder for field visibility: the blank-canvas anti-pattern. Field packs with declarative required/visible rules cover the real cases.
- Open dependency on the leaderboard/earnings module for the reassignment attribution rule: if won-deal Earnings do not stay with the original closer on contact.owner_changed, the public leaderboard becomes contestable and the motivational effect inverts.

## Open questions
- Event-ownership audit: is contacts-360 the sole emitter of contact.created (with lead-intake emitting lead.created as the preceding channel event), or should intake-sourced contacts skip contact.created? Duplicated emission would double-fire every downstream automation.
- Cold/rot ownership: contacts-360 emits contact.went_cold at the person level while pipeline emits opportunity-level rotting. Confirm both exist deliberately and share one threshold configuration, or collapse to one.
- Whose timeline receives an Aloware call when the same phone number exists in two sellers' books? Proposed rule: the Aloware agent identity on the call decides. Needs client confirmation.
- Should supervisors be able to EDIT a seller's contact fields, or only view and comment? Editing helps data hygiene but blurs ownership; this spec allows edit with full attribution.
- Retention policy for call recordings and transcripts: how long, mirrored or referenced, and who may play them after a seller leaves the agency.
- Verbal-consent capture: is a pointer to the Aloware recording sufficient evidence for this agency's carriers, or is a separate structured consent script and attestation required?
- Does the client want a seller state-licensing check surfaced on the record (can this seller legally write in this contact's state?), or is that handled outside the system?
- Reversible-merge window length (proposed 30 days), and whether a merge may ever be reverted after the surviving record has been worked.
- Do we need per-field visibility rules for sensitive health data (e.g. hiding medications from supervisors), or is role-level access sufficient under this agency's HIPAA/ITC posture?
- Timeline volume: a three-year book with heavy dialing could exceed hundreds of entries per contact and millions per tenant — confirm expected dials per seller per day (OQ-2) before fixing the pagination and retention strategy.
