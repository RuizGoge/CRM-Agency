# 6. Conversations — Aloware-powered Calls, SMS & Email

> **Module id:** `conversations` · part of [`docs/02-functional-map.md`](../02-functional-map.md)

## Verdict
KEEP as-is, and treat it as the single highest-leverage integration module in the product — with one reshape: this module is NOT "an inbox". It is (a) an **execution layer** (place a call, send an SMS/email through the right identity), (b) an **ingestion layer** (Aloware webhooks auto-writing calls/SMS into the record), and (c) a **compliance gate** (TCPA consent, STOP/opt-out, DNC, quiet hours, A2P 10DLC reality) that every other module must route sends through. Two boundary corrections: (1) the *unified per-lead timeline* is a shared surface owned by the lead/contact module — Conversations owns the comms half and feeds it via events, it does not own the timeline widget; (2) sequence/cadence *definitions and triggers* are owned by the automations module — Conversations is the executor and the source of the "lead replied → pause" signal, and only mirrors Aloware-side sequence enrollments. Merging Conversations into Leads would be wrong: the compliance ledger and the webhook ingestion pipeline have their own lifecycle, their own failure modes, and their own admin surface. Nothing here should be cut; several tempting sub-features were cut instead (see risks_dependencies and open_questions).

## Purpose & value
Every call, text and email with your lead lands in one thread by itself — you press "Call now" the second a lead arrives, and the disposition, recording, transcript and AI summary write themselves into the record — while the system silently refuses to text or dial anyone who has not consented, has opted out, or is on a DNC list.

## Screens & states (9)

### Conversation thread (per lead)
The single place a seller reads and continues the relationship: all calls, SMS, email and comms system events in one chronological, filterable thread with the composer and Call now always in reach.

**Key actions:** `Call now (two-legged) / click-to-call` · `Send SMS` · `Send email` · `Insert template or snippet` · `Play recording, read transcript, expand AI summary` · `Log a call made off-platform` · `Schedule send / send later` · `Set disposition and next step` · `Enroll or pause a sequence` · `Filter by channel type`

- **Empty:** Title: "No conversation yet." Body: "Bought leads go cold in minutes — the first call is the one that converts." Primary button: "Call now". Secondary: "Send first text". Helper line under the buttons: "Consent on file: express written, captured 07/30/2026 by <vendor>. Calls and texts allowed." If consent is missing, the buttons are disabled and the helper reads: "No TCPA consent on file for this lead. Add the consent record before calling or texting." with a link "Add consent record".
- **Loading:** Thread skeleton of three message rows plus a disabled composer; the Call now button stays visible and enabled as soon as the lead's consent state resolves, so speed-to-lead is never blocked by history loading.
- **Error:** Inline banner: "We couldn't load this conversation. Your messages are safe — nothing was lost." with "Try again". If Aloware is degraded: "Calling and texting are unavailable right now (Aloware connection issue). You can still log a call you made yourself." and the composer switches to manual-log mode.
- **No permission:** Full-panel message: "This lead belongs to another seller. Only its owner, supervisors and admins can open this conversation." No lead name, no phone number, no message preview is rendered.
- **Mobile:** Single column, sticky bottom bar with Call now + message field; recordings play inline; transcripts collapse behind "Read transcript"; the consent badge stays pinned to the header so it is never scrolled out of view.

### My Conversations (inbox)
A silo-scoped worklist of the seller's own threads that need a reply, oldest unanswered first, so inbound never rots.

**Key actions:** `Open thread` · `Quick reply inline` · `Mark as handled` · `Filter: needs reply / unread / missed calls / blocked sends` · `Search my conversations`

- **Empty:** Title: "You're all caught up." Body: "Replies from your leads land here — texts, emails and missed calls. Nothing is waiting on you right now." Secondary button: "Go to My Day". For a brand-new seller with no leads at all: "No conversations yet. Your first text or call to a lead starts a thread here." with button "Open my pipeline".
- **Loading:** Five shimmer rows preserving channel-icon and timestamp positions; filter chips remain interactive.
- **Error:** "We couldn't load your inbox." with "Retry" and a note: "Incoming messages are still being received and will appear once this loads."
- **No permission:** Not reachable — every authenticated seller has their own inbox. Supervisors landing here see their own (usually empty) inbox with a link: "Looking for your team? Open Team conversations."
- **Mobile:** This is the mobile home screen for comms; pull-to-refresh, swipe-to-quick-reply, push notification deep-links straight into the thread.

### Call detail (recording, transcript, AI summary)
Everything a single call produced, in one panel: outcome, duration, recording, transcript with search, AloAi summary, and the note derived from it.

**Key actions:** `Play / scrub recording` · `Search within transcript` · `Copy summary into the note` · `Change disposition (with reason logged)` · `Create follow-up activity` · `Download recording (permission-gated)`

- **Empty:** When a call completed but media has not arrived yet: "Call logged. The recording, transcript and AI summary usually arrive within a few minutes — we'll add them here automatically." No action required from the user.
- **Loading:** Waveform placeholder and greyed transcript lines; the disposition and duration render immediately because they arrive on the first webhook.
- **Error:** "The recording for this call isn't available." plus the specific cause when we know it: "Recording was disabled for this call" / "Recording has passed the retention period (90 days)" / "We couldn't reach Aloware for the media file."
- **No permission:** "You can see that this call happened, but recordings and transcripts for other sellers' leads are restricted." Metadata (date, duration, outcome) remains visible to supervisors and admins only.
- **Mobile:** Recording plays through the OS media session so it survives screen lock; transcript in a bottom sheet.

## Typical automations
- When a lead is created and consent is on file -> prime "Call now" at the top of the seller's My Day and start the speed-to-lead timer.
- When a lead is created and NO consent is on file -> block calling and texting on that lead, badge it, and notify the seller to add the consent record before working it.
- When a call ends with no answer -> send the "Missed you" SMS template if SMS consent allows, and create a callback task for the next business morning.
- When a call ends with voicemail left -> log it, attach the AI summary when it arrives, and schedule the second attempt for a different hour of day.
- When an inbound call is missed -> push-notify the owning seller on mobile and auto text back within 60 seconds (compliance-gated, quiet-hours aware).
- When a lead replies on any channel -> pause every active sequence for that lead, mark the thread needs-reply, and notify the owning seller.
- When an appointment is scheduled -> send the confirmation SMS and email, then reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before, in the lead's local time.
- When an appointment is no-showed -> send the reschedule template and reopen a call task on the thread.
- When an opportunity moves into a Closed-Won stage -> send the policy-delivery/welcome email template and disenroll the lead from every cadence.
- When an opportunity moves into a Closed-Lost stage -> cancel all scheduled sends and reminders for that lead, keeping the thread intact for future recycling.
- When a lead texts STOP -> hard-block SMS across all of that lead's numbers, record the revocation, kill active cadences, and notify the seller in the thread itself.
- When an AloAi call summary arrives -> attach it to the call entry and prefill the seller's call note.
- When a lead has had no activity for 7 days (configurable) -> add them to the seller's suggested Power Dialer recycling list — surfaced, never auto-dialed.
- When a send falls outside quiet hours in the lead's timezone -> hold it and release automatically at 8:00 AM local, re-checking consent at release.
- When an email hard-bounces -> mark the email channel unusable for that lead and prompt the seller to confirm the address on the next call.
- When webhook lag exceeds the threshold or the dead-letter queue grows -> flag the integration as degraded, notify admins, and switch the call button to manual-log mode.
- When a seller's Aloware link expires -> disable their send buttons with an explicit reconnect prompt instead of failing sends silently.

## KPIs
- Median speed-to-lead: seconds from lead.created to the first call.initiated (target: under 120 seconds; this is the #1 conversion lever for purchased FE/IUL leads)
- Auto-log coverage: share of calls written by webhook versus manual entry (target above 95% — the direct measure of whether CRM data rot is beaten)
- Call connect rate: call.connected / call.initiated, by seller and by hour-of-day
- Contact rate per lead: share of leads reaching at least one connected conversation within 72 hours
- Attempts to first contact: median dial+text attempts before a live conversation
- SMS delivery rate and SMS reply rate, by template
- Opt-out rate per 1,000 SMS sent, by seller and by template (a rising number here is an early warning of both compliance and message-quality problems)
- Consent coverage: share of active leads with a valid, provable consent record per channel (target 100% before any outbound)
- Blocked-send volume by reason (no_consent / opted_out / dnc / quiet_hours / a2p_not_registered / identity_unhealthy)
- First-response time on inbound: median minutes from conversation.reply_received to the seller's next outbound
- Unanswered inbound backlog: threads with needs_reply older than 4 business hours
- Webhook freshness: p95 seconds from call end to the call appearing in the thread; dead-letter count
- Media completeness: share of connected calls with a recording, transcript and AI summary attached
- Power Dialer productivity: contacts reached per session and per hour
- Comms cost per closed-won opportunity (call minutes + SMS segments in USD)
- Seller connection health: number of sellers with an unlinked or unhealthy channel identity (should always be zero)

## Insurance specifics (Life / FE / IUL) & vertical-agnostic core
Genuinely Life/FE/IUL-specific here: (1) **Purchased-lead consent provenance.** FE and IUL leads arrive by ping-post from lead vendors, so consent is not something we collect — it is something we *inherit and must prove*. The consent_record carries vendor_name plus the vendor's consent certificate reference from the intake payload, because in a TCPA dispute the question is always "who obtained this consent, when, and for whom". (2) **Speed-to-lead as a compliance-constrained race.** The product must dial in seconds while still refusing to dial when consent is absent — an unusual pairing that generic CRMs get wrong in one direction or the other. (3) **Phone-first, senior demographic.** FE prospects skew elderly: calls and voicemails carry the relationship, email is often absent, chat widgets are useless, and MMS is sometimes the only way to get an illustration in front of someone — which is why the thread is call-centric and the mobile surface uses large type and a single obvious Call now. (4) **Recorded tele-sales.** FE applications are frequently taken and voice-signed on a recorded call, so recording retention and two-party-consent states (the recording is evidence, not just coaching material) are first-class, not an afterthought. (5) **Quiet hours across a national footprint.** ~50 sellers calling all 50 states means the lead's local time — not the seller's — governs legality. (6) **Licensing context.** The lead's state is surfaced on the thread because an agent must be licensed where the applicant resides; we display it, we do not enforce it (licensing tracking is out of this module's scope). (7) **Cadence asymmetry.** FE converts on speed and few touches; IUL is a longer consultative nurture — the same sequence engine must serve both, which is why cadence design is configuration, not code. How the CORE stays vertical-agnostic: the engine knows only *channel*, *identity*, *consent scope*, *jurisdiction*, *template*, and *outcome taxonomy* — never "policy", "premium", "carrier" or "underwriting". Consent types, quiet-hour windows, retention periods, disposition mappings, required-consent-per-channel rules and the template starter packs are all tenant/vertical configuration rows. Swapping to a mortgage, solar or auto vertical means new templates, a new disposition map and different consent settings — zero schema change and zero new code in the send path.

## ⚔️ Adversarial review (sales-ops critic)

**Verdict:** NEEDS WORK — the compliance ledger, webhook reliability layer and silo-at-the-data-layer thinking are the strongest parts of this spec, but the module claims one universal compliance gate it cannot enforce on click-to-call or Power Dialer, fails closed on manual dials in a way that will push 50 sellers back to their personal cells, ships a telephony disposition taxonomy instead of a life-insurance one, and omits the four things an FE floor actually runs on daily — voicemail drop, caller-ID reputation/number rotation, lead-vendor performance attribution, and an instant new-lead ring — while over-building email, cost ledgers and template analytics.

### Must fix — missing for real operation (11)
- LEAD-VENDOR PERFORMANCE FEEDBACK LOOP — the biggest omission in the module. vendor_name exists only inside consent_record, as a legal field. On a bought-lead FE floor the owner's daily decision is which vendor/campaign to keep buying and which to shut off, and the seller's daily grievance is 'these leads are junk'. Conversations is where the evidence is generated (connect rate, wrong-number rate, 'never requested info' rate, talk time, application rate) and it emits none of it with vendor/campaign attribution. Add vendor_id + campaign_id to every call/message event, add a 'bad lead — request credit' disposition that produces an exportable vendor credit-request list. Without it the agency owner is buying blind and will keep his current spreadsheet.
- VOICEMAIL DROP. 60-70%+ of FE dials hit an answering machine. Aloware supports pre-recorded voicemail drop; the spec never mentions it. A seller leaving the same 20-second message 80 times a day by mouth is the difference between 200 and 320 dials. This is not a nice-to-have on a life floor, it is table stakes — and it must be gated (drop only on machine detection, consent-checked, logged as an outbound touch).
- CALLER-ID REPUTATION AND NUMBER ROTATION. channel_identity carries exactly ONE from_number per seller. Real US life telesales runs local-presence number pools per seller and monitors spam labeling — when a number gets tagged 'Spam Likely' the seller's answer rate halves overnight and nobody knows why for two weeks. Need: multiple from_numbers per seller with a selection rule (local presence to lead area code), per-number answer-rate tracking, a spam-flag/reputation surface in the admin console, and a one-click 'retire and replace this number'. This is a top-3 operational pain and it is completely absent.
- AGENT STATE LICENSING / APPOINTMENT GUARD. A US life agent may only solicit in states where they are licensed and carrier-appointed. Nothing in the model knows which states a seller can write. Sellers will burn dials and, worse, present and sell in a state they cannot write — an E&O and DOI issue, not just wasted time. Add licensed_states to the user profile and surface it in the composer/Call now as a warning (not a hard block — quoting vs. soliciting is a judgment call), plus a Power Dialer pre-flight filter.
- CALL-ATTEMPT CADENCE AND FREQUENCY CAPS. KPI 'attempts to first contact' exists but nothing counts attempts. There is no attempt counter per lead per number, no day-part rotation policy object (the 'schedule the second attempt for a different hour' automation has nothing to read), no per-day/per-week contact frequency cap. Several states cap call frequency and repeated dialing is the standard harassment allegation. Add contact_attempt tracking + a tenant attempt policy (max/day, max/week, day-part rotation, stop-after-N-with-no-contact → cold).
- MULTI-NUMBER LEADS. FE leads routinely arrive with 2-3 numbers (cell, landline, adult child). The thread is per-lead with an implied single contact value; the composer, Call now, and Power Dialer have no number selection, no per-number status (bad/wrong person/disconnected), and no 'try the other number' flow. Meanwhile opt_out and suppression ARE keyed by contact_value_hash — so the data model already knows numbers are the unit and the UI pretends they aren't.
- INBOUND VOICEMAIL. Outbound recordings, transcripts and AI summaries are covered in detail; the voicemail a lead LEAVES the seller is not ingested, transcribed, or threaded anywhere. call.missed carries a voicemail_left boolean and nothing else. On a silo model with no coverage queue, that voicemail IS the callback.
- 'RING THE SELLER ON NEW LEAD ARRIVAL'. The module claims speed-to-lead as its reason to exist, then delivers a 'primed Call now' and a counter — both of which require the seller to be staring at the screen. Ping-post FE leads decay in minutes and the seller is often mid-call or driving. Need an actual instant-dial bridge (Aloware two-legged: ring the seller, announce the new lead, bridge) plus a push notification, with a per-seller availability toggle. Without it, median speed-to-lead under 120 seconds will not be hit and the module's headline KPI fails.
- SALE CAPTURE AT THE MOMENT OF SALE. The stage-gating pattern requires annual premium before Closed-Won, and the call is when premium becomes known. There is no path from the call panel to 'application submitted, carrier X, annual premium $Y' → opportunity update → Earnings → leaderboard re-rank. Conversations produces the event that makes the leaderboard move and never says so.
- PER-STATE QUIET HOURS AND RECORDING DISCLOSURE. comms_policy has one tenant-wide 8am-9pm window; multiple states are stricter and some restrict Sundays/holidays. And two_party_consent_states is stored but no behavior is attached — no auto disclosure prompt, no per-state recording suppression. A stored flag with no enforcement is worse than nothing in a demand letter.
- SPANISH. A large share of the US FE market is Hispanic-market. message_template has no language field, there is no per-lead preferred_language driving template selection, and transcripts store language without using it. i18n-ready UI does not cover the actual selling language.

### Too generic / boilerplate (8)
- THE COMPLIANCE GATE IS A LIE FOR 2 OF 4 DIAL PATHS. The spec claims 'Every outbound send — manual, sequence-driven, or automation-driven — passes one gate' and 'there is exactly one code path to the outside world'. False. Click-to-call via the Aloware Talk Chrome extension originates INSIDE Aloware, not through our API — our gate never runs, no consent_record_id is stamped, and call_detail.consent snapshot is fabricated after the fact from a webhook. Same for any dial the seller makes in Aloware's own UI, and the Power Dialer list is only gated at list-build time (a lead can opt out mid-session). Rewrite the gate as: HARD gate on our API paths (two-legged, SMS, email, sequence, automation), PRE-FLIGHT scrub + mid-session re-check on Power Dialer, and POST-HOC detection on extension/native calls with a 'contacted while blocked' compliance exception record. Anything else is liability theater.
- FAIL-CLOSED ON MANUAL DIALS WILL GET THE PRODUCT UNINSTALLED. 'unknown renders as blocked', 'Calling and texting are blocked for this lead until consent is recorded', and consent-panel errors that pause calling are correct for SMS and wrong for a one-to-one manual dial to a purchased FE lead. On a real floor, the day the intake mapping drops a vendor certificate field, 50 sellers cannot call the leads the owner just paid $18-$35 each for — they will dial from their personal cell and the CRM goes blind, which is the exact data-rot this module exists to prevent. Split the policy: SMS/automated = hard block; manual single dial with no consent record = amber warn + one-tap attestation + compliance.dial_unverified event + intake data-quality alert to admin.
- THE DISPOSITION TAXONOMY IS A TELEPHONY TAXONOMY, NOT A LIFE-INSURANCE ONE. connected / no_answer / voicemail / busy / wrong_number / failed / do_not_call is what any call-center CRM ships. An FE/IUL floor coaches and forecasts on SALES outcomes: not_interested, already_covered, price_objection, health_dq, age_dq, spouse/decision_maker_absent, callback_after_check_date (SSI/pension deposit dates drive FE callbacks), presentation_given, application_started, application_submitted, pending_carrier_decision, bad_lead_credit_request. Model outcome as TWO fields — connection_result (telephony, mapped from Aloware) and sales_outcome (our vertical-configurable list, set by the seller) — or the disposition mapping table becomes an argument about semantics and every downstream automation fires on the wrong thing.
- EMAIL IS SIZED FOR A B2B SAAS, NOT FOR FINAL EXPENSE. 'Email send with per-seller sender identity' is complexity:high and mvp_candidate:true (tenant sending domain, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, threading headers) while 'Inbound email capture' is NOT MVP — you are shipping a channel that can talk and cannot listen, on the channel that a 68-year-old FE prospect uses least. Either demote outbound email out of MVP (transactional-only from a tenant domain, reply-to = seller's real mailbox) or ship both. Open tracking is self-admittedly the weakest feature in the module and should be deleted, not toggled.
- KPI LIST IS A CONTACT-CENTER DASHBOARD WITH NO MONEY IN IT. Sixteen KPIs and not one connects a conversation to annual premium. Missing and mandatory for this product: premium written per talk hour, dials-to-application, contact-to-appointment and appointment-to-application rates, and — the single number an FE owner runs the business on — connect/close/premium BY LEAD VENDOR AND CAMPAIGN. 'Comms cost per closed-won opportunity' is the only revenue-adjacent metric and it is the least useful one.
- 'Snippets / quick replies' duplicates 'personal templates'. Two authoring surfaces, two data paths, same job. One object with a short-form flag.
- Chrome-extension presence 'detection' is hand-waved. A page cannot reliably detect an arbitrary extension unless that extension injects a detectable marker; if Aloware Talk does not, this silently degrades to 'we guess'. Specify the detection mechanism or drop to: always render click-to-call AND Call now, let the seller pick, remember the choice per device.
- 'Flag a message for compliance review' has no reviewer, no queue, no state machine, no SLA. Half a feature. Either define the review queue in the admin console or cut the button.

### Bloat to cut (9)
- Email open tracking — cut the feature, the toggle, the email.opened event and the tracking pixel infrastructure. The spec itself calls it the weakest feature here; a pixel that hurts deliverability to tell a seller an FE prospect 'opened' something is not worth the DMARC conversation.
- comms_cost_ledger as an owned entity with per-seller period rollups and a comms.cost_recorded event — Aloware bills the tenant, not the seller. Replace with a read-through admin panel of Aloware usage and a single 'estimated spend this period' number. A whole ledger entity to avoid a billing surprise on a <$100/month infra target is inverted priorities.
- Template performance stats as a standalone feature (reply/opt-out/blocked rate per template, tenant-wide and personal) — keep opt-out rate per template as a compliance alert, cut the rest until there is enough volume for the numbers to mean anything.
- Admin disposition MAPPING TABLE UI — a config screen, a health warning system and an editing workflow for a list that changes maybe twice a year. Ship a code-level mapping plus an 'unmapped disposition detected' alert with the raw string. Keep disposition_raw persistence (that part is right).
- MMS — cut from v1 entirely rather than parking it as non-MVP. Carrier/A2P variance plus a link fallback means you build two paths to send one plan illustration to a demographic that mostly cannot open either.
- Redaction + CCPA erasure as a conversations-owned high-complexity feature — erasure is a platform capability spanning leads, documents and comms. Conversations should own only the executor (purge bodies/media, call Aloware's delete endpoint, emit message.redacted); the request intake, scoping and completion record belong to admin/privacy.
- Snippets/quick replies — fold into personal templates.
- 'Integration health monitor + degraded mode' as a tile-sprawl console — keep degraded mode and the dead-letter list (those are load-bearing), cut the multi-tile health dashboard to one status line with last-check age.
- Supervisor 'coaching notes visible to that seller' plus 'flag for compliance review' plus 'export compliance audit' plus 'opt-out hotspots' in one screen — pick coaching notes and audit export for v2 and cut the rest; a review workflow with no reviewer role is a button that does nothing.

### Event gaps (11)
- NO CANONICAL OUTBOUND TOUCH EVENT. The spec correctly invents conversation.reply_received as the one canonical inbound signal 'so consumers never have to subscribe per channel' — and then fails to do the mirror image. Every consumer that cares about 'was this lead touched' (the 7-day cold rule, days-since-last-touch on the kanban card, rot alerts, My Day) has to subscribe to call.initiated + call.completed + sms.sent + email.sent + manual logs and decide which count. Emit conversation.touch_recorded {tenant_id, lead_id, opportunity_id, user_id, channel, direction, was_connected, occurred_at}. Without it, three modules will independently implement 'what counts as activity' and they will disagree.
- opportunity_id APPEARS IN PAYLOADS WITH NO RESOLUTION RULE. Threads are per-LEAD; a lead can hold two open opportunities (FE policy now, IUL cross-sell later) — this is a stated vertical for this product. call.completed, sms.sent and conversation.reply_received all carry opportunity_id with no rule for which one. Define it: active opportunity if exactly one, else most-recently-touched, else null and let the consumer decide. Silence here means the call lands on the wrong deal card.
- NO SPEED-TO-LEAD EVENT. seconds_since_lead_created is a payload FIELD on call.initiated only. The headline KPI, the coaching artifact and any leaderboard secondary category must therefore be recomputed by joining lead.created to the first call event. Emit lead.first_touch_recorded and lead.first_contact_made (first CONNECTED conversation) — the second one is also what the cold-lead rule and the pipeline actually need and neither exists.
- consent.revoked LEAKS THE MODEL'S BEST IDEA. opt_out_record is keyed by contact_value_hash 'so an opt-out follows the phone number even into a different (duplicate) lead record' — correct. The consent.revoked event carries lead_id and channel but NO contact_value_hash, so no consumer can apply the suppression to the duplicate. The data model is right and the event throws the key away.
- NO BLOCKED-DIAL EVENT AND NO QUIET-HOURS RELEASE EVENT. compliance.send_blocked covers sends; a refused DIAL is a different act with different consequences and should be its own event (or carry channel=call plus attempt origin). Separately, a message held for quiet hours has no lifecycle events — nothing emits when it is queued and nothing emits when it releases or expires, so the seller's message vanishes into a black box and the composer countdown is the only evidence it exists.
- RECONCILIATION BACKFILL IS SILENT. The webhook reliability layer backfills missed calls via API — which retroactively changes days-since-last-touch, may un-cold a lead and may alter leaderboard-adjacent activity counts, with no event emitted. Emit call.backfilled (or a corrected flag on call.completed) so downstream state machines can re-evaluate instead of drifting.
- NO AUTOMATION CORRELATION ID. sms.sent carries sequence_enrollment_id but neither sms.sent nor email.sent nor compliance.send_blocked carries the automation_run_id / step_id from automation.action_requested. The automations module is expected to 'pause the cadence rather than retry blindly' on a blocked send and has nothing to correlate the refusal back to the step that requested it.
- powerdialer.session_completed LISTS leaderboard AS A CONSUMER WITH NO METRIC. Earnings is the leaderboard's money metric; dialer sessions produce activity, not earnings. Either define the activity/recognition metric this feeds (this is exactly the 'Most Improved'/secondary category the research says is needed to stop a never-reset revenue board from demoting most of the floor) or remove leaderboard from the consumer list.
- NO EVENT FOR RISING OPT-OUT RATE despite it being a stated KPI and an explicit supervisor concern. Nothing fires when a seller or template crosses an opt-out threshold, so 'the seller who is generating opt-outs' is discovered by someone reading a report.
- INBOUND CALL vs REPLY SEMANTICS UNDEFINED. conversation.reply_received includes 'inbound call answered'. A MISSED inbound call is arguably the strongest possible reply signal (the lead is trying to reach you) and does not pause cadences under this wiring — so an automated nudge can fire minutes after the lead called back.
- NO number.reputation_changed / identity spam-flag event (pairs with the missing caller-ID reputation feature), and no email.unsubscribed distinct from consent.revoked — email opt-out is CAN-SPAM, not TCPA, and collapsing them into one revocation loses the channel-scope distinction the consent ledger was built to preserve.

### 🚨 Silo violations (7)
- UNMATCHED AND AMBIGUOUS INBOUND IS UNDEFINED — AND THE GAP IS A ROUTING ENGINE WAITING TO BE BUILT. 'Inbound and missed calls land in the owning seller's thread' assumes every inbound number resolves to exactly one lead owned by exactly one seller. Purchased FE leads are RESOLD; the same phone number legitimately exists in two sellers' silos. It also assumes a match exists at all. Specify explicitly: (a) inbound to a seller's dedicated number always lands in THAT seller's silo regardless of which lead matches, (b) unmatched inbound creates a lead in that seller's own book, (c) NO shared inbound pool, NO 'next available agent', NO overflow. Leaving this blank guarantees someone implements routing.
- TENANT-WIDE SUPPRESSION IS A CROSS-SILO WRITE THAT ANY SELLER CAN PERFORM. Permissions grant sellers 'Add only' to the internal do-not-contact list, and suppression_entry is keyed by contact_value_hash at TENANT level. Seller A can therefore permanently block a number that sits in Seller B's book, with no approval, no notification and no audit surface named. Legally defensible, operationally a weapon. Require: seller adds are scoped to their own lead by default, tenant-wide internal entries are admin-only, and every internal (non-dnc_*) entry logs actor + reason + affected-lead count.
- OPT-OUT MIRRORING LEAKS THE EXISTENCE OF ANOTHER SELLER'S ACTIVITY. Because opt-outs follow contact_value_hash tenant-wide, Seller B opens a fresh lead and finds SMS already hard-blocked — inferring that someone else in the org already worked this person. The block is correct; the copy is not specified. Mandate the microcopy: 'This number has opted out of text messages.' Never surface who contacted them, when, or from which lead. Same rule for suppression and DNC blocks.
- 'Team conversations' EMPTY STATE OFFERS 'Invite a seller' — user provisioning does not belong in the comms module and quietly implies a supervisor-driven org-building flow adjacent to assignment. Cut the button, link to admin.
- lead.owner_changed IS CONSUMED BUT ITS ORIGIN IS NEVER CONSTRAINED. The MVP has no assignment engine; the spec should state that ownership transfer is a deliberate admin action only, and that consuming this event must never be interpretable as routing. As written, an implementer reads 'move thread ownership and channel routing to the new owner' and builds the reassignment UI nobody asked for.
- Supervisor permission 'can disenroll on any lead for compliance reasons' is acceptable oversight, but pair it with a mandatory reason + notification to the owning seller, otherwise sequences stop for reasons the seller cannot see inside their own silo.
- Power Dialer 'reconcile results back into threads' — state that reconciliation is silo-bound: if an Aloware list result maps to a lead the running seller does not own (possible after an ownership change mid-session), the result must be dropped to a dead-letter/exception queue, never written into another seller's thread.

## Risks & dependencies
- **No embeddable softphone.** We are not an Aloware native partner, so there is no iframe/SDK dial pad for our app. Desktop calling depends on the Aloware Talk Chrome extension being installed per machine (a support burden across ~50 sellers), and mobile depends entirely on the Two-Legged Call API. If two-legged calling turns out to be rate-limited or unable to present the seller's own caller ID, mobile speed-to-lead degrades to native tap-to-call with manual logging.
- **Webhook fragility is existential for this module.** Auto-logging is our build on their events. Dropped, duplicated, out-of-order or unsigned webhooks corrupt the timeline, which corrupts days-since-last-touch, which corrupts the cold-lead rule and the rot alerts. Mitigation is non-optional: signature verification, idempotency by external id, dead-letter with replay, and a scheduled API reconciliation backfill.
- **Aloware disposition drift.** Any rename on their side silently breaks automations and reporting unless the mapping layer holds and unmapped values raise a health alert rather than being dropped.
- **A2P 10DLC registration is a hard gate with real lead time.** SMS cannot ship before the brand/campaign is approved, and carrier throughput limits will shape how sequences pace. Treat SMS availability as a dependency on an external approval we do not control.
- **Two-party-consent recording states.** Several US states require all-party consent to record. Aloware provides the recording; the disclosure obligation is ours to surface. Getting this wrong turns our best coaching asset into liability.
- **Consent is per contact value, not per record.** Duplicate leads, changed phone numbers and merges are the realistic paths to contacting someone who opted out. The opt-out mirror is keyed by hashed contact value specifically to survive that — this must be tested deliberately.
- **CCPA erasure spans two systems.** Recordings and transcripts live on Aloware as well as with us. If their deletion API is limited, we cannot fully honor an erasure request; this must be verified before we promise it.
- **Per-minute and per-segment overage.** Aloware usage costs sit outside the sub-$100/month infra target and scale with success. The cost meter exists so this surprises nobody, but the pricing tier itself is an unresolved commercial dependency.
- **Email sending reputation.** Fifty sellers on one tenant domain means one careless sender can damage deliverability for everyone. Per-seller sending identity on a shared authenticated domain is the compromise; volume limits and bounce handling are mandatory.
- **Depends on other modules:** lead-management (lead ownership, phone/email, merge events, intake consent payload), automations (cadence definitions, trigger vocabulary, sequence.step_due), activities/calendar (appointment events driving reminders and no-show follow-up), pipeline (stage-change events for auto-pause and stage-bound templates), admin-settings (tenant policy), notifications (mobile push).
- **CUT — in-app WebRTC softphone.** Rebuilding a dialer we already pay Aloware for, with no partner API to do it properly. Nothing breaks without it: click-to-call plus two-legged calling covers desktop and mobile.
- **CUT — bulk email/SMS blasting.** Pure agency bloat, and a direct A2P/TCPA hazard on a purchased-lead book. Sequences plus 1:1 sending cover every real need in this vertical.
- **CUT — AI autopilot replies (AI answering leads unsupervised).** High demo appeal, but nothing breaks without it, and an unsupervised bot texting insurance prospects is a compliance and brand risk we would be inventing for ourselves. AloAi summarization stays; AloAi speaking on our behalf does not.
- **CUT — shared team inbox with conversation assignment and handoff.** It directly contradicts the per-seller silo model and would smuggle a routing engine in through the comms door.
- **CUT — email click tracking.** Measurable deliverability harm, marginal insight. Open tracking survives, off by default.
- **CUT — Facebook/Instagram/webchat channels.** Classic GHL bloat with no evidence of use in FE/IUL tele-sales; the channel abstraction leaves the door open if that ever changes.
- **CUT — local presence / caller-ID rotation.** Answer-rate gains are real but the regulatory and carrier-reputation risk is not ours to take; Aloware's branded caller ID is the sanctioned path.
- **CUT — call scoring and QA scorecards.** Supervisor review plus coaching notes cover the actual need; scorecards are a product of their own.

## Open questions
- What is our actual Aloware tier, and does it include the Sequence API, Power Dialer API and AloAi transcription/summarization, or are those add-ons? Several features here are tier-dependent.
- Does the Two-Legged Call API let each seller present their own assigned number as caller ID, or does it dial from a shared line? If shared, per-seller webhook attribution and callback routing both need a different design.
- Does Aloware expose a usage/billing API? Without it, the cost meter is our own estimate from send counts and call durations, and must be labeled as an estimate.
- Is there an API to delete recordings and transcripts on Aloware's side? This determines whether we can truthfully honor a CCPA erasure end to end.
- Is voicemail drop (pre-recorded voicemail) available to us via API? It would materially change FE attempt economics, but it was not verified in Phase 1 and must not be assumed.
- Who is the system of record for the contact — do we push every lead into Aloware, or only on first contact? Pushing everything is simpler for webhook attribution but inflates their contact counts and possibly our bill.
- Do we use Aloware sequences as the cadence engine, or build our own and use Aloware only as the send API? Aloware's engine is faster to reach but splits the automation surface across two products and weakens the auto-pause guarantee.
- Which inbound-email provider handles reply parsing, and is the sending domain per tenant or per seller? This is the single biggest unresolved decision in the email half of this module.
- Who owns the A2P 10DLC brand registration — the client's agency entity or ours? This blocks SMS entirely and is an external, slow approval.
- Do the client's lead vendors actually deliver a consent certificate reference in the ping-post payload, and in what field? If they do not, our consent ledger degrades from provable to asserted, and the compliance story weakens substantially. (Related to open question OQ-2 on real lead channels.)
- Should a supervisor ever be able to send on a seller's behalf — for example while a seller is out? The current design says no, because impersonation destroys the audit trail on TCPA-relevant sends. Confirm with the client.
- Should transcripts of recorded FE applications be retained longer than ordinary call recordings, given they may function as evidence of a voice-signed application?
- Reserved WhatsApp slot: which provider, and does the client want it as an outbound channel at all, given the US life market's strong SMS preference?
