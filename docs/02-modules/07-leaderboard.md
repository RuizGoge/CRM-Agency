# 7. Leaderboard (Public Performance & Recognition)

> **Module id:** `leaderboard` · part of [`docs/02-functional-map.md`](../02-functional-map.md)

## Verdict
KEEP — and treat it as the product's headline differentiator, not a reporting sub-tab. Two reshapes, no cuts to the module itself: (1) keep the client's decision (ALL-TIME + REAL-TIME) as the default and only board period in v1, but write `period_key` on every earnings ledger entry from day one so monthly/quarterly/weekly boards are a config flip, never a migration; (2) ship the secondary recognition layer (Most Improved, streaks, personal bests, effort categories) in the SAME release as the money board — research says a never-reset revenue-only board structurally demotes 60-70% of the team, and an all-time board makes that worse for every new hire, so the recognition layer is not a "nice to have", it is the mitigation that makes the client's decision survivable. Do NOT merge into Dashboards/Reporting: dashboards are per-seller and silo-scoped, the leaderboard is the single deliberate PUBLIC surface in the whole system, and merging it would either leak silo data into dashboards or bury the differentiator. Also reshape the metric surface: FE and IUL annual premiums differ by an order of magnitude, so the board must support product-line boards from the start or FE-only sellers become permanently unrankable.

## Purpose & value
Every seller works alone in their own book of business and never sees another seller's leads — the leaderboard is the one place the whole floor sees the same thing: who is closing, how much annual premium is on the board right now, and exactly where you stand and what it takes to move up one spot.

## Screens & states (8)

### Leaderboard (main)
The public standings surface every role opens: podium, ranked list, viewer's own rank with neighbors, board/category switcher, live updates.

**Key actions:** `Switch board (All Products / Final Expense / IUL / Deals Won)` · `Switch tab (Standings / Recognition / My Performance / Hall of Fame)` · `Open a seller's public profile card` · `Jump to my row` · `Mute or unmute celebrations` · `Open kiosk view` · `Copy my rank to share`

- **Empty:** Header: 'No Earnings on the board yet.' Body: 'Earnings appear the moment a deal lands in a Closed-Won stage. Move an opportunity into Closed Won — with its annual premium filled in — and you will be #1.' Primary button: 'Go to my pipeline'. Secondary link: 'How Earnings are calculated'. When the board has standings but the VIEWER has none: 'You are not on the board yet. Your first closed policy puts you here.' with the same primary button.
- **Loading:** Podium and 12 list rows render as skeletons at final height (no layout shift); board/tab switcher is interactive immediately; a 'Loading standings…' status is announced to screen readers. If the live stream has not connected within 3s, the board renders from the last cached projection with a 'Reconnecting…' chip.
- **Error:** Inline card replacing the list: 'We could not load the standings. Your Earnings are safe — this is a display problem.' with 'Try again' and a last-known-good timestamp ('Showing standings from 10:42 AM'). If only the live stream fails, the list stays and the chip reads 'Live updates paused — refreshing every 5 seconds.'
- **No permission:** Not applicable to standings for any signed-in role — the board is public to admin, supervisor and seller by design. Deactivated or non-sales accounts see: 'The leaderboard is available to sales users. Ask an admin if you need access.' Attempts to open another seller's underlying leads from a profile card return: 'Only aggregate performance is shared here. Leads stay private to each seller.'
- **Mobile:** Podium collapses to a horizontal 2-1-3 strip; the ranked list is single-column with the viewer's row pinned as a sticky bottom bar showing rank, Earnings and next-rank gap; board switcher becomes a horizontal chip scroller; celebration is a bottom sheet, not a full-screen takeover; pull-to-refresh forces a projection re-read.

### Kiosk / TV view
No-chrome, unauthenticated, room-legible display for a wall TV that auto-rotates scenes and takes over full-screen on a Closed-Won celebration.

**Key actions:** `Open via kiosk token URL` · `Cycle scene manually (arrow keys)` · `Pause rotation` · `Toggle sound` · `Exit full screen`

- **Empty:** Full-bleed: 'The board is open. First close of all time takes #1.' Subline: 'Standings update the moment a policy is written.' No buttons — a kiosk has no operator. Once standings exist but the current scene has no data (e.g. no Most Improved yet): 'Most Improved unlocks after 30 days of activity.'
- **Loading:** Oversized brand-neutral logotype with an indeterminate progress bar and 'Connecting to live standings…'; never renders partial standings, to avoid a TV showing a half-built ranking to the whole floor.
- **Error:** Full-screen amber banner over the last-known standings: 'Live updates interrupted — showing standings from 10:42 AM. Reconnecting…' with an automatic exponential-backoff retry and a visible countdown. On an invalid or revoked token: 'This display link is no longer active. Ask an admin for a new kiosk link.'
- **No permission:** Expired/revoked/wrong-tenant token renders only: 'This display link is no longer active.' — no standings, no seller names, no partial data, and no login form (a wall screen must never invite credential entry).
- **Mobile:** Kiosk is landscape-first and explicitly not a phone surface; on a phone it renders a reduced portrait variant (podium + top 5 + rotation) intended for a spare device propped on a desk. Screen-wake-lock requested where supported.

### Recognition & Badges
The anti-demotivation surface: secondary public categories (Most Improved, Comeback, Rookie, Consistency, Speed-to-Lead, Most Contacted, Best Close Rate) plus streaks and the badge wall.

**Key actions:** `Switch category` · `See how a category is calculated` · `View my standing in each category` · `Open a winner's profile card` · `Enable/disable categories (admin)`

- **Empty:** 'Recognition categories are warming up. Most Improved compares your last 30 days to the 30 before — check back once there is a month of history.' For a category with no qualifying seller: 'Nobody qualifies for Best Close Rate yet — it needs at least 10 worked opportunities.' For the viewer with no awards: 'No recognition yet. Consistency counts every week you close at least one policy — this week can be your first.'
- **Loading:** Category chips render immediately; each category card shows a three-row skeleton. Categories load independently so a slow metric never blocks the rest.
- **Error:** Per-category card: 'Most Improved could not be calculated right now.' with 'Retry'. Other categories keep rendering — one bad metric must not blank the recognition page.
- **No permission:** Visible to all signed-in sales roles. Category configuration controls are hidden (not disabled) for seller and supervisor; a direct link returns 'Only admins can change recognition categories.'
- **Mobile:** Categories become vertically stacked cards showing top 3 + the viewer's own position; horizontal swipe moves between categories; calculation explainer opens as a bottom sheet.

### My Performance
The seller's private read of their own standing: rank history, Earnings breakdown, product mix, streaks, personal bests, next-rank gap and their own ledger.

**Key actions:** `View rank history (7/30/90 days, all-time)` · `Open my earnings ledger entries` · `Filter my Earnings by product line` · `See my personal bests and badges` · `Dispute or ask about an adjustment (opens a note to admin)`

- **Empty:** 'Nothing to show yet — this fills in with your first closed policy.' Body: 'Your Earnings are the total annual premium of every opportunity you move into a Closed-Won stage.' Primary button: 'Open my pipeline'. Secondary: 'See what counts as Earnings'.
- **Loading:** Sparkline and metric tiles as skeletons; ledger table shows five shimmer rows; numbers never render partially (no flashing zeros before real values).
- **Error:** 'We could not load your performance detail.' with 'Try again'. If only the ledger fails: 'Your totals are up to date, but the entry history could not load.'
- **No permission:** A seller can open only their own My Performance. Supervisor/admin can open any seller's performance in read-only mode with a visible 'Viewing as supervisor — read only' banner. A seller reaching another seller's URL gets: 'You can see everyone's standing on the leaderboard, but performance detail is private to each seller.'
- **Mobile:** Metric tiles in a 2-up grid; rank sparkline full-width; ledger becomes a card list with amount, reason and date; adjustments visually distinguished from closes.

### Seller public profile card
Aggregate-only identity card opened from any board row — the hard boundary between public performance and private book of business.

**Key actions:** `View aggregate stats, badges, streaks, personal bests` · `See product mix` · `Close card` · `(Supervisor/admin only) Open full performance detail`

- **Empty:** 'This seller has not closed a policy yet.' Body: 'Standings, badges and streaks appear after the first Closed-Won opportunity.'
- **Loading:** Card opens instantly with the name and avatar already known from the row; stats area shows skeleton tiles.
- **Error:** 'We could not load this profile.' with 'Try again' inside the card; the card never falls back to showing anything beyond aggregates.
- **No permission:** For sellers, the drill-down actions (leads, opportunities, activity) are absent from the DOM, not merely disabled. Direct navigation returns: 'Only aggregate performance is shared here. Leads stay private to each seller.'
- **Mobile:** Opens as a full-height bottom sheet with a drag handle; stats in a 2-up grid; badges wrap.

### Leaderboard Settings (admin)
Configure boards, metrics, period mode, tie-breaks, qualifying thresholds, recognition categories, celebration tiers and kiosk tokens.

**Key actions:** `Create/edit/reorder boards` · `Set metric and product filter` · `Set period_mode (all_time now; monthly/quarterly reserved)` · `Set tie-break order and minimum qualifying volume` · `Enable/disable and reorder recognition categories` · `Configure celebration tiers and broadcast scope` · `Mint, label, rotate and revoke kiosk tokens` · `Trigger a recompute with diff preview`

- **Empty:** 'One board is live: All Products, all-time Earnings.' Body: 'Add a board to rank a single product line — Final Expense and IUL premiums are very different sizes, so a separate board keeps each fair.' Primary button: 'Add a board'. Kiosk section empty: 'No display links yet. Create one to put the board on a TV without signing anyone in.' Primary: 'Create display link'.
- **Loading:** Form sections render as skeletons; controls stay disabled until the current configuration is loaded so an admin can never save over an unread config.
- **Error:** 'Your changes were not saved.' with the failed field highlighted, the previous value preserved, and 'Try again'. On a recompute failure: 'The rebuild did not finish. Standings were not changed.' — a failed recompute must never leave a partial projection.
- **No permission:** Seller and supervisor never see the settings entry point; direct URL returns 'Only admins can configure the leaderboard.' Kiosk token creation is additionally available to supervisors, with a scoped view that lists only the tokens they created.
- **Mobile:** Fully usable but explicitly desktop-optimized; sections collapse into an accordion; the recompute diff preview is read-only on mobile and requires desktop to confirm.

### Earnings Ledger & Adjustments (admin/supervisor)
Auditable view of every ledger entry across sellers, with the ability (admin) to post reversals, chargebacks and corrections and to release anomaly-flagged entries.

**Key actions:** `Filter by seller, product line, entry type, reason code, date` · `Open the source opportunity` · `Post an adjustment with reason code and mandatory note` · `Approve or reject an anomaly-flagged entry` · `Export CSV`

- **Empty:** 'No earnings entries yet.' Body: 'Every closed policy writes one entry here. Adjustments and chargebacks are added on top — nothing is ever edited or deleted.'
- **Loading:** Filter bar active immediately; table streams in pages of 50 with skeleton rows; totals show a spinner until the full aggregate resolves rather than showing a wrong subtotal.
- **Error:** 'We could not load the ledger.' with 'Try again'. On a failed adjustment: 'The adjustment was not posted. Standings are unchanged.' plus the retained draft.
- **No permission:** Sellers have no access to the cross-seller ledger; they see only their own entries inside My Performance. Direct URL for a seller: 'Earnings history is available for your own book. Ask an admin about team-wide records.' Supervisors get read-only: the 'Post adjustment' action is visible but disabled with tooltip 'Only admins can post adjustments.'
- **Mobile:** Table becomes a card list (seller, amount, type, reason, date); posting an adjustment requires desktop — a public-money write action is deliberately not a one-thumb operation.

### Celebration overlay (transient surface)
The event-driven moment: toast/confetti for the closer, optional floor-wide broadcast, full-screen takeover on kiosk.

**Key actions:** `Dismiss` · `Mute this type of celebration` · `View the seller's profile card` · `Share to the kiosk (admin)`

- **Empty:** Not applicable — the overlay exists only in response to opportunity.won. If a celebration fires while the underlying record is already reversed, nothing renders.
- **Loading:** None by design: the overlay renders from the event payload alone and never waits on a fetch — a delayed celebration is a failed celebration.
- **Error:** If assets (avatar, sound) fail, the overlay degrades to a text-only toast with the name, amount and product; it is never suppressed for an asset failure.
- **No permission:** Broadcast celebrations carry only display name, amount of annual premium and product line — never the client's name, lead source or any opportunity detail — so there is no permission tier: what is shown is safe for every seller to see.
- **Mobile:** Bottom sheet with a 4-second auto-dismiss, haptic tap on own close, no confetti when the device reports reduced motion or low power mode; never blocks the dialer or an active call screen.

## Typical automations
- When an opportunity moves into a Closed-Won stage → post the earnings ledger entry, recompute standings, fire the celebration at the right tier, and tell the closer their new rank.
- When a seller's close moves them past someone → send the passed seller an opt-in, gap-framed nudge ('You are $900 from #6'), never a loss message, and never while they are on a call.
- When a seller reaches the podium for the first time → full celebration tier, podium-debut badge, and a kiosk takeover.
- When a Closed-Won deal is reopened, deleted or its annual premium is edited → post an offsetting ledger entry and re-rank silently, with no celebration and no notification to anyone but the seller and admin.
- When a close is more than the configured multiple of that seller's trailing median premium → hold it out of the public board, flag it for admin, and notify the admin before it ever appears on the TV.
- When nobody has closed for the configured quiet period → the kiosk stops showing a frozen money board and rotates to effort categories, streaks and personal bests instead.
- When a seller beats one of their own records → private celebration always, public celebration if they have opted in.
- When a seller's daily or weekly close streak advances → update the badge on their row; when it breaks, update it silently with no announcement.
- Every night at tenant midnight → freeze a standings snapshot so rank deltas, Most Improved and Comeback have a baseline the next morning.
- Every 30 days → re-evaluate every recognition category, award the new holders and record the previous ones to the Hall of Fame.
- When a new seller is created → place them in an 'On deck' section rather than dead last, and start their 90-day rookie window.
- When a seller is deactivated → freeze their ledger, drop them from the live board, keep their history and records intact.
- When the kiosk has not received a live update within the stale threshold → show the stale banner with the last-good timestamp and start reconnect backoff.
- When an admin posts a chargeback or not-taken adjustment → re-rank, log it to the audit trail, and show the entry in that seller's own ledger with its reason code.
- When a period is enabled and closes (dormant in v1) → archive the standings, award the period winner, and start the next period with the all-time board still available as a toggle.

## KPIs
- Standings freshness: p95 latency from opportunity.won to the rank visibly updated on an open board and on the kiosk (target < 2s).
- Daily board reach: % of active sellers who open the leaderboard or its dashboard widget at least once per working day (target > 70%).
- Anti-demotivation signal (the KPI that decides whether all-time survives): % of sellers ranked outside the top 10 who still open the board at least weekly (target > 60%; below 40% for two consecutive months is the trigger to revisit the period-reset decision).
- Recognition coverage: % of active sellers with at least one recognition award, badge, streak or personal best in the last 30 days (target > 60% — directly measures the mitigation for a revenue-only board).
- Top-10 churn: number of distinct sellers appearing in the top 10 over a rolling 30 days (a board with churn ≤ 10 on an all-time metric is a frozen board and needs a period or category intervention).
- Celebration health: mute rate (% of sellers with celebration_mute_mode != none) and dismiss-within-1s rate — both rising means we are interrupting, not motivating (mute rate target < 20%).
- Earnings data integrity: % of Closed-Won transitions that arrive with a non-zero annual premium (target 100%, enforced by pipeline stage gating) and % of ledger amount reversed in the trailing 90 days.
- Kiosk reliability: % of kiosk uptime rendering fresh data, and count of stale-banner minutes per week (target < 15 min/week).
- Board-to-action correlation: dials and connected calls in the 60 minutes after a seller views the board vs their personal baseline — the only direct test of the motivational hypothesis.
- Anomaly rate: number of anomaly-flagged closes per 100 closes and median time to admin resolution (measures both data quality and gaming pressure).

## Insurance specifics (Life / FE / IUL) & vertical-agnostic core
Genuinely Life/FE/IUL-specific here, all of it isolated as configuration rather than code: (1) PREMIUM ASYMMETRY — deal_value is annual premium, and an FE policy's annual premium is typically a few hundred to ~$1.5k while an IUL's can be many multiples of that. A single mixed all-time board would make an FE specialist structurally unrankable no matter how many policies they write, so the module needs product-line boards and a deals-won board from the start; this is the one insurance fact that most changes the design. (2) CHARGEBACKS AND NOT-TAKEN POLICIES — in Final Expense a real share of issued policies are never taken or lapse in the first months, and carriers claw commission back. A public money board that cannot go DOWN is a lie, which is exactly why Earnings is an append-only ledger with typed reversal reason codes (policy_not_taken, first_year_lapse, carrier_chargeback) instead of a stored sum. (3) ISSUE vs SUBMISSION LAG — application submitted, underwriting, and policy issued are weeks apart in life; the tenant must decide which stage flag counts as Closed-Won for the board, and the ledger records the stage that produced the entry so that decision is auditable and changeable. (4) SPEED-TO-LEAD AS A RECOGNITION CATEGORY — purchased FE leads arrive via ping-post and decay in minutes, so 'fastest median time from lead created to first connected call' is a legitimate public performance category in this vertical in a way it would not be in a long-cycle B2B pipeline. (5) COMPLIANCE POSTURE — the board and kiosk publish seller display name, aggregate annual premium and product line only; no consumer name, phone, health information or lead source ever reaches a public or unauthenticated surface, which keeps the TCPA/CCPA surface area of this module at zero. HOW THE CORE STAYS VERTICAL-AGNOSTIC: the scoring engine knows nothing about insurance. It knows a ledger of typed monetary entries keyed by owner + period + product tag, a metric_key, a period_mode, tie-break rules, and declarative recognition category definitions. 'Annual premium' is a label on deal_value owned by the pipeline module; 'Final Expense' and 'IUL' are values of a product_type tag; chargeback and not-taken are rows in an editable reason-code catalog; Speed-to-Lead is a category definition over lead.created and call.completed, which exist in any vertical. Adding a second vertical means adding boards and reason codes in settings — not touching the leaderboard's code.

## ⚔️ Adversarial review (sales-ops critic)

**Verdict:** NEEDS WORK — the scoring core (append-only earnings ledger, disposable projection, frozen seller_id, product-line boards, top10+2) is genuinely right and worth defending, but the module is scoped as a whole product rather than an MVP, it is missing the two surfaces a real FE/IUL floor runs on daily (today's production and goal attainment), it ignores every Aloware-derived leading indicator (talk time, dials, dispositions, appointments set) while inventing generic activity metrics, chargeback reversal is architecturally correct but operationally dead without carrier/policy-number reconciliation, three of its own KPIs have no event to measure them, and it asserts zero CCPA exposure while publishing named employee earnings on an unauthenticated URL that a supervisor can mint.

### Must fix — missing for real operation (11)
- NO TODAY / THIS-WEEK PRODUCTION SURFACE. This is the single biggest operational miss. A life telesales floor runs on today's board — at 2pm the manager and the floor need to know what's been written TODAY, because that is what changes behavior in the last three hours of dial time. The client chose all-time for the RANKING; that decision does not forbid a today/MTD AP column, a 'written today' ticker, or a daily total on the kiosk. As specified, on a slow Tuesday the TV shows the same frozen order it showed last Tuesday and the recognition-category rotation is a euphemism for 'nothing happened'.
- NO GOAL / QUOTA. Every FE and IUL seller works to a weekly or monthly AP target, and every agency owner manages to it. The board has zero concept of goal, % to goal, or pace-to-goal. 'Distance to next rank' is purely peer-relative — it tells a #38 seller how to pass #37, never whether either of them is on track. An owner evaluating this product will ask 'where's attainment' in the first two minutes.
- NO TEAM / MANAGER DIMENSION. Fifty sellers do not sit flat under one owner; they sit under 3-6 team leads. There is no team_id on standings, no team board, no 'my team' filter for supervisors. This is org structure, not lead routing, so it does not violate the silo rule — and its absence means the supervisor role has global visibility with no way to scope it to the people they actually manage.
- NO CARRIER RECONCILIATION PATH. earnings_ledger_entry has no carrier, policy_number, application_date, or issue_date. Chargebacks and not-taken policies do not originate in the CRM — they arrive on a carrier statement weeks later, keyed by policy number. Without those fields, the 'manual adjustment (admin)' feature is unusable in practice: the admin has a carrier report of 14 lapses and no way to match them to ledger entries. The reversal mechanism is architecturally correct and operationally dead.
- SUBMITTED vs ISSUED/PLACED IS ACKNOWLEDGED AND THEN PUNTED. insurance_specifics names the weeks-long submission→underwriting→issue lag and then resolves it with 'the tenant must decide which stage flag counts as Closed-Won'. That is not a design. Every real FE floor boards SUBMITTED AP for motivation (it's immediate) and reconciles on ISSUED/PLACED AP for money. The ledger needs an entry lifecycle (pending → placed → charged back) or a second board metric, plus a visible 'pending issue' amount, or the public number permanently overstates real production — which the spec itself says is the thing that makes an owner stop trusting the board.
- THE CLOSER IS NEVER TOLD THEIR OWN CLOSE WAS HELD. earnings.updated fires only when the COUNTABLE total changes, and anomaly-flagged entries are is_countable=false. So a seller writes the biggest IUL of their career, gets no celebration, no rank movement, no toast, no explanation — and only the admin is notified. On a floor, that produces an accusation of theft within an hour. There must be a private 'under review' state visible to the closer with an expected resolution time.
- NO ONCE-PER-OPPORTUNITY CELEBRATION GUARD. Nothing prevents close → reopen → close on the same opportunity from firing a fresh confetti + floor broadcast + kiosk takeover every cycle. The ledger nets to zero; the attention economy does not. Celebration must be idempotent per opportunity_id, with re-closes silent.
- NO SPLIT / JOINT CASE HANDLING. In life sales a rookie frequently writes with a trainer or a closer takes over a setter's appointment, and the AP is split. earnings_ledger_entry has a single seller_id. Either support fractional credit (two entries summing to the AP) or state explicitly that splits are unsupported in MVP and that the writing agent takes 100% — silence here means the first split case produces a public number everyone on the floor knows is wrong.
- NO EXACTLY-ONCE GUARANTEE ON THE LEDGER. source_event_id is stored but nothing declares a uniqueness constraint on (tenant_id, source_event_id). Any at-least-once delivery, retry, or event replay double-credits the most publicly visible number in the product. For an append-only public money ledger this is the first invariant, not an implementation detail.
- NO LEAD-VOLUME CONTEXT. Purchased FE leads via ping-post are the input; sellers on different lead flows/vendors/budgets are not comparable. The board publicly ranks output with zero visibility into input. An owner who allocates 60 leads/week to one seller and 20 to another knows the board is measuring his own allocation decision. At minimum: AP per lead worked as an owner-visible (not necessarily public) metric.
- SPEED-TO-LEAD IS THE ONE PING-POST-NATIVE METRIC HERE AND IT IS MEASURED WRONG. It is anchored to call.completed / 'first connected call'. A seller controls how fast they DIAL; they do not control whether the prospect answers. Ranking on time-to-CONNECT publicly ranks lead quality and vendor answer rates, not seller urgency. Anchor to first dial attempt (Aloware call.started / two-legged call initiated), and cap it at the lead's decay window.

### Too generic / boilerplate (11)
- Badge thresholds ($25k / $100k / $250k all-time AP, 10/50/100 policies) are SaaS-generic numbers with no FE/IUL calibration. An IUL writer crosses $100k AP in a quarter; an FE writer doing $8-12k AP/month takes a year. Same badge, wildly different achievement — the badge wall will read as 'IUL people get trophies'. Thresholds must be per product_type (or per-tenure) or they are decoration.
- 'Most Contacted (most connected calls)' is a generic activity metric lifted from any CRM. On a real FE/IUL telesales floor the leading indicators the manager actually boards are TALK TIME (minutes on the phone), DIALS/HOUR, and APPOINTMENTS SET. Aloware hands you call duration, disposition and connect status in the webhook for free and the spec ignores all of it. Replace Most Contacted with Talk Time and Appointments Set.
- 'Best Close Rate (won / worked, with a minimum volume floor)' — 'worked' is undefined on a PUBLIC screen. In FE, close rate is measured against leads worked or presentations given, and the denominator is trivially gamed by simply not creating an opportunity record for a bad lead. An undefined denominator on a public ranking is a dispute generator. Define it as opportunities_created_from_leads or cut it.
- recognition_category.metric_definition as a stored 'definition' field is exactly the blank-canvas data model the project's anti-patterns list forbids. Categories must be an enum of implemented evaluators with config (window, floor, direction) — not a free-form definition nobody can validate.
- The whole gamification stack (badges + Hall of Fame + streaks + personal bests + 7 categories + podium debut + comeback) is portable to any sales SaaS. Nothing in it is anchored to writing life policies. The only insurance-native recognition concepts — persistency/placement rate, first-call-close rate on ping-post leads, AP per lead — are absent.
- 'Copy my rank to share' — share WHERE? Slack/Teams was explicitly cut, there is no in-app social surface, and no seller emails their rank to anyone. Filler action.
- Command palette entry 'Who is #1' is filler. 'Leaderboard' and 'My rank' are the only two that survive the what-breaks-without-it test.
- metric_key = avg_annual_premium as a board metric is generic 'more metrics is better' thinking and is actively harmful here: one $9k IUL and zero other production wins the board outright. Either cut it or it needs the same min_qualifying_volume as the ratio metrics — the spec applies the floor only to 'ratio metrics'.
- KPI 'Board-to-action correlation: dials and connected calls in the 60 minutes after a seller views the board' is unwired twice over: no leaderboard.viewed event is emitted anywhere, and no dial-level event from Aloware is consumed. Same for 'Daily board reach' and the flagship 'Anti-demotivation signal' — all three depend on a view event that does not exist in events_emitted.
- leaderboard_user_preference carries display_name and avatar_url. Those belong to the user/identity module. Duplicating them here creates a second source of truth for the name printed on a wall TV — the classic drift-prone snapshot anti-pattern.
- 'Top-10 churn ≤ 10 over 30 days = frozen board' is a KPI designed to always fail on an all-time board with tenure spread. A metric that is red on day one and forever will be ignored, which is worse than not having it.

### Bloat to cut (10)
- SEVEN recognition categories in v1 (Most Improved, Comeback, Rookie, Consistency, Speed-to-Lead, Most Contacted, Best Close Rate). Each is a separate metric pipeline with a window, a qualifying floor, tie-breaks, an explainer and an empty state, and the whole thing is rated as ONE 'high' feature. Ship three — Most Improved (the actual mitigation), Rookie (protects new hires on an all-time board), and one effort metric (Talk Time). The rest are additive later at zero architectural cost.
- MVP SCOPE IS NOT AN MVP: roughly 25 of 34 features carry mvp_candidate=true, including SSE live re-rank with motion, kiosk tokens, kiosk rotation, product-line boards, ledger, snapshots, 7 categories, celebration tiers, mute modes, accessibility pass and the dashboard widget. The verdict text says 'two reshapes, no cuts'. That is the hollowed-base-plan problem in reverse — everything is P0, so nothing is.
- Kiosk gold-plating: four rotating scenes + burn-in-safe background drift + 16:9 AND portrait layouts + screen wake lock + manual arrow-key scene cycling + pause rotation + sound toggle. A wall TV in v1 needs two scenes (standings, celebration takeover) and a stale banner. Everything else is a second-release polish pass on a screen nobody interacts with.
- Rank delta vs yesterday / vs last 7 days on an ALL-TIME cumulative board. The spec's own justification admits the absolute order barely moves — so the delta column will be blank for ~45 of 50 rows every day. It is compensating for the all-time decision rather than surfacing anything. Keep the nightly snapshot (it powers Most Improved and sparklines); drop the delta arrows until periods exist.
- The anomaly rule as designed ('exceeds a configurable multiple of the seller's trailing median') is broken for exactly this product mix: an FE specialist's first IUL will always be 5-10x their trailing median and will always be held, and a rookie with one close has no median at all. Redesign as a per-product_type hard ceiling (FE ceiling ~$3k AP, IUL ceiling set by the agency) or cut it — a statistical guardrail that fires on every legitimate career milestone will be disabled in week two.
- board_scope_tags on earnings_ledger_entry is undefined and duplicates product_type, which is the only tag any board actually filters on. Speculative generality on the immutable table.
- 'Dispute or ask about an adjustment (opens a note to admin)' — no entity, no event, no admin inbox, no state machine. It is a button that goes nowhere. Either model it (dispute record + event + resolution) or remove it and let the seller message the admin however they already do.
- Standings CSV export. Supervisors and admins already see the full board live and can recompute it; the number they actually need outside the app is the LEDGER (for comp and carrier reconciliation). Keep ledger export, cut standings export.
- Consuming opportunity.stage_changed 'as a safety net' when pipeline.stage_config_changed is ALSO consumed for the same concern. Two consumers, one concern, and a double-count path if the pipeline emits both stage_changed and won for a single drop. Delete the stage_changed consumer.
- Hall of Fame + badges + personal bests + streaks all landing together. Personal bests alone covers the 'a #38 seller can always beat themselves' argument. Hall of Fame is explicitly justified as kiosk filler on a zero-close day — which is a symptom of the missing today-view, not a feature.

### Event gaps (15)
- No uniqueness/idempotency contract on (tenant_id, source_event_id) in earnings_ledger_entry. At-least-once delivery or an event replay silently double-credits public earnings, and the 'append-only, never edited' rule means the bad entry can only be papered over with a correction — after the whole floor saw it.
- The module lists ITSELF ('leaderboard') as an expected consumer of nearly every event it emits (earnings.updated, rank_changed, podium_changed, celebration_triggered, streak_*, badge_awarded...). That is circular and hides the real question: which of these cross a module boundary and which are internal projection updates. Clean the consumer lists or the wiring review is meaningless.
- MISSING leaderboard.viewed (or board_opened) with board_key, surface (app | widget | kiosk) and viewer rank bucket. Three of the module's own KPIs — daily board reach, the anti-demotivation signal that decides whether all-time survives, and board-to-action correlation — are unmeasurable without it. The KPI that governs the riskiest product decision in the module has no telemetry.
- MISSING celebration.shown / celebration_acknowledged. celebration_event persists for kiosk replay and celebration_voided is defined as firing 'before or shortly after display' — but nothing records whether it was displayed, so 'shortly after' is undefined and the replay-on-reconnect logic has no idempotency marker. The mute/dismiss-within-1s KPI also has nowhere to come from.
- MISSING consumption of meeting.scheduled / appointment.set. Appointments set is a core FE floor metric and the natural leading-indicator recognition category. The spec consumes meeting.completed and explicitly non-consumes meeting.no_show, but never the event where the seller actually did the work.
- NO ALOWARE-DERIVED EVENTS CONSUMED. Aloware webhooks deliver disposition, duration, recording, transcript and AI summary — the module consumes only generic call.started / call.completed. 'Most Contacted (connected calls)' needs disposition to distinguish a connect from a voicemail drop, and there is no talk-time input at all. The highest-value integration in the whole product is invisible to the star module.
- Speed-to-Lead is specified twice with two different anchors: feature text says 'best median time from lead.created to first call.completed', insurance_specifics says 'first connected call'. Those are different metrics, and both are the wrong one (see missing_for_real_operation). Pick the dial attempt and say so once.
- earnings.updated fires only when the COUNTABLE total changes, so anomaly-held entries emit nothing to dashboard/reporting. Result: the seller's own dashboard and the reporting module show a Closed-Won opportunity with no matching earnings and no explanation — the exact 'one owner, one number' contract the module claims to enforce, broken by its own guardrail. Payload also lacks is_countable and the list of affected board_keys.
- MISSING opportunity.product_type_changed. Product-line boards (FE vs IUL) are declared load-bearing, and product_type is frozen onto the ledger entry at close. If product type is corrected after close, the FE and IUL boards are permanently wrong with no correction path.
- MISSING an anomaly resolution event. leaderboard.anomaly_flagged is emitted; there is no leaderboard.anomaly_approved / anomaly_rejected. So the audit log records the accusation and not the verdict, and the celebration that was suppressed at flag time has no defined trigger when the entry is later released.
- leaderboard.rank_changed carries triggered_by_seller_id — it names the person who passed you, in an event routed to notifications and the automation-engine. Combined with the (correct) 'never send loss messages' rule, this field exists only to enable the message the module says it will never send. Drop it.
- leaderboard.celebration_triggered carries opportunity_id in a payload whose broadcast_scope can be 'floor' — an identifier from another seller's silo shipped to every logged-in client. The floor payload should be display_name, amount, product_type, tier. Nothing else.
- earnings.updated lists automation-engine as a consumer with no scoping statement. If a seller can author an automation, they can bind it to cross-seller earnings events. That needs an explicit rule (automations may only bind to the authoring seller's own earnings; only admins may bind to floor-wide) or the silo leaks through the automation builder rather than the UI.
- opportunity.owner_changed is consumed and handled correctly (credit stays with the closer), but it presumes an ownership-transfer capability exists in the pipeline module. In a no-assignment-engine MVP that may not exist — confirm it, or this is a dead consumer that quietly becomes the reassignment loophole later.
- leaderboard.period_closed is defined dormant with 'final_standings' as an inline payload field. Standings for 50 sellers inline in an event is a fat-payload smell; make it a snapshot_id reference like the rest of the module already does.

### 🚨 Silo violations (9)
- KIOSK TOKEN IS THE HIGH-SEVERITY ONE, AND THE SPEC DOWNPLAYS IT: an unauthenticated, guessable-if-leaked URL that renders named US employees ranked by earned annual premium. The spec's own risk note says 'a leaked token should be embarrassing, not dangerous'. It is dangerous — that URL is a de-facto compensation-proxy disclosure for 50 named people, indexable if it ever hits a browser sync or a shared bookmark. Mandate: short expiry (not optional), IP/network binding or rotation on every kiosk restart, no seller full names (first name + last initial only on the unauthenticated surface), and a visible 'this link is public' warning at mint time.
- SUPERVISORS CAN MINT KIOSK TOKENS. The highest-risk artifact in the module — an unauthenticated public URL exposing every seller's earnings — is delegated to a role with no stated accountability for it, with visibility scoped only to 'tokens they created' so no supervisor can see the full exposure surface. Admin-only, or admin-approved.
- THE COMPLIANCE CLAIM IS FACTUALLY WRONG: 'no consumer name... which keeps the TCPA/CCPA surface area of this module at zero'. CCPA as amended by CPRA has covered EMPLOYEE personal information since Jan 2023. Seller display name, avatar image, earnings, tenure, and the kiosk's last_seen_ip_hash are all employee PI, and this module publishes them — including to an unauthenticated endpoint. The surface is not zero; it needs a retention position, an access/deletion answer for a departed seller whose Hall of Fame record is permanent, and the client's written acceptance.
- SUPERVISOR DRILL-DOWN IS ROUTED THROUGH THE PUBLIC SCREEN. The permission table puts 'view another seller's leads, opportunities, calls, calendar — Supervisor: Yes, via the seller's performance detail' on a path that starts at a public leaderboard row. Supervisor global visibility is legitimate per the brief; hanging it off the one public surface in the system means every future 'just add a link here' request erodes the boundary from the most-visited screen. Put supervisor drill-down in supervisor tooling and keep the board's profile card aggregate-only for ALL roles, no conditional branch.
- PROFILE CARD LEAKS DERIVED COMPENSATION DETAIL BY ARITHMETIC. Publishing earnings_total + deals_won + product mix lets any seller compute any other seller's average annual premium per policy and infer their commission within a narrow band. That may be acceptable to this client — but it is a deliberate disclosure the spec never names, and it is the version of the board an HR complaint will be about.
- leaderboard.celebration_triggered with broadcast_scope='floor' pushes opportunity_id — a cross-silo record identifier — to every connected seller client. Strip it from floor-scoped payloads.
- automation-engine listed as a consumer of earnings.updated and leaderboard.rank_changed with no authoring-scope rule (see event_gaps). This is the most likely path for a silo leak that passes UI review, because it never renders another seller's data on a screen — it just lets one seller subscribe to another's production.
- NO SERVER-SIDE ENFORCEMENT STATEMENT ON THE STANDINGS API ITSELF. The spec correctly says controls are 'absent from the DOM, not disabled' and that the boundary must be enforced server-side — but never states that the standings/profile endpoints return an aggregate-only projection with no join path to leads, opportunities or activities. Hiding UI and returning a fat object is the defect class this module is most likely to ship.
- user.deactivated 'freezes the ledger, removes from live standings, retains history for Hall of Fame'. A departed seller's name stays permanently on a public wall TV. That needs an explicit admin control to anonymize or retire a former employee's public records, and it is the CCPA deletion question above in concrete form.

## Risks & dependencies
- HARD DEPENDENCY on the pipeline module enforcing stage-transition gating: annual premium must be required and non-zero BEFORE a card can enter a Closed-Won stage. If that gate is not built there, this module's public numbers ship blank or wrong and no amount of leaderboard logic fixes it.
- OWNERSHIP CONFLICT to arbitrate: this module claims the earnings_ledger and the definition of 'Earnings'. If the reporting/dashboard module also computes Earnings from opportunities directly, the two will disagree in public. One owner, one number — everyone else consumes earnings.updated.
- EVENT-NAME COUPLING: this module assumes the pipeline emits semantic opportunity.won / opportunity.reopened / opportunity.value_changed, not just opportunity.stage_changed, and that meetings use meeting.* not appointment.*. Any drift silently stops the board from updating.
- THE DEMOTIVATION RISK IS REAL AND ACCEPTED: an all-time, never-reset, revenue-only board is the exact configuration the research says demotes most of a team, and it is worse for every seller hired after launch. The mitigations (recognition categories, self-rank, personal bests, rookie window, distance-to-next-rank, product-line boards) are load-bearing, not decoration — cutting them in Phase 3 while keeping all-time would ship the documented failure mode on purpose.
- GAMING PRESSURE: making money public creates an incentive to inflate annual premium or close-and-reopen. Mitigated by anomaly flagging, reversals, and an immutable audit trail — but the social risk (a seller accused of padding) needs the owner's buy-in on the anomaly threshold before launch.
- SILO LEAK IS THE HIGH-SEVERITY DEFECT CLASS: this is the only screen where a seller sees another seller's data. Any future 'see their pipeline' or 'compare our leads' affordance breaks the defining architecture. The profile card boundary must be enforced server-side, not by hiding UI.
- KIOSK TOKEN = UNAUTHENTICATED URL exposing per-person earnings. It must be tenant-scoped, revocable, expiring, aggregate-only, and must never render a login form. A leaked token should be embarrassing, not dangerous.
- REAL-TIME TRANSPORT vs the <$100/month infra target: at ~50 concurrent users plus a few kiosks, SSE with a polling fallback is well within budget; a heavier always-on WebSocket fleet is not. Choose in Phase 5 with this constraint explicit.
- HR/LEGAL EXPOSURE: publicly ranking named employees by earnings is a management decision with employment-law and morale implications in the US. Not a TCPA/CCPA issue, but the client should confirm they accept it — the whole module depends on that answer.
- CELEBRATION FATIGUE AND CALL INTERRUPTION: broadcast toasts on a floor of 50 sellers can fire many times an hour. Auto-mute on active call and tiering are required, not optional, or the feature gets switched off tenant-wide within weeks.
- TIMEZONE CORRECTNESS: 50 US sellers can span four time zones; 'today', streaks and snapshots must use one tenant timezone or streaks will break unfairly for the sellers at the edges.
- FEATURES CUT AND WHY (do not reintroduce without a stated problem): Slack/Teams broadcast integration — no Slack in scope, adds an integration surface for a notification we already deliver in-app and on the TV; a points/coins currency and prize store — invents a second scoring system nobody asked for and dilutes the one metric the owner cares about; peer reactions and comments on board rows — turns a performance board into a social feed with a moderation and harassment problem in exchange for no seller or owner outcome; a public bottom-5 or 'needs attention' list — actively harms the majority the module is meant to motivate; a demo/seed data mode — the risk of fake standings appearing in production outweighs the demo convenience; per-seller custom celebration GIF uploads — moderation burden on a screen mounted in a public room.

## Open questions
- Does an entry count at application submitted, at policy issued, or at first premium paid? In FE/IUL these are weeks apart. Which stage flag feeds the board changes what the public number means and who emits the event that confirms issue.
- Is there a post-sale policy-status module in scope that would emit policy.not_taken / policy.lapsed / policy.chargeback? If not, chargebacks stay a manual admin adjustment and the board will overstate real production by the not-taken rate.
- Should Final Expense and IUL be separate boards by DEFAULT (fair, but splits the floor's attention) or one mixed board with a filter (single shared story, but structurally favors IUL writers)? This is a business decision, not a design one.
- Given the board never resets, should sellers in their first 90 days appear on the main board at all, or only on a Rookie board until they graduate? Appearing at #48 on day one may be the single most demotivating first impression in the product.
- Who owns the earnings_ledger and the canonical 'Earnings' number — this module or reporting/dashboards? Must be settled before Phase 3 or two modules will publish different totals.
- Confirm the event contract with the pipeline module: does it emit opportunity.won and opportunity.reopened, or only opportunity.stage_changed with a closed flag? And is it appointment.* or meeting.*?
- Does the client want 'you were passed' nudges at all? They are opt-in and gap-framed here, but a floor of 50 competitive telesales agents may want them louder — or may find them corrosive.
- What is the anomaly threshold for a suspicious annual premium (multiple of trailing median, or a hard USD ceiling), and who is comfortable being the person who holds a colleague's close off the board pending review?
- Tie-break preference on a public screen: earliest close first, then deals won, then alphabetical — or something the owner already uses?
- Legal/HR sign-off on publishing named per-person earnings to every employee and to an unauthenticated TV display link.
- Should personal bests and streaks be public by default or private by default? Public drives more motion; private is safer for the bottom half.
- Confirm the cuts listed in risks (Slack broadcast, points/prize store, peer reactions, bottom-5 list, demo seed mode, custom celebration uploads) — each was cut for a stated reason, and any reinstatement should come with the problem it solves for a seller or the owner.
